# Cal AI — a filmable "golden moment" + a 220k-contact influencer machine with minimum-view clauses

## Idea — how they found it
- Corpus has no founder interview (Zach Yadegari / Blake Anderson). What is in it: the "golden moment" = take a picture of your food and it counts calories; "we were the first app to do that" — [08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=492s), [08:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=499s). Real problem behind the viral feature: most people quit calorie tracking because manual logging is tedious — [24:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1485s).
- Daniel's selection logic (Cal AI as the example): novel mechanic + easy to understand + big existing market ("MyFitnessPal is a massive company") — [37:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2279s). Niche choice: fitness has many, reasonably priced influencers, and calorie tracking doesn't cannibalize a coach's own product — [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=826s), [43:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2597s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Every influencer video shows three core screens — camera, loading, food detail; the team works on making those three instantly legible — [36:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2179s) (Daniel).
- Retention work targeted the **3-day trial**: redesign of the core logging screens, streaks, groups/community feed with leaderboards (Duolingo/Strava insight, network effect), milestone badges and progress photos meant to be shared to Instagram Stories — [26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1563s), [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1663s), [28:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1694s), [29:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1778s), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1798s). Ship speed rule: "human motivation only lasts like about 2 weeks" — [31:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1865s).
- Onboarding asks for a rating before showing reviews — copied by a quit-smoking app (reviews ~50 → ~1,000) — [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s) (*secondhand*). Transaction-abandonment paywall toggles the free trial with no discount; Cal AI treated as a high-intent app — [24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1483s), [25:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1527s) (paywall consultant who worked on it).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Scale**: ~20-30 creators when Alfie joined → 200+ active at once, ~4 videos/month each — [03:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=201s). Goal 50+ signings/month, some months 100 — [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=979s).
- **Outreach machine**: ~25 marketing assistants (hired via an outsourcing company), each pushed to ~100 influencers/day, "2,000 every single day" (he caps it lower than 25x100; real days 40-70 per assistant) — [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2085s), [34:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2098s). Mostly **email**, DMs throttle — [45:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2701s). AI handled initial negotiation replies; the assistant's job ended at message one — [44:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2666s). Don't ask for a call in message one: pitch seamless integration + long-term partnership, then propose a call; a call "probably 10xes your chances" — [37:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2278s). Assistants motivated by monthly competitions — [42:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2553s).
- **Funnel**: 100 contacts → ~10 replies → ~2 calls → ~1 signed; he then says "probably more like one from every thousand" — [35:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2147s), [36:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2176s). Lifetime: ~220,000 contacted, ~2-3k onboarded/signed (~1%) — [35:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2123s), [36:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2180s). "Less picky" than most brands: decent CPM + can fit the app in → yes — same section.
- **Pitch**: "keep doing what you're doing and just try and sneak our app in"; no word-for-word script; this also lowers price — [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=440s), [41:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2490s).
- **Minimum-view clause**: creator brags "I can easily get a million" → agree, set the paid floor at 800k ("you'll hit a million easily") or 700k "so it's not stressful for you"; guarantees a profitable CPM and they can't back down — [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=615s), [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=649s).
- **15-second rule**: app on screen within the first 15 s, "one of the biggest rules" — [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1336s).
- **Best angle**: big creators' day-in-the-life vlogs where they just scan breakfast without naming the app; a Mormon Wives cast member (Jesse) drove "a couple of hundred" to "thousands" of promo-code uses per video — [20:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1249s), [22:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1322s). Posts timed just before/after her show's new season → very low effective CPM — [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1716s).
- **Niches**: fitness split into gym bros / women's fitness / weight loss, later moms — moms convert, know it, some profitable even at $10 CPM — [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1066s), [18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1100s). **Failed**: recipe/cooking/mukbang content (viewers relaxing, don't want to think about calories) — [19:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1185s), [20:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1215s). Comedy creators: great CPM, low intent — [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1112s). Love Island creator underperformed: comments were about his girlfriend — check who the audience is really there for — [31:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1876s).
- **Seven-touch logic**: early, unknown brand → aim for very low CPM, impressions volume; later, recognition made each impression convert better, so they could pay more per deal — [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=766s). Goal: make Cal AI "a trend" — [23:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1416s). But a second creator in the same sub-niche as a big winner flopped — saturation is real per niche — [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1012s).
- **Agents/relationships**: now even 5-10k-view creators have agents, raising prices — [46:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2818s). Referral bonus ~$100-200 for a friend's personal number routes around agencies — [45:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2756s), [46:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2761s). Get the influencer into the group chat, not just the manager.
- **Early UGC**: Dawson Gibbs posted UGC for them at the start — [49:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2962s); Dawson says ~200M views across his page, the brand page (face of brand ~5 months) and ~100 creators he referred for a fee — [04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=256s), [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=284s).
- **Paid ads**: Daniel: the big inflection "is when we started really ramping up on paid ads" — [47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2863s).
- **2026 view (Alfie)**: influencer prices "skyrocketed"; $1-2 CPM deals hard to find; that is why UGC grew in the last ~6 months; he'd run both, UGC = cheaper views, less trust — [48:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2935s), [49:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2996s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Pick an idea whose core action is a 3-second visual (photo → number) inside a huge existing market → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Influencer volume is a numbers game: ~1% of contacts sign; use email, AI first replies, calls before rates, referral bounties around agents → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Price deals by a minimum-view floor set below the creator's own claim; app on screen within 15 s; "sneak it in" briefs → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Test niches by audience intent, not topical fit (food content failed for a calorie app) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Organic/influencers to start, paid ads for the inflection → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Retain through the trial with streaks, groups, shareable milestones → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md); trial toggle on abandonment → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Acquirer bought the distribution funnel, not the tech (Josh, Blue Throne — an acquirer, so interested party) [39:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2392s) → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
- Clones can help the leader: searches for knock-offs surface Cal AI with ~100x reviews (Peter, *secondhand*) [52:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=3139s) → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported by an employee; no founder interview in the corpus. $60M is "at the peak"; the 1,000 → 10 funnel is his own admitted best case. Survivorship: Cal AI is the canonical winner; 2024-25 influencer prices no longer hold (Alfie, 2026-08).
- External claims in the reels-to-revenue card (from video 5fQ-JyHggBI, not in this corpus): "$5.7M revenue in January", a creator-only app build with big logo and pre-filled streak, a "$500k MrBeast deal", "~$2M/month from influencers alone". **Not confirmable in the corpus** — treat as unverified. The corpus only confirms "paid ads = inflection" (Daniel).
- Daniel's retention features are product claims without lift numbers. Superwall hosts; SpyTok sponsor read in episode.
