# Runafy — validate on a proven competitor's format, seed launch with pre-orders, exit in weeks

## Idea — how they found it
- Combined his only hobby (running) with gamer "ranked" mechanics — [00:21:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1290s). The reference was **Liftoff** (ranked gym workouts), making $200–300k/mo at validation time and >$100k/mo for ≥12 months — [00:21:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1312s), [00:23:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1418s).
- Product gap: Liftoff ranks are self-reported (anyone can log a 1,000 kg bench), while running GPS data is "official" — [00:22:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1366s).
- **Ideation checklist:** 1–3 apps in the space each doing ≥$100k/mo. Don't clone them; take the mechanic to an adjacent niche. Distrust leaders that are VC-funded (e.g. Reframe, ~$24M raised per the host), because their revenue doesn't prove the channel is profitable — [00:54:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3246s), [00:54:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3294s).
- Learned from his first app ([paruresis-app](paruresis-app.md)): validate distribution before spending months building, and pick a problem you care about — [00:20:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1247s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Designed the whole app (onboarding, UI, UX) in Figma in 3 days at ~15 h/day, then handed it to devs — [00:31:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1914s). Two hired devs failed after 2–3 weeks; a third (found via inbound from a story post) became CTO and worked ~14 h/day for 1.5 months — [00:07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=429s), [00:31:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1889s).
- Complex for a "vibe-coded" app: social feed, leaderboards, rank logic, Garmin + Strava + Apple Watch integrations. Users were evenly split across the three, so they built all of them — [00:06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=400s), [00:37:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2275s).
- Onboarding copied patterns from Cal AI, Quitter and Life Reset. Conversion "wasn't that high" until they repositioned from "fun ranks" to the outcome (become a better runner) — [00:33:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2005s), [00:34:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2041s).
- Users: 50–80 paying "early adopters" for feedback, ~20 on WhatsApp as TestFlight bug testers, and Instagram story polls to ~2,000 followers — [00:35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2107s), [00:36:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2164s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Format:** copied a Liftoff rankings Reel (rank tiers per distance, ChatGPT-made icons); one copy hit ~2M views — [00:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1466s), [00:24:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1474s). Each piece took ~30 min, plus a scheduler — [00:28:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1692s). Track-and-field distances went most viral — [00:29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1746s).
- **Volume:** nine posts/day, because "Instagram really doesn't punish you for that". On TikTok one video hit ~700k views, then views collapsed when they raised frequency, so they doubled down on IG (early 2026) — [00:29:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1790s), [00:30:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1805s). The CTO built a tool that renders ~10,000 variants of the format in a minute — [00:30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1827s).
- **Fake-door validation:** traffic → ChatGPT-built HTML page → Stripe link ($5 early-adopter lifetime access) or waitlist; Figma mock screenshots pinned on IG — [00:25:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1501s), [00:26:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1594s).
- **App Store pre-order:** ship a bare-bones 2-tab V1 just to pass review, open pre-orders, keep marketing. At release the app auto-downloads to every pre-order and Apple emails them — [00:08:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=504s), [00:08:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=535s). This solved the empty-leaderboard cold start — [00:09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=566s). Trap: pre-orders need a fixed date. He forgot to push it back, and the buggy build went live to ~1,000 users with ~20 complaint emails — [00:39:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2397s).
- **Launch:** waitlist emails (32% landed in spam, ~20% opened → "why I think waitlists are stupid"), half-price referral codes (most of launch-day revenue), Reddit, a Rotato demo on X — [00:42:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2540s), [00:27:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1639s), [00:41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2476s).

**Exit.** A cold X DM (from Dev Shah, who works for a PE firm) followed his bullish "path to $100k/mo" tweets from a ~200-follower account — [00:04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=270s), [00:44:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2669s). Buyer: an app studio targeting wellness, with its own devs and distribution team — [00:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=357s), [00:48:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2927s). Terms: kept 30% equity + 6-month cash earnout + cash bonus — [00:47:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2840s). Diligence took "a couple days" (App Store Connect access to check the users weren't bought); then six contracts and escrow — [00:49:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2947s), [00:49:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2975s). His reasoning: equity upside ("10% of a watermelon"), de-risking, capital for the next app, personal-brand leverage — [00:51:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3062s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Copy the distribution format of a proven ≥$100k/mo app into an adjacent niche, and sell before building (Stripe fake door) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- High-frequency posting (9/day) worked on Instagram but got throttled on TikTok, early 2026 → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Pre-orders beat waitlists for launch seeding (auto-download vs spam-foldered email), especially for social/leaderboard apps → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Onboarding should sell the outcome, not the mechanic → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Buyers paid ~5x ARR on <1 month of revenue for an app with complexity, fit with their studio, and a public, confident founder. Retained equity + earnout bridges the uncertainty → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)

## Caveats
- An extreme outlier: valuation rests on an *estimated* MRR from <1 month of data; strategic fit (a running-obsessed studio team) drove the price.
- Exact cash received is undisclosed; "$100k exit" is the title, and the multiple is recalled loosely.
- Liftoff numbers are secondhand (his recollection plus the host's Sensor Tower: ~300k downloads, ~$700k/mo — [00:22:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1326s)).
- Retention after the sale is unknown; he says it was "hard to know" at 26 days. Superwall/SpyTok promos in the video.
