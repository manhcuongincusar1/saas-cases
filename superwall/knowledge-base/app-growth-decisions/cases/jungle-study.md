# Jungle — a lucky influencer hit you cannot repeat, then product-led word of mouth

## Idea — how they found it
- After 4–5 failed pivots and ~3 months of runway, saw a viral tweet ("my dream AI product: turn anything I'm learning into flashcards") and replied that they were building something similar — [00:03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=213s), [00:03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=217s), [00:03:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=223s).
- That reply alone produced 200+ downloads and ~200 DMs; first 100 users came from DMing everyone who engaged, until his Twitter account was banned — [00:04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=242s), [00:04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=253s), [00:04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=259s).
- DMs were "not the most effective way to grow" but the best feedback channel (one-to-one line open) — [00:06:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=391s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Upload a PDF, slides or YouTube video → practice questions (MCQ, flashcards, open-ended) — [00:02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=135s); gamified like Duolingo because the real student problem is motivation, not efficacy — [00:02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=154s), [00:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=171s).
- First-session experience is stripped down: upload straight from the landing page (one of the most successful of "hundreds of experiments"), no settings, a mini review of the first 7 questions, full feature set only afterwards — [00:14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=895s), [00:15:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=902s), [00:15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=916s), [00:15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=941s).
- A growing tree that reacts to answers raised the core engagement metric (questions answered) by 70% — and doubles as a word-of-mouth loop: students in libraries see each other's screens — [00:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=974s), [00:16:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=983s), [00:16:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=997s).
- Paywall placement rule: show it at the step ~50% of users reach, not one only ~25% reach, or a quarter of users never see it — [00:17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1056s), [00:17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1070s). Onboarding split into 3 phases (sign-up after first generation → exam/notifications → character/"how did you hear about us"/weekly goal) — [00:18:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1087s).
- Freemium, not hard paywall: users can keep studying free and convert later; his view is bootstrappers *can* bet on retention but it is harder when not cash-flow positive, because retention is "literally the hardest metric to move" — [00:21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1270s), [00:21:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1299s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Mentor rule (2 weeks).** "For the next two weeks, don't touch the product… focus purely on distribution… if nothing happens, then no one probably cares… and you should probably pivot" — [00:05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=330s), [00:05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=334s). Then get at least 30–100 people/week in the door so experiments are readable — [00:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=360s) (*heuristic*).

**Early pull.** AI tool directories + creators organically covering a then-novel idea (AI flashcards); he thinks directories are oversaturated now and the novelty factor is gone — [00:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=300s), [00:07:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=464s).

**Influencers (micro/mid-tier).** Targeted 5k–100k-follower study creators for better CPM/ROI than big names, multi-video deals for lower per-video cost, briefs with pain points + proven hooks but creative freedom — [00:08:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=497s), [00:08:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=529s). Had to make content themselves to learn to judge it — [00:10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=626s). One med-student creator (Agogo) had ~3 videos over 1M views; one took MRR from ~$2k to ~$15k in 1–2 weeks and was estimated at ~$20k revenue — [00:11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=695s), [00:11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=707s), [00:11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=716s). They could not replicate it with her or others, and spend outran revenue; in hindsight he would have used minimum-view clauses — [00:12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=763s), [00:12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=777s), [00:13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=789s), [00:13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=818s).

**In-house UGC (current).** Creators open separate accounts and post 10–12 videos/week each, ~30 creators → ~400 videos/week, posing as students who discovered Jungle — [00:22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1342s), [00:22:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1347s), [00:22:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1351s), [00:22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1355s). ~$2 CPM, "way better than influencer marketing", "feels profitable overall" — [00:23:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1389s), [00:23:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1398s). He calls it a temporary growth hack: every brand now does it, study feeds are saturated, and viewers notice every video on an account pushes the same tool — [00:23:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1401s), [00:23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1412s).

**Word of mouth.** 30–40% of new users come from friends (measured via the "how did you hear about us" step) — [00:14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=850s), [00:19:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1149s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Use a fixed distribution-only window (2 weeks) as a kill test before polishing product → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Replying under a viral "I wish this existed" post is a free demand test and a feedback channel, not a growth engine → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- A single creator hit is not a repeatable channel; budget for misses and use minimum-view clauses → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Owned UGC accounts at ~$2 CPM beat influencer deals for steady volume, but the format is saturating in study niches → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Cut first-session time-to-value; put the paywall where most users still are → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Visible-in-public UI (the tree) + clustered audience (campuses) = word of mouth → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported, mostly rounded; the $20k-per-video figure is an internal estimate. Episode title says "no-code" but Julian describes himself as the technical co-founder — [00:05:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=317s).
- Host intro numbers (40 creators, 150M views, "retention 10–100× harder than top of funnel") are not said by Julian — [00:00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=35s), [00:01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=102s).
- UGC accounts "posing as students" is undisclosed advertising — platform/FTC disclosure risk. Compare [studyfetch](studyfetch.md), [coconote](coconote.md) for study-app creator programs that scaled further.
- Late 2025; Superwall-sponsored, host runs Consumer Club.
