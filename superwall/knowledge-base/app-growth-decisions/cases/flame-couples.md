## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Found users returned for a side feature — the daily question couples answer together — not the AI coach; made it the home screen — [00:11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=682s), [00:11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=702s), [00:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=713s).
- BJ Fogg habit model: every retention event tied to a trigger, with **carrot** (morning reminder) and **stick** (evening "you'll lose your streak, your partner will be sad") notifications — [00:12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=724s), [00:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=734s), [00:12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=744s), [00:12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=752s).
- Emotional stakes are the partner, not the streak; you cannot see your partner's answer until you answer — [00:15:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=935s), [00:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s).
- Widgets (copied from Couple Joy's lock-screen widget) added late; distance/days-together widgets pull users back — [00:16:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=997s), [00:16:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1011s), [00:17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1031s).
- D30 went 4.2% → ~30% → 50% over ~8 months — [00:12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=766s), [00:13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=830s). Built for the camera: loader/AI animation designed to look good in TikToks (Cal AI-style) — [00:51:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3069s), [00:51:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3079s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Retention is buildable via one daily ritual + trigger + social stakes; find the feature people actually return for and promote it → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Great retention without a distribution answer did not raise money and nearly killed the company → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Measure conversion on new-user cohorts only; his 15% was inflated by renewals → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Views ≠ downloads: product-in-use videos convert ~5–20× memes/slideshows → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Use view thresholds to separate account problems from content problems before iterating creative → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
