# Clear 30 — tripling revenue by fixing onboarding and paywall, not by finding a viral video

## Idea — how they found it
- Science-based daily program (clinician-designed) to stop or moderate weed; ~1.5 years in at recording — [00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=123s), [00:02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=142s).
- Self-described "complete idiots in this app world"; no origin/validation story given beyond the clinician co-founder — [00:02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=147s).
- Unit-economics argument they lean on: average user spends $55+/week on weed; program costs $30/year — [00:07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=452s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Onboarding built after tearing down Cal AI's (and others') — [00:09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=566s). Early mistake: no tracking until phone-number sign-in, so they could not see top-of-funnel drop-off — [00:09:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=594s).
- Flow: dream-outcome intro ("30 days without weed", 85–90% pass) → ask name, then use it → goals with matched social proof ("81% improve mental clarity") → pain questions (days/week, money spent) → comparison to population with cited sources → "where we'll take you" (incl. "$220 more") → mechanism, "25,000+ people", "improvement in the first 3 days" — [00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=615s), [00:10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=659s), [00:12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=759s), [00:14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=854s), [00:15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=933s), [00:15:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=954s).
- Framing is a value equation (dream outcome × likelihood ÷ time delay × effort) applied screen by screen — [00:11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=679s).
- "Good friction": the two reflective questions (goals, triggers) take users 20–30 s each with almost no drop-off (host reads <5%) — kept, not cut — [00:13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=788s), [00:13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=805s).
- **"This costs money, here's why it's fair" screen** (copied from the Ahead app) anchoring price against the cost of the habit: ~+3% conversion — [00:17:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1053s), [00:18:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1099s). Goal: cut "why is this paid" complaints on social by 50% — [00:19:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1147s).
- Phone number capture → human, funny SMS via Twilio as the main re-engagement channel — [00:19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1189s), [00:19:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1194s).
- Free personalised feedback before the paywall (strengths, money you could save) — [00:20:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1230s).
- **In-onboarding rating prompt** shown before the reviews screen (learned from Cal AI): App Store ratings went from ~50 to almost 1,000 — [00:21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s), [00:21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1296s).
- **Paywall:** Blinkist-style trial timeline, "no payment due now", 3-day trial; most pick yearly. Price tests around $30/yr (vs $40/$50 anchors) — [00:22:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1372s), [00:23:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1427s), [00:24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1446s).
- **The 20% → 30% change:** only the first paywall screen changed, from a feature carousel to a day-by-day "what your first 7 days look like" outline with quick wins (attacking *time delay*: "it won't take 30 days to feel better") — [00:24:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1474s), [00:25:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1556s), [00:26:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1566s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Tried influencers, Reddit, organic content and SEO first — "an uphill battle to convert people"; growth started only with paid ads — [00:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=190s), [00:03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=200s). Paid only works once the product converts at "multiple dollars per install" — [00:03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=239s).
- Self-filmed, scripted comedy ads (Asher's father is an actor) with hyper-specific call-outs ("hit your pen in the basement till 3 a.m. watching skate videos") — [00:04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=258s), [00:05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=311s).
- Age matters more than likes: 26–30-year-olds convert at almost double the rate of 18–20; a calm ad with Asher's mom explaining the problem is among the best converters — [00:06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=407s), [00:07:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=431s).
- Attribution: MMP for the ad algorithms + an in-onboarding "where did you hear about us" question; ROAS after Apple's cut tracked in Amplitude — [00:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=491s), [00:08:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=523s).
- Creative learnings (no numbers): a woman outperformed a man; phone-camera look beat polished — [00:28:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1706s). A "take our quiz" ad (copied from Reframe) did not work for them — [00:27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1649s).

## Lessons → decisions
- If organic is not converting, fix onboarding + paywall before buying traffic; paid scaled only once install value was high → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Paywall first screen: a concrete day-by-day plan that shrinks perceived time-to-result beat a feature carousel (+10 pts) → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Ask for the rating inside onboarding after delivering a small personalised win → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md), [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Instrument the funnel from first open; reflective questions with long dwell and low drop-off are commitment, not friction → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Judge ads by conversion by age cohort, not likes; older, calmer creatives can win in recovery categories → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported, from Superwall dashboards shown on a Superwall-sponsored episode ("they use Superwall") — [00:01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=106s). Early stage ($30k/mo) and only ~2 months of the "after" period.
- The rating prompt before use is Apple-guideline-sensitive (prompting before the user has experienced the app); the hosts call it "the dumbest thing" from a consumer view.
- Many onboarding claims (81%, 94–96% more than average, 25,000+ people) are marketing copy on screens, not audited outcomes.
- "10M views and made $1,000" at the open is about *other* people's organic, not Clear 30 — [00:29:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1791s).
- Future plans (University of Michigan partnership, B2B, upsells) were pending, not results — [00:30:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1857s).
