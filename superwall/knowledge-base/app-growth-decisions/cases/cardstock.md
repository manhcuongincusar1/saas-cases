# Cardstock — what one keyword is worth, and what happens when you lose it

## Idea — how they found it
- Own problem: wanted to catalog his baseball cards, searched the App Store, "no app existed" — [00:03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=185s), [00:03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=204s).
- Validated with a post in r/baseballcards before building; ~10–15 people replied "I'd use this" — [00:03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=239s), [00:05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=303s). A Reddit stranger DMing "still working on this?" is why he finished it.
- Launch post in the same subreddit referenced the validation post, so it did not read as an ad — [00:06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=370s). Got banned from another card subreddit for self-promo — [00:07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=465s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Scan card → show market value. First app ever for "baseball card scanner" (his claim) — [00:14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=850s).
- Started as $4.99 paid download; did not believe anyone would pay $5 *recurring*. Switching to free + subscription ($5/mo · $50/yr · $99 lifetime) was "by far the biggest revenue jump ever" (2021) — [00:10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=644s), [00:12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=742s), [00:19:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1191s).
- Paywall: 5 free scans, plus a paywall shown right at launch with an X button; most subs came from people buying on that first screen, before using the product — [00:30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1814s).
- Later added a hard paywall; together with the Christmas organic spike, revenue jumped to ~$30k/mo — [00:35:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2101s).
- Built for the camera: app logo inside the scanner view; Scanomon has a hidden setting that shows promo text so marketing videos carry the brand name (copied from Cal AI / Fig) — [00:27:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1626s), [00:39:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2362s).
- Scanomon = same framework re-skinned for Pokémon, built with AI dev tools — [00:38:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2287s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Phase 1 — ASO (passive).** Did not know ASO existed; title was just "Cardstock", no subtitle — [00:17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1027s). Apple's $100 free Search Ads credit pushed him to read about ASO — [00:13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=792s). ~1–2 weeks of research with free Sensor Tower trials (and Astro), then packed the top keywords into title/subtitle: "Cardstock: Sports Card Scanner" + "find value for baseball cards" (because "baseball card scanner" did not fit) — [00:15:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=901s), [00:15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=927s), [00:21:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1263s). Held #1 for "baseball card scanner" 2–3 years — [00:14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=843s).

**Phase 2 — TikTok after losing the rank (May 2024).** Hired friend Jacob (from Wendy's). Brand-style videos got ~23 likes and zero downloads; a growing brand page did not convert because the app was not shown — [00:22:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1365s), [00:23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1412s). Winning format, filmed at card shows: show a desirable card 3–5 s with a text hook ("what is this MJ card worth?"), scan it with the app in hand, reveal the value; the goal is "what's the app?" comments — [00:25:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1509s), [00:25:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1546s). Chose formats where every variable is controllable so they can be repeated — [00:28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1709s).

**Phase 3 — slideshow scale via Noise.** "Card show tips" slideshow format, then mass distribution on Noise: creators paid ~$1–2 CPM, Noise keeps half — [00:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s). Also tried SideShift creators and TikTok DMs — [00:50:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3003s).

## Lessons → decisions
- A niche with zero apps + exact-match keyword in the title can carry a solo app at ~$10k/mo for years; it is a six-figure, not seven-figure, channel → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Rank is rented, not owned: build the second channel while #1, not after losing it → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Paid download → free + subscription was the biggest jump; a first-screen paywall (even dismissable) captured most subs → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- On TikTok, brand/meme content without the app on screen did not convert; demo-in-hand + reveal does → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Do the channel yourself first so you can judge an agency/creator later (bootstrapped constraint) — [00:33:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2009s) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Reddit "I'm building this" post = cheapest demand test for a hobby niche → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)

## Caveats
- All numbers self-reported, partly read off a revenue chart on screen; "~$10k/mo" vs "14K, probably 9–10K recurring" shows the rounding.
- First-mover in a then-empty niche (2021); the keyword is now crowded ("a billion of them") — [00:14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=855s). Do not expect the same ease today.
- He recommends Noise, which took ~$1,300 from them — [00:51:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3118s); Noise is a distribution platform (see [noise](noise.md)). Host runs Consumer Club; video sponsored by Superwall.
- He notes slideshows were "trending out" on TikTok at recording (late 2025) — [00:47:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2845s).
