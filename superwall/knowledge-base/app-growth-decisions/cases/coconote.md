# Coconote — 10M views that convert beat 40M that don't; a tiny "Navy SEALs" creator team; onboarding/paywall/cancel-flow wins

## Idea — how they found it
- Blend of three earlier projects: Crew (a music app inside Zack's venture-backed studio Scout that went viral with UGC-style content and taught him "if you build it, they will not come") — [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1382s), [23:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1399s); Essence, a GPT-3 Chrome extension summarizing blogs/YouTube — [23:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1424s); a hack replacing Siri with GPT-4 via double-tap-back (voice as input) — [24:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1448s).
- Market gap: AI note-takers getting App Store traction "were really more towards professionals" — [21:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1316s). "Momentum is like oxygen for a startup" — [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=236s).
- Channel chosen up front: short-form video is where students are; leaned into ADHD from day one — [25:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1556s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Message continuity: App Store screenshot #1 says "great for ADHD" (host observation) — [26:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1577s); copy is users' own words back ("never miss a key detail") — [26:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1596s).
- **Creator-only screens**: the giant rainbow waveform recording screen and transcribing screen "are not real screens in the product" — built because they film better and show the app name huge — [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=671s). Creators sit in Slack with engineers and request such screens — [20:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1222s).
- **Onboarding**: 1–2-screen iterations gave small wins; they "at least doubled" length, ~5 → ~13 screens → trial conversion "went up 16%" — [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s), [29:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1783s), [29:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1797s).
- **Login after the paywall** (pay before creating an account) — "significant" — [30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1814s).
- **Paywall after invested use**: let users record an entire lecture (an hour+) before asking to pay/start trial — "a big win" — [31:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1869s).
- **Cancel flow**: instead of value reminders/surveys, offer a +7-day trial extension → "saving like 27% of the people who go through the cancel flow" — [32:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1959s); clarified: 27% don't cancel "in that moment", "a smaller percentage convert" — [33:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1989s).
- Small team: four engineers (host's count) who flex across iOS/Android/backend, all-native apps — [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1624s).
- Product thesis for the AI era: wow moments decay fast and "a hundred of those copycat products next week" — [41:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2487s); without a 7-Powers moat, repeatedly manufacturing wow is the growth engine — [41:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2503s). Best products: repeatable use case + tied to identity + wow (Ladder, Runna) — [45:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2747s). Prompt: what is possible with AI now that wasn't 3–6 months ago — [47:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2834s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Converting format (Mar–May 2024)**: lecture-hall hook, pan down to phone recording the professor, notes appear — "My mom just changed my college life forever"; 18M views — [08:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=530s); nearly all within 2 months, "99% of those views are organic"; paid boosting "haven't really found a ton of success" — [09:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=567s), [09:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=573s), [09:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=581s). Several ~20M-view videos on @coconote IG "were converting much better" because they framed it as a solution — [07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=468s).
- **Novelty fades**: a "shock hook + product demo" is a "lazier form of content" that works only while the category is new — [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=611s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=648s).
- **The views trap — PDF-to-brainrot** (8–9 months in): lead-magnet site turning text into Minecraft-parkour videos; one TikTok 40M views, 100–200M views for the feature overall — [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=270s), [04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=279s); "from all of these videos, 3,000 trial starts over the course of a couple days" (their record) — [04:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=298s); ~$25k revenue vs $360k at 100% conversion — [05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s). Younger, lower-income audience; product framed as a toy — [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s). Doctrine: "rather have 10 million views on a video that converts well... as opposed to a 40 million view video" — [08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=500s).
- **"Navy SEALs, not the Navy"** — [13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=806s): content team peaked at ~10–12 part-time contractor creators with high freedom — [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=823s). Content lead (Allison) "ended up doing hundreds of millions of views" — leaders need the team's respect earned by results — [14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=850s).
- **Sourcing = talent scouting**: Handshake, IG/TikTok DMs, email, referrals — [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=885s); nobody identifies as an influencer — hire marketers — [15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=920s). Zack recruited his online Spanish tutor 15 minutes into a lesson; 9 months later one of their best creators (LatAm) — [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=992s), [16:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1008s).
- **Creator pyramid**: base = want to win (show up, camera on, engage in Slack); middle = deconstruct others' content (judge virality with view counts hidden); top = create your own trends — [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1042s), [18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1089s), [18:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1116s). Founder interviews every creator, decision in 15–30 min — [19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1163s); 1 content coach per 12 creators — [19:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1177s). Team-based production (one films, one edits).
- **Exit**: strategic inbound started "when we were a couple months old" — [34:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2074s). Advice: most M&A calls are a bad use of time; take them only for respected acquirers with strategic fit; ask for a ballpark range early — [37:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2279s); once serious, use an investment bank to keep focus (they used Chris Parker, Creative Partners) — [35:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2141s), [36:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2189s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Pick a gap where incumbents target a different audience (professionals → students) and frame for an identity (ADHD) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Judge content by conversion of the right audience, not views; novelty formats decay as the category fills → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Small, coached, part-time creator team of marketers can carry a multi-million ARR app; build creator-only screens → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Longer onboarding, login after paywall, paywall after invested use, trial extension in cancel flow → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Strategic inbound early: filter hard, get a number early, use a bank once serious → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
