# Steve Young / Green Noise — keyword first, app second; buy distribution, then fix monetization

## Idea — how they found it
- **Keyword first, then build.** "The ones that have gotten to profitability really quick for us … we find the keyword first … and then build the app" — [00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=485s).
- Start from the category with the highest RPI, then niche down until the term is a painkiller. Meditation is a vitamin; green noise, binaural beats and migraine tracker are niche painkillers you can hard-paywall — [00:10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=659s), [00:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s), [00:12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=732s).
- Target = a niche with proven revenue but weak execution. Migraine Buddy does ~$50k/mo with a "not good" onboarding and paywall — [00:12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=724s).
- Strategy is to hide in a corner: go after keywords nobody targets — [00:07:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=464s), [00:09:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=589s). "ASO is a pool": if nobody searches for it, or it is as crowded as calorie trackers, ASO is not your game — [00:07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=439s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- **Green Noise app:** title "Green Noise App"; subtitle "Green Noise for Better Sleep" copied straight from App Store autofill; keyword field "green, noise, app, [calm], free" — [00:21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1278s), [00:21:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1311s). It hit #1 for a while; he stopped tending it. It is still #3 but "doing nothing" / "not a whole lot" of revenue — [00:11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=683s), [00:21:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1298s).
- **Paywall stance:** start with a hard paywall (no X) to force a PMF test; trials usually beat no trial — [00:29:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1749s), [00:29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1761s). Targets: ≥5–10% trial-start; 30–40% trial→paid ("industry average"). Below that, you have a product problem, not a channel problem — [00:29:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1795s). An App Review rejection of a hard paywall means "the wrong reviewer" — [00:28:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1699s). Cost: hard paywalls attract 1-star reviews — [00:20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1250s).
- Idea he relays from AppStack's Lucas (secondhand): hard paywall for Meta traffic, soft paywall for search/referral users to avoid 1-star reviews; price by onboarding answers — [00:38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2336s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Mechanics:** title + subtitle ≈ 80% of ASO ("100%. That's all I pay attention to") — [00:21:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1269s). Ranking order: optimization first, then ratings, then maybe revenue; he has beaten apps with more ratings and revenue, and a client outranked Canva on a niche term — [00:25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1558s), [00:26:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1601s).
- **Read competition by eye.** Look at the top 3 apps: how many reviews, and how many have the term in their title. If few use the term in the title and #2–3 have only ~100+ reviews, it is beatable, whatever the tool's difficulty score says — [00:12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=751s), [00:12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=769s).
- **Find hidden keywords.** Spot the app whose review count doesn't belong (…71,000 · 42,000 · 4,000), or one ranking #3 with 138 reviews, then load it into AppFollow / ASO Mobile to see what it ranks for — [00:23:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1427s), [00:24:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1441s). Check Sensor Tower / Meta Ads Library to make sure they aren't winning with paid — [00:25:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1501s).
- **Volume threshold ~30+** (AppFollow / ASO Mobile scale; he admits he doesn't know what the unit means) — [00:15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=934s), [00:17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s).
- **Scaling an acquired app:** keep loading variants of the main term into the title (he found 24 keywords with ≥24 volume that contain the core word); cut Apple Ads from ~$10k to ~$5k/mo because ASO had headroom — [00:47:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2825s), [00:09:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=568s).
- **Meta only after ≥$3 RPI** (measure RPI with organic + Apple Ads). Many "big MRR" founders run at ~100% ROAS, i.e. break-even — [00:17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1029s), [00:16:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1009s). One of his apps (~$3k/mo organic) had RPI ~$1 and Meta failed — [00:34:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2065s).

**Acquisitions.** Best buys "have distribution but are undermonetized" or can take a new channel (e.g. Meta for an older audience) — [00:42:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2535s). Prefers organic, because a paid-dependent app is "a money eating machine" — [00:44:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2645s). Loan math (illustrative): buy a $10k/mo-profit app for ~$350k with a 10-year loan (~$5k/mo repayment) and keep ~$5k/mo from day one — [00:43:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2583s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Pick the keyword before the product. Name the app after the term (title + autofill subtitle) → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md), [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Niche painkiller terms convert well enough for a hard paywall; check that trial→paid holds when trial starts double → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- ASO is a six-figure channel; add Meta only once RPI ≥ ~$3 → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Buy apps with organic distribution and weak monetization; debt-financing can make day-one cash flow positive → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
- Weak trial→paid with good trial starts = product problem; no downloads = channel problem → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)

## Caveats
- **COI:** sells ASO/marketing consulting and content (App Masters, "clients", founder community); ASO Mobile sponsors his channel ([00:11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=683s)). Withholds keywords and categories ("without giving away all my secrets").
- Green Noise itself is a *small* earner; the big numbers come from other apps. One of those (the $6M one) may be a client's.
- The intro voiceover says he "renames the app" the week after buying. In the transcript, the 2022 turnaround was paywall + price + onboarding only; title keyword expansion is the plan for the 2024 app.
- The "~30 volume" threshold is generalized from one acquisition; the "57 700 traffic" keyword at [00:24:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1451s) is garbled in the captions (unverified).
- AppFollow lost keyword data after Apple API changes ([00:25:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1521s)); the tooling advice is dated (2026-07).
