# Bloom — narrative-first organic, then re-run the winners as paid ads to flatten the spikes

## Idea — how they found it
- "Robinhood and Duolingo together": learn investing from zero to P/E ratios and financial statements, test via mock trading and gamification, then invest for real — [01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=74s). The interview covers distribution, not the origin story.
- Jay's stated takeaway: "people overlook product market fit and just focus their time mostly on marketing" — [00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=46s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Fintech conversion is hard: personal info, KYC, real money → onboarding revised "almost every quarter" — [19:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1190s).
- **Onboarding order**: urgency (invest at 18 → ~$1M by 65; wait until 30 → ~$340k) → animated, colorful features with memeified stocks (Netflix, Tesla) → trust last (Fox, Yahoo, SIPC, YC, reviews) — [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1232s), [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1253s), [21:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1311s). (Shown as a not-yet-live version.)
- **Tested**: one word-heavy screen split into several tap-through story-style screens lifted download → personal-info sign-up — [25:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1521s), [26:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1565s); **removing the "Get Started" button worked better** — people read instead of skipping — [26:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1597s); reason: Instagram Stories trained users to tap right — [26:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1612s). No lift magnitude given.
- **Incentive**: "up to $75" free **stock** (not cash) — really $2–4 at sign-up plus fractional stock from loot boxes bought with in-game currency earned by learning — [23:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1393s), [23:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1411s). Tested $40 / $60 / $75 headline amounts; effect on subscription "isn't as much" — [22:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1370s).
- No pricing tests: "we already have a pretty decent sized user" base — [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1507s).
- Claims retention "really worked well" despite get-rich hooks, because the in-app content teaches long-term compounding — [04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=256s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Channel sequence**: 2023 "massively scaled TikTok paid ads" → CAC deteriorated by end of 2023 → 2024 doubled down on organic — [01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=108s).
- **Narratives, not features**: 2024 "how to get rich" (HeyGen avatar videos, "what does the billionaire nanny do for their kids" → they start investing early), then Reddit-story carousels, then 2025 relatability/StudyTok — [02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=121s), [03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=183s). Narratives cycle every 3–6 months; billionaire-nanny ran ~6 months — [28:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1684s), [28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1695s). StudyTok got views but **lower conversion** "because we were a fintech company" — [28:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1697s). TikTok is pushing carousels — [28:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1722s).
- **Three engines**: (1) an AI-content agency with 5–10 accounts that already had following, ~2 posts/day each; when one hits, propagate to ~20 accounts — [05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=322s); (2) internal team warming up zero-follower accounts; (3) micro-creators picked for content quality (angle, light, narrative, trends), "not a massive following", who open a new account and warm it up — [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=400s), [06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=416s). Profiles look personal ("learn to invest with XYZ") — [09:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=582s); creators run 1–2 accounts to A/B the algorithm — [08:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=539s).
- **Indirect CTA + comment team**: organic videos never say "download Bloom" (reads as an ad); a comment team names the app — [05:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=349s), [06:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=361s).
- **Creator pay**: $1,000/month flat + CPM bonus past 10k/50k/100k views, or $500/month for 60 videos with a higher CPM; bonus $2–4 per 1,000 views — [07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=472s), [08:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=487s). Bonus floor 10k for new accounts, 100k for 5–10k-follower creators — [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=617s), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=633s); max $1,000 per video, reached around 1M views — [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=645s).
- **Why that structure**: pure CPM made new creators "fall off in the very beginning" — [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=730s); 10k–50k bonuses are for motivation/retention ("below 100k it's usually not highest conversion"), 100k+ is where conversion and real pay live — [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=661s), [12:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=748s).
- **Organic → paid loop**: organic downloads spike and fade; once a viral video tapers, run it as a paid ad targeted by age and country — [14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=874s). Sometimes add a ~15-s app CTA that wasn't in the organic cut — [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1105s), worded natively ("download now to flex on your friends") — [18:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1137s). Example: "you downloaded Bloom as a freshman and now you're a junior with the only Tesla in the parking lot" — [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=929s); Reddit-style "underrated iPhone app" ad with only a small disclaimer — [16:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=982s).
- **Next angles** (untested at recording): Bible (Proverbs 13:11 on saving) and self-care/therapy narratives on 1–2 test accounts — [31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1887s), [33:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1998s).
- **$10k starter budget**: AI video (Veo 2/3) for B-roll/lifestyle ("if you invested in Netflix five years ago you might be sitting in this house") — [37:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2228s), [40:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2419s); recruit 2–3 creators yourself via SideShift or Handshake, then 5 — [37:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2265s). "90 days is the minimum"; he was in a 60-day dry spell — [43:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2613s), [44:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2644s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Organic finds pre-validated creative; re-run tapering winners as paid to smooth growth and cut ad spend → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Paid TikTok CAC can decay within a year; they switched channel mix rather than quitting → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Small retainer + tiered CPM bonuses keep new creators through the warm-up phase; pure CPM churns them → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Views from an adjacent audience (StudyTok) can underconvert; match narrative to the buyer → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- High-trust products: tap-through story screens, no "Get Started" button, urgency → features → trust; incentive as stock → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Give a new organic program ≥90 days before killing it → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)

## Caveats
- No revenue, conversion or CAC numbers from Jay; scale claims come from the host and the video title. Onboarding lifts are directional ("pretty big change"), no figures.
- Jay is a Consumer Club member; he credits creator-sourcing help from people the host referred (Lauren from Pebble) [06:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=394s). SideShift mention = a creator-marketplace vendor. Superwall sponsors.
- Indirect CTAs, seeded comments and ads styled as organic Reddit stories are disclosure-light; a "get rich" hook for a brokerage carries regulatory/ethics risk (Jay calls it "borderline" [03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=230s)).
- Dated 2025-07 (HeyGen era, Veo 3 just released); formats age in 3–6 months by his own estimate.
