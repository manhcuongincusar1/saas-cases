# Paruresis app — the ultra-niche ASO painkiller: superb conversion, tiny ceiling

## Idea — how they found it
- A personal pain: he couldn't urinate while observed during a pre-employment drug test and sat in the waiting room for ~2 hours — [00:10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=653s), [00:11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=672s).
- Searching for a fix, he found a Reddit community with "thousands of people with this exact problem" and learned it is a named condition — [00:11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=679s). The condition name (paruresis, shy bladder) became the search terms — [00:12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=761s).
- He admits: "really niche… quite small" — [00:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=701s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Built with FlutterFlow (no-code). It was his first app — [00:11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=708s).
- In hindsight he spent "months building a product" before testing distribution, the main thing he changed on his next app — [00:20:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1247s).
- He had no passion for the problem ("helping men go to the toilet"), which he calls a negative. His next app was tied to his own hobby (running) — [00:21:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1272s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Tried organic content himself; "this product's very difficult for me to market" — [00:12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=743s). He later calls it "the worst app to execute [UGC/influencers] on" — [00:16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=979s).
- "In the end the majority of users and revenue came through ASO" — people searching a few known terms for the condition — [00:12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=750s), [00:12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=765s).
- No keyword volumes, rank or paywall details are given in the video.

## Lessons → decisions
- A medical/embarrassing condition with a name is a ready-made keyword. Searchers have very high intent, so conversion is excellent → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Embarrassing problems are nearly un-marketable on social: search is the only channel where the demand shows up → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- A Reddit community of thousands proves the pain exists, not that the market is big. Check the ceiling before building → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- High conversion cannot rescue a tiny TAM. He moved on, calling it "not a viable option" for his goals — [00:20:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1218s) → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Founder–problem fit matters for stamina; his next app came from a passion → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
