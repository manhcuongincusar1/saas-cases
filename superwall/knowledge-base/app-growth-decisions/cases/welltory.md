# Welltory — an onboarding that sells with the user's own data, at $18M ARR

## Idea — how they found it
- Mature company (10-year anniversary in 2026), not a fresh launch — [00:05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=306s).
- Human problem: people come home with no energy left and doom-scroll; Welltory promises "get your evenings back" by showing where energy drains during the day — [00:03:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=220s), [00:04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=276s).
- Value is *interpretation*: Apple Health shows an HRV number but does not interpret it — [00:12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=737s), [00:12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=753s). Competitors named: Rise, Bevel — [00:43:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2619s).
- Her biggest lesson: spend months describing the human problem concretely (the moment in the day it hurts most); "not something AI can do for you" — [00:20:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1251s), [00:21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1270s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Reads Apple Watch signals → measures "sedentary stress" (elevated heart rate while still), recovery, energy — [00:10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=621s), [00:10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=636s).
- **Six-ingredient pitch** used for onboarding: human problem → product promise → "key" (what only you know) → mechanism → proof → FOMO (what they lose *today* if they don't pay) — [00:18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1118s). The FOMO step is where she "kept failing spectacularly" — [00:19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1165s).
- **Screen 1 = data-sync CTA**, no intro slides ("Your Apple Watch knows what makes you tired and stressed"). Every prelude she tried did worse, though she admits it is under-tested; >90% connect health data — [00:27:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1642s), [00:27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1649s).
- **Notification permission asked right away**, against best practice — "it has worked best for us right there" — [00:28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1709s).
- Personal "key" from real data: "today you already have 192 minutes of it… this is all real" — [00:21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1318s); then an empowerment turn (your body recovered here; you only relieve ~20–30% of stress) with a 30-day animated stress calendar — [00:31:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1913s), [00:33:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1982s), [00:33:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2005s).
- **Proof:** the strongest proof is the user's own data, above reviews/millions of users/journal publications/university partners — [00:23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1391s), [00:23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1412s). A trust screen is placed right before the "woo-woo science" — [00:29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1796s).
- Segmentation question insight: ~70% of women answer "low energy/drained"; men answer "sometimes stressed / no complaints" even with worse data — [00:29:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1759s).
- Story arc > copy: as ex-copywriter, polishing words did not work until the whole arc was right — [00:25:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1543s).
- **Paywall:** pre-paywall headline is the problem ("stress is building right now"), not benefits. A benefits/progress ending ("you'll feel this much better in a week/month") tested worse — [00:35:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2127s), [00:36:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2176s). Her rule: trigger-driven apps (Uber, Spotify) sell benefits; no-trigger categories (health, language learning) close better on loss/FOMO — [00:37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2251s), [00:38:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2284s).
- Pricing: mostly a single annual plan, $99.99/yr (shown as $8.33/mo), sometimes a monthly option; "hardish" paywall — no trial, closable — [00:39:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2366s), [00:40:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2405s), [00:40:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2413s).
- Free tier keeps "vital"/potentially life-saving metrics (energy, stress, sleep score, blood pressure); paid = trends, day timeline, recovery recommendations — [00:41:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2475s), [00:41:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2508s). This is what drives the long conversion tail.

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Heavy Meta ads. Long-term promises ("you'll be less tired") "tend to work poorly" on Meta; ads must break through with something shocking: "something scary is probably happening inside your body" — [00:06:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=395s), [00:06:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=410s).
- Ads sell the premise, not features: don't build the specific features an ad shows; extract the overall promise ("make the invisible inside your body visible") — [00:09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=562s).
- Marketing and product lived in silos for years; spending 1.5–2 years closing that rift had "a big payoff" (no number given) — [00:08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=534s), [00:09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=556s).
- App Store first asset is a video; tagline "find out how you're really feeling in 2 minutes" tees up the onboarding — [00:17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1028s).

## Lessons → decisions
- If your app can read data the user already has (HealthKit, photos, screen time), use it as the first screen and as the proof; it beat social proof here → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Asking permissions (data, notifications) early can work when the request *is* the value promise — test it rather than following "value first" dogma → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- For no-trigger categories (health, learning), end the funnel on loss/FOMO, not benefits; benefits ending tested worse → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- A free tier with real daily value + a closable no-trial paywall can produce a long conversion tail (>60% after day 1) → measure conversion over weeks, not just D0 → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Meta ads for an abstract benefit need a concrete, slightly scary hook; ads sell the premise, onboarding delivers the specifics → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)

## Caveats
- Single operator, self-reported; no A/B numbers given for the sync-first screen, notification timing or benefit-vs-FOMO test ("worked worse" only). Host slips once to "16 million ARR" — [00:05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=320s); she says 18M twice.
- Ad claims vs product: an ad reads "this Apple Watch app catches high cortisol" — [00:05:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=349s), while she says "if anyone tells you they can measure cortisol… that's not true" — [00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=615s). Fear-based health ads carry Apple/Meta policy and trust risk.
- Requires an Apple Watch — the ">90% sync" is among people who already own one and came from a watch-specific ad.
- 10-year-old brand with research partnerships; small apps cannot copy the proof stack. Superwall sponsor reads (Rork, paywallexperiments) in the episode.
