# RizzGPT · Umax — the template the 2025–26 generation copied (seed a niche content wave, then out-spend the copycat)

> **Source note.** The primary interview is on Omar Waseem's channel ("The 23-Year-Old Genius Building Million Dollar Apps"), not in the Superwall corpus, so `search.py` will not find it. Numbers below were verified against that video's YouTube auto-captions (fetched 2026-10-08); timestamps are caption-snippet starts (±3 s). Superwall-corpus mentions are listed separately and are all secondhand.

## Idea — how they found it
- RizzGPT: a roommate kept asking what to text girls on dating apps, a few months after ChatGPT launched → have GPT generate replies — [00:03:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=223s). He was broke (brother lending grocery money) — [00:04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=248s).
- Umax: after leaving RizzGPT he browsed social media for under-served spaces; "looksmaxxing" had taken over TikTok, and viewers kept asking two things — how attractive am I, how do I improve — [00:06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=415s), [00:07:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=436s), [00:07:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=477s). Timing: GPT Vision API had just shipped — [00:08:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=486s).
- Pattern: an existing content wave + a new model capability + a question the audience already asks in comments.

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- RizzGPT: upload a screenshot of the chat → suggested replies — [00:05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=305s). Launched with no notifications, no reviews, "horrible" paywall — "a train wreck", but it delivered value and was viral — [00:05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=347s).
- Umax: selfie → GPT Vision rating (jawline score etc.) → improvement tips. Core loop "download → scan face → get results" was protected; feature additions that risked revenue were rejected — [00:15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=909s).
- Built with no software background: ChatGPT wrote ~95% of RizzGPT's code and ~90% of Umax's — [00:08:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=524s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **RizzGPT:** TikTok launch did "reasonably well"; the break came from an untapped niche of "rizz" content (people showing off texting skills). Two faceless accounts, $50 promo each — [00:05:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=317s), [00:05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=326s).
- **Outreach to unreachable faceless accounts:** DM on TikTok → Instagram → their mom → spam their Discord, until they reply — [00:23:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1423s).
- **Umax:** partnered (equity) with looksmaxxing influencer Sam Zia (~200k followers then); launch video ~100k views, a few thousand downloads, ~$100k revenue in month one — [00:09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=545s), [00:10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=654s). Rationale: in a smaller niche (looksmaxxing vs weight loss), owning the top voice "confers massive benefits"; in a huge market (Cal AI) no single influencer matters, so Cal AI used only paid influencer marketing — [00:21:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1299s), [00:22:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1323s).
- **Copycat response:** mid-January a clone copied everything and passed them in the App Store. He had been holding back to fix retention ("not burn the forest"); instead spent >$200k on influencers in 4 days — more than they had earned to date — [00:11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=705s), [00:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=746s), [00:13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=831s). Clone hit Umax's 1.5-month download count in 2 weeks, but Umax then ran ~4x its downloads and revenue — [00:14:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=879s).
- **Influencer selection (heuristic):** most influencers produce negative ROI, a few produce 10x; with influencers attribution is hard (unlike split-testing 100 paid creatives), so immerse yourself in the niche until you know whom its audience obeys — [00:19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1162s), [00:19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1189s), [00:20:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3FSoXHjEfs&t=1249s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Ride an existing content wave whose comment section already asks the question your app answers; a new model capability (GPT, Vision) is the opening → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Ship ugly if distribution is ready; fix retention later — but know you are spending your one first impression → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Cheap paid promos on niche faceless pages can test demand for ~$100 → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Small niche → partner (equity) with its top voice; huge market → many paid influencers → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- When a clone copies you, speed of distribution spend decided the winner here → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Same template seeded GlamUp, Sway, PrayScreen and Cal AI — see [cal-ai](cal-ai.md) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)

## Caveats
