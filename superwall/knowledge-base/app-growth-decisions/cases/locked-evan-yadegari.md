# Locked — a teenager's influencer-deal playbook, with the unit math to price it

## Idea — how they found it
- Pain he sees in his own cohort: Gen Z struggling to focus amid "brain rot"; a friend asked for a gamified app to stay on task. Applied video-game mechanics (XP, competing) to self-improvement — [00:04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=254s), [00:04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=277s), [00:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=300s).
- Ideation rule: problems you or similar people face, with a big enough TAM; trends (e.g. the "imposter" party game) and new AI model releases are secondary openings — [00:26:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1562s), [00:25:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1515s).
- Background is content, not code: grew a gaming-site TikTok from ~30k to ~100k followers, paid $1k then $2k/month at 12–13 — [00:03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=191s), [00:03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=207s). Prior app Problem Pal reached $2k/mo, mostly from one ~200k-view founder video — [00:03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=226s), [00:21:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1267s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Tested Reddit, X, influencers and paid ads; influencers won — [00:06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=373s), [00:06:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=378s). (The cold open splices "Reddit the most effective channel" — [00:00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=40s) — contradicting his own answer; trust the full answer.)
- **Creator niches:** self-improvement, gym, and "day in the life"; DITL was best because the creator naturally checks off tasks in the app all day — [00:06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=418s), [00:07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=453s).
- **Reference video:** Jeremiah Jones, hook "this schedule will keep you locked in for 2026", app shown after every task: ~1M views across IG/FB/TikTok, ≥$3k revenue, first $1k day and first 1,000-download day — [00:08:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=494s), [00:08:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=520s), [00:08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=534s).
- **Selection:** followers "don't matter at all"; look at views (5k to millions per video) and comment quality — real questions, replies between commenters, few emoji-only comments. Ignoring this was his biggest early loss — [00:10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=604s), [00:18:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1139s), [00:19:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1157s).
- **Outreach:** search niche terms on IG/TikTok, DM in bulk; 3–4% reply rate on Instagram (from an account with 1k+ followers and verified), ~2% on TikTok; ~100 DMs/day per account before rate limits, so multiple accounts; 500+ DMs/day at peak; 5–10k creators contacted in total — [00:11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=687s), [00:11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=696s), [00:12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=720s), [00:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=733s), [00:12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=743s).
- DM opens with "paid promo?" to stand out in creator inboxes; always push to a call rather than negotiate in DMs — [00:13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=782s), [00:13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=807s).
- **Four deal structures** — [00:13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=823s):
  1. Flat: ~$500 for 1 video or $1,200 for 4 — only for creators who consistently pull big views.
  2. CPM: $1–2 per 1k views with a cap (e.g. $500); CPM must stay below your RPM — [00:13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=836s).
  3. Minimum-view clause (his best): e.g. $1,000/post with a 500k-view floor; creator keeps posting until the videos sum to the floor — [00:14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=856s), [00:15:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=944s).
  4. Bonus: e.g. $250 + $250 if the video passes 250k views; can be mixed with the others — [00:14:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=875s).
- Setting the floor: price at a $1–2 effective CPM ($800 → 400–800k views); optional pro-rata exit ($400 for 400k; stops at 50k → paid $50) — [00:15:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=951s), [00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=975s).
- Brief: show the app within the first 15 s (earlier reads as an ad, later loses retention); seed a "what's this app called? I need this" comment from a personal account, creator replies and pins it; posts approved before going live, otherwise creative freedom — [00:17:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1022s), [00:17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1031s), [00:17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1055s).
- Attribution by download spikes (Instagram gives no link attribution); renew winners, then renegotiate bigger deals — [00:17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1070s), [00:18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1096s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Price creator deals from your own RPM (revenue per 1k views): keep effective CPM under it; a minimum-view clause converts a flat fee into a guaranteed CPM → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Pick creator *formats* where the app is used naturally on camera (day-in-the-life for a task app), not the most on-topic niche → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Vet by comment quality and per-video views, not followers → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Test several channels cheaply, then commit to the one that moves installs (here influencers over Reddit/X/paid) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Cheap annual ($30) + weekly ($7) with ~10% download→paid is enough for a creator-driven app to be CPM-positive at $1–2 → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Compare with the adult-scale version of the same machine: [cal-ai](cal-ai.md) (minimum-view clauses, 15-second rule).
