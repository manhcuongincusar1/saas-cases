# StudyFetch — design the feature to be filmable, then shift from "virality at all costs" to high-intent audiences

## Idea — how they found it
- Founders first ran an essay-writing platform earlier in 2023 that "was doing pretty well", then pivoted to a learning platform because "a cheating platform" did not contribute to education — [07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=465s). Demand already existed; they changed the product face.
- Launched 15 Sep 2023 — [03:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=215s). Kieran was asked to join on launch day only to make TikToks about the product — same timestamp.

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Upload lecture slides / textbooks / notes → AI study plan, quizzes, flashcards, AI tutor "Sparky" — [07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=433s).
- **Features designed to be visually viral before filming**: "we really tried to make sure they were optimized for virality online" — the live-lecture feature streams notes onto the screen; on camera the hands are visibly away from the keyboard = the wow — [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s), [26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1609s).
- Rule: when the product itself is why the video works, it converts; a video about something else with the product looped in the last ~5 s "is not going to convert well" — [32:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1925s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **First format**: camera pointed at a laptop screen, at a desk or in a real lecture, showing features in real time. First video (TikTok cross-posted to Reels): ~3M views overnight, "tens of thousands of sign-ups" in 24 h — [04:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=260s). Kieran had never made a product TikTok (photo/video background) — [45:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2723s).
- **Lecture-hall video** (Dec 2023): 75M views (host's figure) → "hundreds of thousands of sign ups"; Kieran credits novelty — few AI study tools existed then — [32:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1975s), [33:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2033s), [34:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2054s). Problem/solution formats "worked very well a couple years ago" but are harder now because of copycats — [30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1850s).
- **Three creator strategies, "in general an even mix"** — [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=724s): (a) hundreds of normal UGC creators (peak almost 1,000) — [43:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2582s); (b) **zero-follower brand accounts run by influencers** — the influencer opens a new StudyFetch account and their audience follows over; (c) occasional sponsored posts. Attribution between them is "very tough"; evidence the influencer accounts work is mostly comments asking about the product — [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=689s).
- Zero-follower deal pricing: "five-figure a month range for multiple months", typically **10–20× a UGC creator** (UGC = a couple hundred to a couple thousand $/month) — [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=811s); ~$10k/month total for ~daily posting from an influencer with ~750k followers on her main account — [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1069s), [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1085s). Fails "all the time" (shadowbans, wrong reach) — a numbers game — [18:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1124s).
- **Creator selection**: comments above everything; a 10k-view creator with an active comment section beats a big account without a following — [14:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=857s). Prefer TikTok Shop creators ("paid on affiliate, so they have to make videos that convert") and brand-deal veterans — [23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1425s). Cross-check strong IG creators on TikTok; weak on TikTok → skip — [23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1391s).
- **Pay**: base $50–$2,500 per video historically — [41:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2519s); bonus **$100 per 100k views** on TikTok or IG — [41:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2483s). Recruiting page pitch: "Earn up to $1,200 per hour" — [40:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2452s).
- **CTA by creator type**: demo creators put the website on screen for the last 4–5 s (hard CTA) — [27:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1668s); known influencers integrate the product 2–3× into lifestyle vlogs with no visible CTA because "people are naturally curious" — [29:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1791s). Every creator has "StudyFetch" in the account name — [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1000s).
- **TikTok > Reels for conversion**: TikTok audience "way higher intent"; viral Reels can convert "close to none" (international audience) — [19:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1169s). Target >80% US audience; US creators mostly land US viewers — [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s).
- **Team**: contractors for creator ops failed (misaligned incentives) → full-time hires — [37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2252s). Outbound sourcing team (TikTok/IG DMs, email, LinkedIn) preferred over inbound applications — [38:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2284s); full-time creative managers; a head of creator program — [38:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2315s).
- **Shift**: from "virality at all costs" to receptive audiences (med students, type-A learners who "don't mind that the video is an ad") — [06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=364s); "if we're going for views, which we aren't anymore, we will hire as many creators as possible" — [43:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2597s). Series A money went to product, scaling the creator program and brand content (cafe pop-ups) — [36:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2219s), [36:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2176s).
- First marketing hire advice: a growth generalist; TikTok Shop talent for execution ("they know how to sell") — [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2837s), [48:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2882s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Build the core feature so its wow reads on camera in seconds (hands-off-keyboard live notes) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Pick creators by comment engagement and selling skill (TikTok Shop), not followers; outbound > inbound → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Influencer-run zero-follower brand accounts as a hedge against "this is an ad" fatigue; expensive (10–20× UGC), frequently fail → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- For a US-monetized app, TikTok converts better than Reels; check viewer geography, not views → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Once scaled, trade raw views for high-intent sub-audiences and fewer creators → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- All numbers self-reported by an employee; revenue given as ranges ("seven figures MRR"). No paywall/pricing data in this interview.
- The intro narration claims the zero-follower accounts "are outperforming everything else" [00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=54s); Kieran himself says attribution is "very tough" and the mix is even — treat the outperformance claim as unproven.
- Main follower count of the influencer is inconsistent in-interview (Gemma: 100k on main account [08:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=531s); later "close to 750,000" for "her" main account).
- Lecture-hall success (Dec 2023) relied on category novelty; Kieran says it is reproducible only with a genuinely original product. Hosted by Superwall (sells paywalls); SpyTok sponsor read inside the episode.
