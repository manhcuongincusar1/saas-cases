# Clover Labs — industrialised format cloning and AI-search seeding (agency claims; several tactics break platform rules)

## Idea — how they found it
- Thesis: do not invent content, clone what already works. "Cloning content is really about 90% of any campaign… 10% is the crazy experiments worth testing" — [00:03:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=211s).
- Started as an AI-search product (mass publishing on Reddit + blogs), then moved into TikTok/Instagram — [00:29:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1770s). Company "a year and like three weeks old" at recording — [00:39:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2351s).
- Roadmap: four "leaves" (search, video, ads, outreach), then private-equity style — buy companies and run distribution on them agentically — [00:39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s), [00:40:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2403s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Format discovery: database of ~4M TikTok/IG accounts scraped continuously, plus live search for competitor posts worth cloning — [00:09:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=547s).
- One-click slideshow cloning with subtle/moderate/dramatic variation sliders, explicitly so TikTok does not detect a duplicate — [00:04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=254s), [00:08:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=518s). ~6–7 min per cloned set incl. image generation — [00:09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=595s).
- Posting infrastructure: own physical-phone network for North America, rented phones for other countries (~half of campaigns); can launch accounts in 20 countries — [00:11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=687s), [00:12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=727s). (The live demo had US accounts only — [00:27:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1636s).) Accounts post 2–3×/day at random — [00:27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1649s).
- "Hook & stitch": split the first ~2 s hook from the demo (AI-detected cut), swap demos/hooks; "evergreen" campaigns auto-repost any creator video above a threshold (e.g. 1M views) with a new hook/demo on other accounts — [00:17:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1062s), [00:19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1146s).
- AI translation: subbed, dubbed and lip-synced per dialect; lip-sync was the recent unlock — [00:13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=821s), [00:25:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1521s).

## Marketing — the tactics they report
**Clone rule (slideshows).** Trucker slideshow: original 110k views; clones did ~34k in 1.5 weeks (~1/3). Rule: median clone ≈ 20% of the original, so 5–10 clones ≈ the original's volume; extreme clones do 2–6× the original — [00:03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=232s), [00:04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=282s), [00:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=292s), [00:05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=320s). Variables tested: hook, CTA, avatar; for slideshows "the aesthetic of the account matters more than anything else" — [00:05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=351s), [00:06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=363s).

**Multiply by country.** Each new country adds "about a 20% lift across any view campaign" (rule of thumb: 1M views/mo → +200k); ~5 countries work well: Spain easiest, Germany, Australia; then Italy, Brazil. Adding Canada to a US campaign = "easy 20%" (stated from ads, "same is true of organic") — [00:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=863s), [00:14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=877s), [00:15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s), [00:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=966s). Rhyme (travel apps): a real, non-AI video with 37.4M views was re-posted with German text overlay; a *different* video was dubbed + lip-synced into German, because Germany was Rhyme's highest-converting audience outside North America (higher purchase/repurchase; conversion "pretty much around the same") — [00:13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=781s), [00:13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=795s), [00:13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=821s), [00:15:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=902s).

**Repost math.** A creator posting 2×/day = 60 posts/mo; ~10 clear the threshold; make 3 variants each → 30 posts on another account. Instagram tolerates reposting the same video "without any hard restrictions", TikTok less — [00:20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1227s), [00:20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1250s).

**Paying for campaigns.** Base + performance is best (e.g. $3/post + $3.5 CPM). Cloned/proven content → overweight base; new-format exploration → pay CPM (higher), switch to base once the format is proven — [00:28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1700s), [00:28:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1714s).

**AI search (RedRover).** (1) List ~2,000 prompts; no public prompt volume exists, so take Google volume × an assumed ~30% (e.g. "best app for food tracking" 15k/mo on Google → "let's say 5,000" on AI search); Ahrefs / Search Console suffice — [00:29:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1795s), [00:30:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1820s), [00:30:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1842s). (2) The hard part is finding which URLs each prompt cites — [00:31:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1882s); one Reddit post was cited for 5 prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overview (example query was B2B: "best mobile app development agency for an MVP") — [00:32:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1936s), [00:32:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1952s). (3) Sources drift: in the prior 3 weeks Gemini/Perplexity/AI Overview cited more Reddit/Quora/Pinterest; ChatGPT less Reddit, still blogs — [00:31:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1904s), [00:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1919s). (4) Seed: *post seeding* (new post mirroring the cited title/body to outrank it) or *comment seeding* (chatbots increasingly cite a comment, not the post) — [00:33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2024s), [00:35:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2124s). Link to social: when a viral video sends people to search the app name, a Reddit post/blog about the app surfaces — [00:38:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2298s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Before inventing formats, clone proven ones changing one variable; plan on ~20% of the original per clone and win on volume → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Pay base for proven formats, CPM for exploration → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Cheapest reach multiplier for a working format: Canada + 1–3 non-English markets with dubbed versions (check where your buyers already convert) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- AI search: estimate demand from Google volume, then target the *cited URL* (often a Reddit thread or comment), not a ranking → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Own what shows up when people search your app name after a viral video → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Do not read agency view totals as app outcomes when choosing a channel or vendor → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- **Agency, not an app operator.** Every number is agency-reported views across clients, or Clover's own revenue. No app revenue, retention or CAC is shown; the only conversion datapoint is ~$1k "in downloads" over 5 days. The AI-search example was a B2B query.
- **Conflict of interest.** Matt sells Echos and RedRover (links in the video description) and offers viewers a free month — [00:41:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2502s). Superwall, which hosts the show, is a Clover customer — [00:03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=180s). The host also plugs SpyTok in this episode.
- **Several tactics break platform rules.** Owning ~4,000 subreddits plus a network of accounts to keep seeded posts/comments live — [00:37:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2237s), [00:37:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2246s). Posting "my favorite is number five" five different times in one thread — [00:36:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2178s). Editing a cited comment so the chatbot's next refresh names only one company — [00:34:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2071s). Image tweaks to evade TikTok duplicate detection, plus mass accounts on owned or rented phones. This is coordinated inauthentic behaviour and vote manipulation, which Reddit's rules (and TikTok's and Meta's authenticity rules) prohibit. Bans or a chatbot changing how it picks sources can wipe it out overnight; the citation mix had already shifted within 3 weeks.
- Dated September 2026; AI-search citation behaviour changes fast. Treat it as a description of what the market does, not as a recommendation.
