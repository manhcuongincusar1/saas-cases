# Wrestle AI / Brawler — own an un-sponsored niche with its top creator, then scale on raw paid ads

## Idea — how they found it
- Saw a Rork ad a year earlier, "thought it was BS", and built Fight AI for himself: upload training footage, get told what you did right and wrong — [00:03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=213s), [00:03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=230s). He and friends posted about it and made about $1,000 at pure margin, which he took as validation — [00:04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=254s).
- Wrestle AI came from niching down: users kept asking "does it do wrestling?" — [00:04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=261s). Same template then reused per sport he practised: wrestling → boxing → an MMA app in negotiation with UFC names — [00:17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1042s), [00:17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1049s).
- Found his partner by accident: about to sponsor a smaller creator, he saw "followed by Kaden Henshaw" on that profile, cancelled the call and had the contact DM Kaden — [00:06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=368s). The intro came through a VA running his Instagram DMs, which he calls the first hire everyone should make — [00:05:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=330s), [00:05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=338s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Core loop in all three: upload a video (training, sparring, match) → AI breaks it down frame by frame → what you did right/wrong → drills — [00:17:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1065s). Everything built on Rork — [00:19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1164s).
- Brawler adds live modes: coach mode (a live AI call that stops until your hands are back up) and an arcade mode with music, "Beat Saber for boxing" — [00:18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1098s), [00:18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1118s).
- The combo-calling "voice AI" is Kaden's recorded voice, randomly mashed — [00:10:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=601s).
- **Daily-use feature beside the occasional one.** Match analysis only happens after a match, so a calorie tracker for making weight "helped our retention a ton" — [00:20:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1214s), [00:20:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1222s); an uploaded match schedule drives weight reminders — [00:21:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1307s).
- **In-app content feed** (Twitter-style list → TikTok-style page), idea taken from Ladder. Every signed creator must also post inside the app, which solves cold start; ranking is a simple point system weighted to new posts. Untested: "we're gonna see if it works" — [00:36:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2189s), [00:37:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2247s), [00:39:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2393s), [00:40:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2415s).
- **Notifications** reverse-engineered from Duolingo and tied to what the user did yesterday; he does not track which ones get clicks — [00:41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s), [00:42:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2544s).
- **Founder calls.** On the third app open Brawler offers a call with a founder; ~10–15 calls so far, about an hour a week. Finding: features built over weeks that users never noticed. A creator filming the wrong feature exposed the same clarity problem — [00:42:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2567s), [00:42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2578s), [00:43:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2601s), [00:43:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2616s). Fix: a short creator-recorded demo the first time a feature is opened — [00:44:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2679s), [00:44:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2698s).
- **Onboarding template** (same in all three): show the main feature → personalise with a few pain-point questions → build the program → paywall with an example (not personalised) breakdown — [00:19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1189s), [00:21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1265s), [00:22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1357s), [00:26:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1581s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Wrestle AI = creators only** until a week before recording: Kaden plus roughly 10 creators, trimmed over time to the ones that worked; never 100 — [00:09:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=543s), [00:29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1746s), [00:29:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1752s). Launch: Kaden hyped an App Store pre-order — [00:08:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=503s).
- **Why it was cheap:** the only wrestling app, so the audience had not been hit by sponsorships. Fitness is the opposite: Cal AI "fatigued" subtle integrations — [00:29:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1768s), [00:29:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1774s). Boxing has demand but too few creators who can both box and teach; pick creators whose audience wants to get good, not to watch — [00:32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1927s), [00:32:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1961s).
- **His sequencing rule (heuristic):** small budget → influencers ("you could 10x your money"); paid ads scale further but the pixel has to learn first — [00:29:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1782s), [00:29:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1793s).
- **Paid formats that work for him:** stitch / reaction videos that call out a first clip — [00:09:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=551s), [00:09:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=580s); "ranking the best apps" videos, which flop organically and "print" as ads — [00:11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=699s), [00:12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=730s); raw screen recordings with CapCut captions — [00:12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=761s). Polished visuals returned "zero" revenue at very high CPA — [00:13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s). Rule: clarity over polish, CTA inside the first 15 s — [00:10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=657s), [00:14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=849s).
- **Measurement:** attribution is "kind of cooked" (a contractor from Cal AI's ads side was just hired), so he steers by CPA against a computed break-even CPA — [00:14:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=890s), [00:15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=908s). No trial, so CPA means cost per purchase — [00:15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=946s), [00:15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=953s).
- **Scaling limit:** raising spend fast pushes CAC up; the bottleneck is creative volume ("10 times the amount of creatives") — [00:30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1827s), [00:30:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1835s), [00:31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1866s).
- **Influencers as trust priming for ads:** one boxing deal was bought at a planned loss so boxers would see a trusted name before the ads reached them — [00:27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1621s), [00:27:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1671s). Same logic behind a clipping campaign for AdMax he is willing to run below break-even — [00:33:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2021s), [00:34:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2093s).
- **Word of mouth is seasonal:** in season the whole team sees a wrestler upload his match; season runs roughly September to March — [00:16:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=980s), [00:16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1005s).

## Lessons → decisions
- A sport or hobby with passionate users and no app sponsorships yet is cheap to win with ~10 creators → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Giving the niche's top creator equity worked, but write in minimum posts and vesting (compare [fitted](fitted.md), [sunflower](sunflower.md)) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Launch with a creator-hyped pre-order (compare [runafy](runafy.md)) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- For paid: raw screen recordings, stitch/reaction and ranking formats beat polished creative; steer by CPA vs break-even CPA → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Influencer and clipping spend can be justified as trust priming that lowers paid CAC, but he has no measurement for it → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Pair an occasional "wow" feature with a daily one, add content and notifications, and call users → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- No trial, $59/yr vs $9.99/mo, plan mix ~50/50 → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
