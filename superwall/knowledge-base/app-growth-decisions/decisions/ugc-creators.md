# How do I run a UGC / creator program?

Companion tool: [UGC creator brief + deal checklist](../checklists/ugc-creator-brief.md). Channel choice comes first: [choose-channel](choose-channel.md). Boosting and paid: [paid-ads](paid-ads.md).

## Inputs to collect first
- **Has the founder already found a format that converts?** (own account, ≥ dozens of posts). If not, rule 1 applies before anything else.
- **Monthly creator budget and cash runway.** Also: will you put ad spend behind creator videos later? That changes which usage rights you need.
- **Product type.** Is it naturally viral and impulse (Gen Z, cheap, funny)? Or a considered, high-trust purchase (money, travel, health)? And is the app UI itself filmable?
- **Buyer profile.** Age, income, country. This drives creator age/persona and platform.
- **Time.** Who reviews videos daily? Founder only, or is there a manager?
- **Attribution available.** Tracked links per creator, an onboarding "where did you hear about us" question, App Store analytics, and a churn split by acquisition cohort.

## Default answer
1. The founder finds and proves the format first.
2. Then recruit a **small** roster of 5–8 creators with ~$2–4k for the first month. Look for camera-charismatic storytellers who are new to UGC, whose persona and age match the buyer. Follower count does not matter.
3. Pay a small guaranteed base plus capped view bonuses. Use milestones or tiered CPM, not pure CPM. For anyone with a big audience, add a minimum-view floor.
4. Brief with reference videos and hooks, not word-for-word scripts. Approve every video before it posts.
5. When a video beats your thresholds, have creators repost and remake it within days. Push it to the rest of the roster, then into paid.
6. Judge creators on installs, paid conversions and churn by cohort, not on views. AI-generated UGC is for testing formats, not the backbone.

## Decision rules
1. **If nobody on the team has made a video for this app go viral yet → the founder posts first, finds the format, and only then hires.** Early on, finding formats and reviewing every creator video is the founder's highest-value use of time. **[strong]** — [Nomad Table: "all the flopping's done on my end"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1596s) · [GlamUp: 3 accounts each, scaled only after a winner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s) · [Pingo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1141s) · [Driss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=3133s) · [SideShift 00:08:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=533s)
2. **Source formats, don't invent them.** Three levels (Joseph Choi):
   - *Copy* a format that worked for another app: fastest, saturates fastest.
   - *Iterate*: keep the video, change one variable.
   - *Referential concepting*: carry a whole concept into your niche. Riskiest, but you are first. Pingo did this and reached $500k/mo.

   In practice ~90% of output is clones or remixes (10–20% changed) and ~10% is experiments. Each remix should *improve* one element. Frame-for-frame copies flop. **[strong]** — [layers 00:08:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=513s), [Pingo 00:11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=684s) · [Clover 90/10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=211s) · [SideShift remix 10–20%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=432s) · [Jenni: identical outfits flopped](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1577s), [improve one element](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1615s) · [Nomad Table hook from another niche](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=829s) · [Jack Frics: scroll 1 h/day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=288s), [remade one video ~400×](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=323s)
3. **Every video must sell; the product has to be in it.**
   - Show the app (or its result) within ~15 s.
   - Frame it as the solution to a problem, not as a novelty.
   - A person talking to camera for at least 3–5 s converts better than 5-second text loops or silent reaction videos. Those formats farm views.
   - Keep the CTA soft. Casper never says the app's name out loud.

   **[strong]** — [Cal AI 00:22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1336s) · [Pingo 00:16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1005s) · [Jenni: viral video without the app "completely useless"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2034s) · [Coconote solution framing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=482s) · [Evo talking ≥3–5 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2362s), [text loops convert "very bad"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2338s) · [Payout: never say the name](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1235s), [CTAs reduce retention](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1202s)
4. **Pick the creator's voice by purchase type.**
   - **Considered, higher-trust purchase** (flights, buying a business, finance) → a *credible* creator: "ex-flight attendant, here's my #1 hack", or borrowed credibility ("my mom's an interior designer", a green-screen reaction to the founder).
   - **Impulse purchase** (a $40 dupe, a Gen Z Bible app) → a *relatable* peer: "I'm you, here's the secret".

   **[weak]**, one agency — [Evo: trust needed 00:10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=617s), [credible vs relatable 00:07:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=447s), [borrowed credibility 00:19:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1157s)
5. **Match the creator ICP to the buyer, not to the view count.** Evo's 20-year-old "get rich quick" creators drove 40M views and ~$1.2M for a buy-a-business marketplace, with ~45% churn. Switching to 35–40-year-old creators and a credibility format fixed it. Evo says 30+ women creators "convert the highest". Marcus Burke says older users convert better for "99% of apps". Also check who the audience is really watching for. **[medium]** — [Evo 00:17:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1033s), [00:17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1050s), [00:46:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2811s) · [Marcus Burke 00:44:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2657s) · [Cal AI: Love Island creator's viewers watched for his partner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1881s)
6. **Creator selection checklist. Ignore follower count.**
   - Real engagement, especially comments. Pep AI signed someone with 120k views in an hour, zero comments and zero signups.
   - At least ~5k views per video.
   - On-camera charisma and storytelling: emotive, a good first frame, an extroverted "yapper". Scout in unrelated niches.
   - Relatively new to UGC, so cheap and loyal.
   - Responsive on the onboarding call.
   - On ≤5 other campaigns.
   - TikTok Shop sellers know how to sell.
   - Don't trust past view counts.

   **[strong]** — [Evan: followers don't matter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=604s), [sub-5k pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=625s) · [Pep AI 00:44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2687s) · [StudyFetch comments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=909s), [TikTok Shop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1494s) · [Jenni camera charisma](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=420s), [unrelated niches](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=362s) · [SideShift: newish](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=593s), [storytelling](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=741s), [past performance misleads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=705s), [>5 campaigns = red flag](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=822s) · [Fitted: 50–100k-follower creators "know they're an influencer"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1613s)
7. **Start small and scale creators only after content-market fit.**
   - Test with 5–8 creators and ~$2–4k for the first month.
   - SideShift defines content-market fit as ~5–6 videos consistently above 10k views in the first month.
   - Fewer than 20 consistent creators made Cluely's 100M views in two weeks. Another company with 2,000 creators got 500k views in a month.
   - Then scale. Halo ran 85 creators and GlamUp had 200+ active at peak.

   **[medium]** (roster size is disputed, see below) — [SideShift first month](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=524s), [CMF](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=602s) · [Driss <20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=1463s), [2,000 → 500k](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=1493s) · [Coconote 10–12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=823s)
8. **Pay structure: guaranteed base + capped view bonuses beats pure CPM.**
   - Pure CPM made new creators quit early (Bloom).
   - Offered CPM vs base + milestones (and told CPM paid slightly more), 29 of 30 creators picked base + milestones (Halo).
   - Cap the per-video payout.
   - Pay high CPM while exploring new formats. Go base-heavy and low-CPM once you are cloning proven winners.
   - Real numbers: Thresholds table.

   **[strong]** — [Bloom 00:12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=730s) · [Halo 00:34:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2044s) · [Clover 00:28:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1714s), [00:29:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1742s) · [Nomad Table: CPM-only needs proof first](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2366s)
9. **If the creator has a real audience and is paid per post → add a minimum-view clause set below their own claim.** Example: they say "easily a million", you write 800k. Pay pro-rata if they fall short. **[medium]** — [Cal AI 00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=615s) · [Evan: $1,000/post with 500k floor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=856s), [pro-rata](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=975s)
10. **If you have no cash but a creator fits perfectly → crawl–walk–run.** Start at ~$20/video on a sub-account, then rev share, then equity. If one creator dominates the niche, hire them outright. **[medium]** — [Fitted 00:27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1662s), [00:28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1700s) · [Sunflower "VP of Sobriety"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=237s)
11. **Recruit at volume and expect ~1% to sign.**
   - Cal AI: 25 assistants × 100 outreaches/day each, mostly email; 220k contacted, ~1% signed.
   - A call ~10×'s the chance of a good deal.
   - Pay existing creators $100–200 to refer friends.
   - DM first line: "Paid promo / affiliate deal?" Then move to WhatsApp and a call.
   - Post in Facebook UGC groups: 30–50 applicants/day.

   **[strong]** — [Cal AI 00:34:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2093s), [~1%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2180s), [email](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2701s), [call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2278s), [referrals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2761s) · [Pep AI 00:42:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2527s), [1,000+/week](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1216s) · [Halo 00:11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=707s), [00:12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=774s) · [Alex: 1,000 Gmails × 10 emails/day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=73s)
12. **Brief with references, train, and approve before posting.**
    - Brief = reference videos, hook ideas, CTA slots and messaging themes.
    - Run onboarding as a course plus a content bank, with feedback in a group chat or via Loom walkthroughs.
    - Review every video before it goes live until a creator is proven. A manager who rubber-stamps makes TikTok "a lottery".
    - Visual hook rules (Evo): hands always doing something; extra faces to hold the first 5 s.

    **[strong]** — [SideShift brief 00:15:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=962s), [review all](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=972s), [Loom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1027s) · [Evo approves every video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1732s), [hands](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1737s) · [Joseph Choi 00:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=772s) · [GlamUp content bank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2761s) · [SideShift group chat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=636s)
13. **When a video beats your threshold → replicate within a day or so.**
    - Repost it (Instagram tolerates exact reposts better than TikTok).
    - Have the same creator re-shoot the same script with a new text hook.
    - Push it to every creator: Halo's 10M-view hit became ~80 same-day remakes at 2–3M each.
    - Collab-post it to the brand page.
    - Make 3 variants each of the top ~10 of 60 posts on other accounts. A clone averages ~20% of the original's views and occasionally 2–6× it.

    **[strong]** — [Evo repost rule 00:43:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2597s), [collab at ~20k](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2163s) · [Halo 00:26:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1569s) · [Bloom: to 20 accounts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=322s) · [Clover 00:20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1227s), [00:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=292s) · [Stronger: competitor ran our format on ~10 accounts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=564s)
14. **Before replicating, check whether the format won or the person did.** A post far above its account's median is format evidence; a famous face is not. Ryan's 66.9M-view video failed when 20–40 other creators copied it. **[medium]** — [Yannis >200× median 00:49:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2999s) · [Ryan 00:21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1278s)
15. **Run two jobs at once: awareness and conversion.**
    - Weeks 1–2: broad hooks, creator types and formats; optimise for views. Long-text and comment-bait posts are fine here and find the audience.
    - Then move the winning scripts into converting formats: talking head, credibility.
    - Cheap, hard-CTA posts with only 500–2,000 views act as retargeting for people who saw the viral one.
    - On Instagram, Stories with giveaways are the bottom of the funnel.

    **[medium]** — [Evo 00:50:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=3047s), [Stories 00:33:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2016s) · [Noise 00:03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=235s), [Noise 00:26:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1579s), [<500 views still convert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1883s) · [Yannis seed post 00:13:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=794s)
16. **Attribute per creator, and judge on revenue and churn, not views.**
    - Tracked link per creator (OneLink).
    - A benefit-named vanity domain per campaign.
    - An onboarding "where did you hear about us?" question.
    - Read App Store rank moves (organic hits surface first in several English-speaking countries, not the US).
    - Like-to-view ratio separates boosted videos from organic ones: 2M views/2k likes = boosted; 2M/50k = organic.
    - Split churn by creator cohort.

    **[strong]** — [Pep AI links](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=724s) · [Evo domain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=761s) · [Clear 30 question](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=523s) · [Yannis 00:47:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2869s) · [Payout 00:10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=624s)
17. **Put money behind creator winners, not more creators.** Budget ~20% for making content and ~80% for amplifying winners. Organic → paid is agreed by many operators; the specific boost thresholds below are **SideShift-only** ([Thresholds](#thresholds-to-score-yourself)). Don't pay view bonuses on paid (sparked) views. Mechanics in [paid-ads](paid-ads.md). **[strong]** for the principle, **[weak]** for exact thresholds — [SideShift 20/80](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=58s), [spark bonus trap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1786s) · [Pep AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=377s) · [Bloom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=920s)
18. **Organise the team by phase.** **[strong]**
    - *Discovery phase:* a strategist/coach who finds formats and reviews videos (founder first).
    - *Scale phase:* recruiters.
    - First hire is a generalist manager: ~$2–4k/mo pilot (~$150 per creator) + ~5% of creator bonuses.
    - Keep logistics (creator manager) separate from creative feedback (content coach). Coconote ran 1 coach per 12 creators.
    - Joseph Choi's three non-overlapping roles: head of social growth, creator manager/coach, talent scout.
    - Best source of managers: your top creators.

    Sources — [SideShift phases](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=483s), [manager pay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=311s), [5%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=341s), [creators → managers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2175s), [manager vs coach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=569s) · [Coconote 1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1180s) · [Joseph Choi 00:12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=741s)
19. **AI-generated UGC → use it for testing, not as the program.** **[medium]** — [SideShift: "I've not seen it convert"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=830s) · [Zuhair: AI 95%, human 5%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=934s) · [Evo: talking humans beat "AI bot"-looking video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2362s)
20. **Go international only after the core market works.** LATAM creators cost ~$15/video vs ~$25 in the US for similar quality. Test 3–5 creators per market, one market at a time, with a native-language manager. **[weak]** — [SideShift 00:33:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1987s), [00:35:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2102s)

## When the default flips
- **No time to manage people → own the creative and use "reposters".** Yannis opens 50–100 accounts/week and keeps creative in-house; ambassadors get an 80/20 structure ([00:00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=5s), [00:05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=359s), [00:24:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1490s)). The ~$20/video reposter cost is the host's estimate (*unverified* as an operator figure). Alternatively use a distribution platform or agency. Joseph Choi suggests that only around $50–100k/mo.
- **One creator owns the niche → hire them** instead of 30 micro-creators. Sunflower: 50–100M views/mo, 50–100k link clicks/mo from one person ([00:03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=233s), [00:04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=290s)).
- **"UGC reads as ads now" → pay real influencers to start brand-new branded accounts.** StudyFetch pays five figures/mo, about 10–20× a UGC creator, ~$10k/mo for a daily video ([00:01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=63s), [00:13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=811s), [00:18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1080s)).
- **Creators only as ad talent (no organic).** Alex: ~1 in 85 video ads works, so the whole machine exists to produce volume ([00:00:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s)). → [paid-ads](paid-ads.md)
- **Trust-heavy product whose UI isn't filmable** → credible creator + vanity domain + Stories CTAs (rules 4, 15, 16) instead of app demos.

## Evidence against / where operators disagree
- **Roster size.** Small, elite rosters: Driss <20, Coconote's "Navy SEALs, not the Navy" ([00:13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=806s)), SideShift's 5–8 to start. Large rosters: Halo 85, Nomad Table 60, Jenni 150, Pingo ~100, Sprout >1,000 cycled ([00:16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=970s)). *Reconcile by phase:* small until content-market fit, wide once a format is proven and cloneable.
- **Scripts vs freedom.** Line-by-line scripts with emotion and shot per line (Nicole, [00:46:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2773s)); Raphael writes scripts himself ([00:20:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1240s)); Yannis 80% fixed. Against: Canyon (no word-for-word scripts) and Cedric ("you know your audience best", [00:18:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1130s)). Cedric found his own scripts that copied competitors failed. *(An older synthesis listed Raphael as anti-script. The transcript says the opposite.)*
- **Does replication work?** Yes: Halo, Bloom, Stronger, Clover. No: Ryan's hit didn't transfer across 20–40 creators. Hits that depend on the person don't clone.
- **Is UGC still working?** StudyFetch (2026-05): "less and less effective" because audiences spot ads. SideShift and Evo (2026-09/10) report programs at 9–10-figure monthly views. Both sell, or depend on, their side.
- **Multi-account farms.** Yannis, Clover, Bloom use them. Kobe calls it arbitrage he avoids after being "screwed by platforms". Evo itself lost a ~1M-follower page to a ban ([00:30:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1851s)).
- **AI.** SideShift: AI UGC doesn't convert. Social app studio runs ~4,000 fully AI video *ads* a month profitably ([00:04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=267s)). Paid ads ≠ organic UGC. → [paid-ads](paid-ads.md)

## Traps
- **Paying for views from the wrong people.** Coconote: 40M views, low intent. Evo: 45% churn. Measure revenue and churn per creator.
- **Rewarding a creator for a viral video that never shows the app** ("completely useless", Jenni).
- **Pure CPM with new creators.** They quit before the account warms up (Bloom).
- **No cap and no floor.** One lucky video eats the budget; one flop wastes a big fee.
- **Paying view bonuses on views you bought with spark ads** ([SideShift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1794s)).
- **Signing on follower count or bought views.** Pep AI: 120k views in an hour, no comments, no signups.
- **Stopping experiments once one format works.** Driss calls this the main reason UGC programs fail ([00:01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=66s)).
- **Hiring an agency or big influencers before you know the format** ([Joseph Choi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=717s)).
- **Copying frame-for-frame** instead of improving one element (Jenni).

## Gaps
- No randomized comparison of pay structures or roster sizes. Each claim comes from one program and is self-reported, often by people selling creator services (SideShift, Evo, Clover, Noise, Consumer Club).
- No reliable organic attribution exists. Every method above is a proxy. There is no corpus data on incrementality.
- Contract and legal detail (usage rights duration, FTC/#ad disclosure, platform terms on multi-accounting and reposting) is barely covered. Don't fill this in from the corpus.
- Retention of users by creator persona is shown only by Evo's churn anecdote.
- Rates are 2025–2026 US-centric and decay quickly; re-check CPM and base rates before quoting them.
