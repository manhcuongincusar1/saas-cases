# Jenni AI — recruit for "camera charisma" not stats, write the on-screen copy yourself, and aim for engagement bait that still plugs the product

## Idea — how they found it
- Matt's own product: a fake-citation checker for students (startup name garbled in transcript) — vibe-coded over 11 months per narrator [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=0s). Solved one narrow slice of Jenni's problem; Jenni had "already solved fake citations", so the deal was "essentially in the end an aqua hire" — [44:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2673s).
- Acquisition started via Instagram DM: Jenni messaged one of his creator accounts to poach the creator; he replied offering an acquisition — [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2647s), [54:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3247s). DM said the CEO was "free right now"; the call happened the next day — [54:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3260s), [54:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3267s); ~4–5 months from February to close — [53:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3205s).
- His advice: niching down on a startup you admire works, but the deciding factor is the culture/person you'd work under — [44:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2689s).
- **Reverse-build experiment**: building a new study tool backwards from a TikTok format seen in another niche — "if we had a feature that did this… can we build it?" — [47:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2864s), [48:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2915s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Jenni = AI that writes essays *with* you, built by an ML-heavy product team; Matt calls growth "quite product-led" and sticky — [03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=180s), [44:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2673s). ## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Program results**: 0 → 75M views at < $2 CPM; 0 → 150 creators "in different forms" — [05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=300s), [05:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=306s). Target and result: 80 creators in the first month (joined early August) — [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=815s). Effort: "7 days a week, at least 16 hours" — [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=327s).
- **Creator selection — camera charisma**: ignores stats and Modash; scrolls creators in unrelated niches — [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=359s). Do I want to watch/rewatch? "If you can't answer [why], it's quite a good sign" — [07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=439s). Checklist: (1) emotive [07:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=475s); (2) pause on the first second — is it aesthetic (light, framing, even on-screen text size) = proxy for "viral sense" [08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=491s); (3) extroverted "yapper" who tells stories [08:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=536s). Hidden gems have one exceptional trait; the rest is teachable — [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=718s).
- **Patience**: best creator last month needed 25 videos to pass 10k views, then made "over five grand" in a month on bonuses — [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=743s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=746s). Don't expect a million views in week one — [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=730s).
- **Outreach**: response rates lower than mass influencer DMs (creators don't check DMs, skeptical of scam brands) but call close rate high — [15:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=911s), [15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=941s). DMs all lowercase, casual, specific social proof ("five, six million users") — [16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=969s). No track record → start with founder-led content.
- **Founder on camera first**: his first-ever TikTok (Dec 2024, talking to phone, adapted "absurdly long URL" format) did ~245k views — [18:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1122s), [19:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1176s). "You do not need to reinvent the wheel" at the start — [21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1296s).
- **Copy vs innovate**: mimicking is "not a bad strategy" to $10k–100k MRR; at hundreds of thousands of MRR "you need to be novel" — [04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=255s), [04:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=260s). Frame-for-frame copies (identical outfits/backgrounds by another brand) didn't go viral — [26:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1577s); ask "what's the one thing I would change to make it better?" — [26:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1612s).
- **Luck**: two near-identical videos (same script, creator, 3 days apart) had a 2,000× view gap — "it was luck"; optimize surface area for luck — [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1502s), [25:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1513s). Research: 30 min/day scrolling inside the niche, 30 min outside — [23:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1390s), [23:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1395s).
- **Engagement bait, not rage bait**: Ruby's 5-second clip (surprised face + text block he wrote: "just seen a girl in the library writing an essay. no notes, no open tabs, no ChatGPT, no Jenni AI… like a psychopath") got 30M organic views on Twitter after a screenshot; a defending quote-tweet got 15M — [28:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1713s), [31:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1878s), [32:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1942s). Sweet spot: a defensible point so comments split both ways.
- **Views must carry the product**: "A viral study TikTok that didn't mention Jenni is completely useless" — [33:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2034s). Higher-intent CTA line ("Chat, I am begging you to please use a humanizer for the grammar and Jenni for the fake citations") — copy "written verbatim by me" — [36:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2185s), [36:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2208s).
- **Who writes this**: acqui-hire young founders — they understand conversion, not just creativity — [37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2239s); learn from TikTok Shop / ecom — [40:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2438s). Creator retention via rapport: Ruby turned down other brands after going viral — [51:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3104s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Recruit low-follower creators on charisma/aesthetic/storytelling; expect ~25 videos before a hit; retain through rapport → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Founder makes the first videos to get social proof for outreach and the judgment to coach → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Every viral video must name/plug the product; tune between views and plug strength → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Build a narrow feature a bigger player needs → acqui-hire exit path; judge acquirer on people → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md), [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Reverse-build features from a proven content format → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
