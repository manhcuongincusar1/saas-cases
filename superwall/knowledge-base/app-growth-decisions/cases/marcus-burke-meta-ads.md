# Marcus Burke — how app ads differ from e-com ads, and how to structure a Meta account to scale

## Idea — how they found it
Not a founder story. 14 years of Meta UA — [00:02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=148s): mobile games at InnoGames, which spent "millions every month" next to King/Supercell — [00:04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=242s); Blinkist from 2019, early at sending paid traffic to a blog instead of the App Store (web-to-app) — [00:05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=347s); consulting since 2022 — [00:06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=376s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- He also works on onboarding, paywall and activation, because "register → paywall" is no longer enough to make Meta work — [00:06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=386s), [00:06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=416s).
- App ads use a *soft* CTA because the long onboarding does the selling — [00:14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=886s). A web funnel can sell people who are far from problem-aware; an app ad has to sell more up front because the App Store is a big friction point — [00:17:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1032s), [00:20:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1223s).
- Ask age in onboarding so you can compute trial→paid per age bucket later — [00:42:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2532s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Creative rules (2026-06).**
- D2C talking-head UGC (hook → pain → product) "isn't the best format for apps" — [00:07:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=477s).
- Prime the viewer that they are about to land on the App Store (say "app" in the copy, or show a demo), "otherwise your install rate tends to suffer" — [00:08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=502s), [00:08:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=515s).
- The $1M concept: an absurd sleep scene → glowing green orb → benefit lines; ~100 versions of the ad, ~400 iterations of the concept (copy, scenes, people). A tinnitus-masking angle came from research — [00:09:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=591s), [00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=615s).
- Text walls are a core format: emotional text in the audience's words + "we made this app" — [00:11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=702s), [00:12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=738s).
- **Copy = targeting.** Targeting is broad; highly specific language makes the right people engage, so the algorithm finds them. Generic copy drifts toward broad, hard-to-convert audiences — [00:13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=823s), [00:14:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=851s).

**Research pipeline.** Most of the time goes to research, not generation — [00:23:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1429s). Reddit (anonymous, honest, engagement shows what matters) → Claude clusters 10–20 high-engagement threads into sub-audiences, pain points, desires and vivid phrases → one context file per sub-audience — [00:24:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1461s), [00:32:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1957s). Also use your own and competitors' app reviews — [00:25:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1559s). An LLM without that context writes "marketing blah blah" — [00:33:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2028s). Train your own Reels feed on the audience: ~5 min of engagement → ~80% of the feed is from that audience — [00:30:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1818s).

**AI production.** Freelancers make "99% of it with AI". The reason is controllable, testable variables, not cost; he expects it to be cheap in ~6 months — [00:22:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1337s), [00:22:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1374s). Generate an image first (GPT Image 2 / Nano Banana), then animate it (Seedance 2 / Veo 3) via Higgsfield; Weavy/Flora node workflows produce hundreds of iterations of an established concept — [00:35:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2157s), [00:36:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2194s).

**Placement = targeting.** Message + media + content type × funnel = audience — [00:38:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2302s).
- Statics deliver to Facebook feed (older users); 9:16 video goes to IG/FB Reels (younger) — [00:21:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1262s), [00:39:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2359s).
- Street interviews pull young people; raw text walls pull older ones — [00:39:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2374s).
- App-promotion campaigns deliver statics poorly, so start with video and still test statics — [00:19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1163s), [00:20:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1258s).

**Age & value rules.** Cheap trials come from young, low-value users; older users cost more per trial but convert better — [00:43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2591s), [00:43:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2631s). Fix it with value rules (e.g. tell Meta 45+ is 30–40% more valuable), and make creatives that older users engage with — [00:44:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2657s). The optimization event must fire on day 0–1; trial conversion on day 7 is too late for the algorithm — [00:45:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2757s).

**Account structure.** If all ads share one ad set, Meta puts ~80% of spend on one ad and never tests other placements or pains. Pull out under-target outliers (e.g. Facebook-feed-heavy ads) into their own ad set — [00:47:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2874s), [00:48:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2894s). At scale: ad sets by media type → format (text walls / memes / UGC) → message (one pain = one ad set), with budgets set at **ad set** level and a target per bucket (memes must come in at ~half the cost because the traffic is lower quality) — [00:50:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3058s), [00:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3136s), [00:52:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3177s), [00:53:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3210s). **Bootstrapped/low budget:** one ad set, see what wins, repeat weekly until it stops losing money; split only once you are near break-even — [00:54:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3262s), [00:55:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3317s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Prime the App Store hop in the creative; organic-style "never say app" hooks do not carry over to app-install ads → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Copy and format are the targeting; scale by adding pains (messages) and media types, each in its own budgeted ad set → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Optimizing for cheap trials buys young, low-value users; check trial→paid by age and push spend older → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- The funnel (onboarding + paywall) has to sell before Meta can scale; fix it first → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Low budget: one ad set, weekly iteration, no fancy structure → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- **COI:** sells Meta-ads/funnel consulting (since 2022) and posts on LinkedIn/Substack. Video carries SpyTok, Rork and Superwall promotions.
- The "$2M/month" and "single ad >$1M" claims come from the host's framing. Marcus's own words: a *concept* with many iterations, "probably well above" $1M.
- Opinions, not controlled tests; the app examples (Moonate, Picnic, Nibble, Living) are partly other advertisers' ads, i.e. secondhand.
- Tool names (Seedance 2, GPT Image 2, Weavy) and the AI cost outlook are as of 2026-06 and will decay fast.
