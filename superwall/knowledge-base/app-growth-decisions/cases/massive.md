# Massive — waitlists don't convert; the words in your hook pick your audience

## Idea — how they found it
- Pivot after a $3M-funded product failed; an engineer suggested "use GPT to fill out job applications"; Dan thought it was dumb — [00:01:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=118s), [00:02:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=129s).
- Tested by posting "if I built this, would anyone want it?" on LinkedIn with ~500 connections → ~8M impressions, ~5,000 comments — [00:02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=140s), [00:02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=144s).
- Personal pain point he had thought about for years — [00:02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=124s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Upload résumé → matched jobs → AI customizes résumé/cover letter and applies on company sites daily. "Tinder for jobs" positioning is clearer than "job search on autopilot" — [00:01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=86s), [00:03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=194s).
- Hard paywall subscription: "if it solves a problem, people want to pay for it" — [00:03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=230s).
- **Doubled revenue from pricing changes** — [00:30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1848s) (no detail on what changed).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Launch (waitlist failure).** ~2 months to build; collected 40–50k waitlist signups via LinkedIn posts ("top 200 companies hiring now") and early UGC — [00:04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=263s), [00:04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=277s). At launch (April) only ~0.5% of the waitlist even signed up — [00:05:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=319s). Then manual grind: Hacker News, scraped emails, heartfelt DMs → first ~100–500 paying users; April→December to ~$20k MRR with one engineer, weeks from running out of money — [00:05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=347s), [00:05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=353s), [00:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=366s).
**His rule now:** "waitlists are useless" — get people to pay, even for a small version — [00:06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s), [00:06:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=388s). To validate a new app: $1–2k max on a handful of influencers with proven consistent views and a tight value prop; not UGC (too slow to iterate) — [00:06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=407s), [00:06:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=412s).

**Audience targeting through words.** "Stop using LinkedIn to apply to jobs" → ~1M views and converts; same script with "Indeed" → almost no conversion and hate comments (blue-collar audience won't pay) — [00:09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=561s), [00:09:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=565s), [00:09:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=571s). Organic targeting = calling out the audience in the first 3 seconds — [00:10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=604s). College students and skits don't convert — [00:08:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=517s), [00:09:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=547s).

**Views ≠ conversions.** An 11M-view video produced only ~$20k MRR; watching live, conversions came only between ~3M and 3.5M views as the algorithm hit the right audience briefly — [00:17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1028s), [00:17:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1065s), [00:17:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1072s). Now 3–4M views/week, but at ~500k/week they converted about as much — scaling views broke conversion — [00:07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=461s), [00:07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=466s). Learn which videos convert while volume is low — [00:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=491s).

**Formats.** Organic at scale = ~80% replicate proven viral formats, ~20% new (*heuristic*) — [00:13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=807s). Same format, same creator: 382 views vs 1.7M; the difference is authenticity/micro-expressions and weeks of iteration — [00:15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=959s), [00:16:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1001s).

**UGC vs paid.** UGC "doesn't scale that hard" for him (would need 200–400 creators on one style; heavy management); a restarted UGC program produced the 11M video in 4 days then died — [00:19:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1175s), [00:20:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1252s). If conversions matter, paid gives faster, real conversion data — [00:20:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1207s). Doing ads "in a specific way" added ~50% revenue — [00:30:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1851s). Monthly-retainer influencers got views but "killed by lack of conversions" — [00:31:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1905s).

**Red line.** Calls out a competitor that copied Massive's onboarding and ran fake "I landed a job" testimonials / comment-seeding — "definitely illegal" (FTC risk) — [00:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1300s), [00:22:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1344s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Validate with a public "would you use this" post, then charge immediately; don't build a waitlist → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Audience = who pays. The noun in the hook (LinkedIn vs Indeed) selects the audience; older/white-collar audiences pay more per download → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Judge videos by conversions, not views; if you can't attribute, paid ads give cleaner signal → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Pricing changes doubled revenue; hard paywall from day one → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported; no dashboard. "$2M ARR" is the title; Dan says "about 200K a month" without exact figures.
- Job search is a high-intent, white-collar niche; his conclusion that UGC "doesn't scale" is product-specific (he says so — [00:20:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1237s)).
- Mid-2025 evidence. Host is a Consumer Club founder; Dan is a member; Superwall sponsors.
