# Social — buy apps after their viral peak, then grow them with AI ads and constant A/B tests

## Idea — how they find apps
- Two theses: (1) retentive utilities that sit inside existing behavior (keyboards, fonts, stickers); (2) apps that look like a short viral spike but tap a huge-TAM curiosity. Many bought "after they had a viral peak, then they were declining" and the sellers didn't know how to grow — [00:09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=593s).
- Celebs (celebrity look-alike) is the example: ~6 years old, "bigger than ever"; Patrick's TAM argument is that at least half of people are curious who they look like (*heuristic*) — [00:09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=593s), [00:11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=716s).
- Diligence = top-down market (willingness to pay, growth) + bottom-up: they **run standardized ads for apps that don't exist yet or that they are considering buying** and compare against their benchmark — [00:06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=413s), [00:07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=421s). Buy test: "can we scale this long-term profitably" — [00:11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=667s).
- Avoid: games (different skill set) and most social networks (need huge scale to monetize) — [00:05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=336s), [00:05:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=356s).

## Product — monetization machine
- Growth loop: good UA brings users → ~dozens of A/B tests per app per month raise revenue per user → can afford more UA. RPU roughly **3× over 18 months, 2× over 12 months** across apps — [00:13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=825s). Portfolio LTV:CAC ≈ **3×** — [00:14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=866s).
- Test hit rate (Pablo, *heuristic*): of ~20 tests, 10 neutral, 5 terrible, 3 interesting, 2 good; scale winners from small to big traffic — [00:15:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=939s).
- **Removing the onboarding paywall on Celebs was a big win**: get users fast to the "moment of maximal intrigue" — [00:16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s), [00:16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=970s). It had failed in earlier tests and only won once the other ~10 conversion placements in the app (share, reopen…) were optimized — [00:16:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=983s), [00:17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s), [00:18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1098s). Re-run old tests when the context changes — [00:16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1017s).
- Counter-rule for a new app: unless you know a *better* moment to ask for the subscription later, keep the paywall in onboarding (hard or trial) — [00:22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1377s), [00:23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1391s). Friction can also be useful: long onboarding creates conversion signal for ad optimization — [00:19:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1172s).
- Every test is tagged with hypothesis + result + learning so AI agents and new hires can reuse it — [00:19:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1151s).
- Wise Sam (consumer OpenClaw agent): web funnel from ads, long question-heavy onboarding ("happy friction") that reflects answers back, then $1 first week → $10/week via Stripe (host reading the screen) — [00:26:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1587s), [00:28:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1683s), [00:28:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1701s). Too new for results.

## Marketing — the AI ad machine
- ~4,000 video ads/month, hundreds of playables, thousands of statics; ~100 A/B tests/month — [00:04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=267s), [00:04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=271s), [00:04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=276s). "Fully AI for all our ads for over 2 years" — [00:01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=87s).
- Humans supply ideas/taste; AI executes. Ads team = 6 people; the creative team **tripled** in 6 months while automating, because more volume needs more idea diversity — [00:41:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2480s), [00:42:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2551s), [00:43:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2619s). Editing partly outsourced to an agentic editing vendor — [00:44:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2642s).
- "Infinite search space": endless variations of one winner fatigue; keep exploring new concepts, then exploit pockets that work; you cannot brute-force (not enough impressions to test) — [00:46:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2762s), [00:48:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2889s), [00:48:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2925s).
- They don't copy the market leader (they usually are it); they mine other industries' marketing for ideas — [00:47:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2869s), [00:50:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=3018s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Buying a declining post-viral app can beat building, if you own a UA + monetization machine; pre-test demand with standardized ads → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md), [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Onboarding paywall is the default; removing it wins only after downstream paywall placements are strong — test, and re-test later → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- AI ad production scales volume but needs *more* human idea people, not fewer; explore concepts, don't just vary winners → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported; app-level revenue rounded. The onboarding-paywall result has no lift number.
- Studio advantages (data across 22 apps, ad benchmark, team) don't transfer to a solo founder; their own advice for small apps is the opposite default.
- Episode carries Superwall and Roark sponsor reads; Social is pitching itself (hiring, positioning as acquirer). Mid-2026 tactics.
