# How should onboarding work, and how do I fix retention?

> **Read with care.** Onboarding evidence here is mostly about *converting at the paywall*; very few operators share retention curves. The corpus is dominated by distribution experts, and several admit retention is their weak spot (see Traps). Superwall (paywall vendor) hosts the show, so "onboarding exists to sell the paywall" is the house view.

## Inputs to collect first
- Screen-by-screen onboarding map with drop-off per screen; install → paywall-view rate.
- What must happen for a user to feel value, and whether it can happen *inside* onboarding without leaving the app (photos to upload, data to sync, a scan, a first result).
- Data you can read with one permission (Apple Health, screen time, camera roll) to personalize.
- Age split (under-25 vs 25+) and where traffic comes from (which ad/video promise the user arrives with).
- Retention numbers: D1/D7/D30 for free users, trial cancel rate, trial→paid, month-2/3 churn by acquisition cohort or creator, year-1 renewal rate.
- Usage shape: is there a natural daily trigger (hunger, a daily task) or must you build one (health, language, relationships)?

## Default answer
Treat onboarding as a **sales pitch**, not a data form: lead with a curiosity promise that matches the ad, explain what the app does, ask a handful of easy tap-to-answer questions that personalize, name the user's problem with *their* data or answers, show how the product fixes it, prove it, close on what they lose by waiting, then the paywall. Longer usually beats shorter when the added screens personalize or prove (Coconote +16% going from 5 to ~13 screens), but keep it short for under-25s. Let users feel the core mechanic before paying only if it costs them no effort. For retention, build a **daily trigger** (carrot-and-stick notifications, streaks, widgets, groups), measure churn by **audience cohort**, and don't scale acquisition into a product whose trialists cancel or whose renewals collapse.

## Decision rules
1. **Design onboarding as a pitch that primes the purchase, not as analytics.** **[strong]** — [Welltory](../cases/welltory.md) six-ingredient pitch: problem → promise → key → mechanism → proof → FOMO ([00:18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1118s)); [Nicole](../cases/glamup-sprout.md) "a lot of people think onboarding is for data analytics… I completely disagree" ([00:17:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1064s)); [Sway/Cal AI](../cases/sway-ai.md) goal is "to raise your motivation" before the paywall ([00:15:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=952s)); [Clear 30](../cases/clear-30.md) "your onboarding should be the best conversation" ([00:26:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1601s)).
2. **If onboarding is short and few people reach or accept the paywall → lengthen it with personalization, social proof and explanation screens; change many screens at once rather than one or two.** **[strong]** — [Coconote](../cases/coconote.md) 5 → ~13 screens: +16% trial conversion after 1–2-screen tweaks gave little ([00:29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s), [00:29:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1783s)); Phoenix Do added social proof, a video and a few questions: install→paywall 50–60% → 85–90%, "doubled our conversion" ([00:12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s)); [PushScroll](../cases/pushscroll.md) "7% to like 16" after rethinking the onboarding framework ([00:51:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3110s)).
3. **If a segment bails on long onboarding (typically under-25s) → branch: short path for them, long persuasion path for 25+.** **[weak]** — [Pep AI](../cases/pep-ai.md) ([00:28:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1738s)); PushScroll is testing a shorter version because its long one hurts paywall rate ([00:50:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3002s)).
4. **Explain what the app does *before* asking questions; make questions trivially easy (tap, multiple choice), one idea per screen.** **[strong]** — Sway: users emailed "what does this app even do?" until explainer screens came first ([00:23:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1435s)); avoid questions that are "hard for you to come up with an answer" ([00:24:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1495s)); Phoenix: ≤5 words per screen ([00:13:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=839s)); [Bloom](../cases/bloom.md): one dense screen split into ~5 story-style screens lifted sign-up ([00:25:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1521s)), and removing the "Get started" button made people read ([00:26:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1604s)).
5. **Personalize the pitch with the user's own answers or data; the strongest proof is their own data.** **[strong]** — Welltory shows "192 minutes" of the user's own stress from their watch ([00:21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1318s), [00:23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1391s)); Phoenix: questions that "make that experience feel personal" doubled conversion ([00:12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=776s)); Clear 30 echoes the chosen goal back with an outcome stat ([00:12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=759s)); Coconote: much of the 13-screen gain was "making the app feel more personalized" ([00:30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1814s)).
6. **If one permission unlocks personalization (health data, screen time) → ask for it first and frame it as curiosity, not setup.** **[weak]** — Welltory: the sync request is the first CTA and >90% accept ([00:27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1649s)), but "probably we haven't tested it enough" ([00:27:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1642s)). Welltory also asks for notifications right away, against best practice, because it worked best for them ([00:28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1709s)).
7. **If a permission looks scary (screen time, health) → put trust screens (who you are, social proof, privacy) right before it.** **[medium]** — PushScroll: the screen-time permission is "one big drop off", fixed with credibility and social-proof screens ([00:44:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2666s)); Welltory adds data-privacy and university-partner trust screens ([00:28:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1702s), [00:29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1796s)).
8. **Put the aha (or a taste of it) before the paywall only if it costs zero effort; otherwise keep the pre-paywall path thoughtless and deliver the aha right after.** **[strong]** — Sway tested photo uploads before the paywall: users left to fetch screenshots and never came back ([00:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1300s)); PushScroll lets users try push-up detection once ([00:49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2952s)); [GlamUp](../cases/glamup-sprout.md) runs a (fake) face scan and blurs results — the API only runs after payment ([00:26:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1601s)); [Raphael](../cases/raphael-kramer-meme-games.md) scan first for "sunk cost", then an exaggerated "you're really bloated" ([00:25:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1504s)).
9. **Add micro-commitments before the ask.** **[medium]** — PushScroll: pick a workout time, sign your name under "from this day onward I will…" ([00:49:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2986s)); Clear 30 asks for a rating before showing reviews (borrowed from Cal AI): 50 → ~1,000 ratings ([00:21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1296s)).
10. **If the app has no obvious daily trigger (health, language, self-improvement) → end on what the user loses today, not on benefits.** **[weak]** — Welltory: benefits ending "worked worse than this" ([00:35:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2127s)); for trigger apps (Uber, Spotify) benefits are fine ([00:37:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2273s)).
11. **Keep the ad promise and the onboarding promise identical; read onboarding drop-off by traffic source.** **[medium]** — [Social studio](../cases/social-app-studio.md) "the story starts at the ads" ([00:26:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1569s)); Clear 30's first screen converts 85–90% but moves with which ads run ([00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=615s)); Welltory's App Store video CTA "find out how you're really feeling in 2 minutes" tees up onboarding ([00:17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1028s)).
12. **For retention, build one daily trigger and wire notifications, streaks and widgets around it.** **[strong]** — [PrayScreen](../cases/prayscreen.md) (*measured*): pray-to-unlock four times a day gave 60% D30 ([00:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=335s)); [Flame](../cases/flame-couples.md) (*measured*): D30 0.8% (date planner) → 4.2% (AI wrapper) → ~30% → 50% after adding a daily couples' question with carrot (morning) and stick (evening) notifications ([00:12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=744s), [00:12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=773s)); widgets "discovered too late" ([00:16:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=997s)); [Cal AI](../cases/cal-ai.md) added streaks and group leaderboards to keep users through its 3-day trial (*heuristic*, [00:26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1563s), [00:28:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1694s)).
13. **If trial cancellations are high → give the canceller more time, not a survey.** **[weak]** — Coconote: +7-day trial extension in the cancel flow saves 27% of cancel-flow entrants ([00:32:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1959s)).
14. **Judge audience quality, not view or sign-up volume: cohort churn by creator/format/country before scaling it.** **[strong]** — Coconote "PDF to brainrot": 3,000 trials in days but ~$25k vs $360k if all converted ([00:05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s)); [Evo / SMB Market](../cases/dawson-gibbs-ugc.md): 40M views and $1.2M, then ~45% churn 2–3 months later from Gen Z "get rich quick" creators; fixed with 35–40-year-old creators and a credibility format ([00:17:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1033s), [00:17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1050s)); [Pingo](../cases/pingo.md): 200k+ Russian users in a month "who couldn't pay us anything" ([00:01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=114s)); [Marcus Burke](../cases/marcus-burke-meta-ads.md): 18–24s can't afford ~$70/yr plans; cheap young trials convert worst ([00:41:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2490s)).
15. **Measure retention as subscriber renewal and the power-user curve, not DAU.** **[weak]** — Josh, M&A buyer: watch when the top-20%-by-engagement curve flattens ([00:15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=931s)); year-1 resubscribe rate is "the real test" ([00:19:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1180s)).
16. **If you can get downloads but trialists churn or nobody renews → stop scaling acquisition and fix the product (or the audience).** **[strong]** — [Fitted](../cases/fitted.md) "I haven't found product market fit yet" after ~$150k revenue ([00:10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=623s)); Raphael "we never really cracked retention" ([00:13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=838s)); [Bloom](../cases/bloom.md) "people overlook product market fit" ([00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=46s)); [Sunflower](../cases/sunflower.md): the AI companion alone has "horrible" engagement and retention ([00:15:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=948s)).

## When the default flips
- **Shorter onboarding wins** for impatient, low-intent or young audiences (Pep AI under-25 path; PushScroll testing shorter) and when the long version lowers paywall-view rate more than it raises paywall conversion — the trade PushScroll describes for permission screens: "good for conversion but bad for paywall rate" ([00:44:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2679s)).
- **No onboarding paywall; get to the "moment of maximal intrigue" fast** when downstream placements already convert ([Social studio 00:16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=970s)) — see [paywall-pricing](paywall-pricing.md) rule 3.
- **Retention isn't the business if you can't get distribution or money.** Flame reached 50% D30 and was "almost out of money" — couldn't raise on retention alone ([00:14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=849s)). Fix distribution in parallel → [choose-channel](choose-channel.md).
- **High retention, ordinary conversion → monetize the non-payers with ads** instead of forcing the paywall: PrayScreen (~2.5% install→paid example) made ~5¢/user/day from ads and recouped spend in ~14–15 days ([00:09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=541s), [00:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=548s)).
- **Low retention can be acceptable** for ad-monetized or one-shot novelty products built to be filmed (Raphael's meme games: ~10¢/user, "super low retention" by design — [00:10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=618s)) — but those don't sell as subscriptions or at good multiples.

## Evidence against / where operators disagree
- **Long vs short.** Coconote and Phoenix gained by lengthening; PushScroll's long version hurts its paywall rate and Pep cuts it for under-25s. Long works when screens add personalization or proof, not when they add friction (permissions, typing, uploads).
- **Value before the ask?** Sway: aha *after* the paywall (uploads before it lost). PushScroll/GlamUp/Raphael: a taste before it. Coconote: the in-app paywall after an hour of use is "a big win". Both hold: effortless taste before, effortful value after.
- **Notifications early or late?** Welltory asks first ("I know it's not best practice") — single source, not A/B-quantified.
- **Login before or after paywall.** Coconote after (lift); Halo before ("could be either way").
- **Fear vs aspiration.** Welltory and Raphael close on loss/fear; Clear 30 and PushScroll sell the "dream outcome" ([00:45:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2708s)). Welltory's own caveat: depends on whether the app has a natural trigger.
- **Retention vs distribution first.** Bloom and Fitted say fix PMF; Flame had retention and nearly died for lack of distribution. Sequence matters: a working channel *and* a reason to return.

## Traps
- Building onboarding as a feature tour or analytics survey; "I used to get really fixated on getting like the copy perfect, but it didn't work until… the whole story arc" ([Welltory 00:25:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1559s)).
- Asking users to leave the app (fetch screenshots, find files) before the paywall ([Sway 00:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1300s)).
- Iterating one or two screens at a time and seeing only noise ([Coconote 00:28:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1738s)).
- Celebrating views and sign-ups from an audience that can't or won't pay (Coconote brainrot, Evo get-rich-quick, Pingo Russia).
- Shipping an AI companion/wrapper with no daily reason to return ([Sunflower 00:15:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=948s); Flame's AI version stayed at 4.2% D30).
- Not re-engaging people who skipped the paywall: Halo had no follow-up notifications yet ([00:46:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2783s)).
- Admitted by the best distributors in the corpus: "I've just never had a good product" ([Raphael 00:31:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1896s)).

## Gaps
- Almost no full retention curves (D1/D7/D30/M3) for paid subscribers; Flame's D30 is for a free social product, the TikTok/Instagram/X comparisons are recalled, not sourced.
- No A/B data on notification-permission timing, widget impact, streak impact, or win-back campaigns (email/push) for churned subscribers.
- Onboarding length tests are bundled (many changes at once), so which screen types drive the lift is unknown.
- Android-specific onboarding behaviour and non-English localization effects are barely covered.
- Cancel-reason data and refund drivers: not reported by anyone.
