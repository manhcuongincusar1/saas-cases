# Halo AI — UGC as arithmetic: one format, many creators, milestone pay, and a forecastable plateau

## Idea — how they found it
- After ~18 months of failed products with his partner and "almost nothing in the bank" (narrator) [00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=5s), Krishan noticed "the Gemini API came out recently" (last July) → possible app — [03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=206s). Built in 2–3 weeks in September, live mid/late October — [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=226s).
- No prior consumer/UGC experience; learned from Twitter/YouTube stories and "first principles" — [04:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=275s), [49:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2995s).
- Idea heuristic he gives: per modality (text/image/audio/video), take a newly capable model and point it at existing demand (Character AI/Replika → same concept with real-time voice) — [54:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3250s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Upload a photo, type a prompt, get an edit — [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=195s). Dashboard shows trending/featured templates (cartoons, celebrities, couples, hairstyles, room decor, tattoos), echoing what the viewer just saw on TikTok — [44:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2642s).
- Paywall: standard 3-page paywall, trial reminder, $8.99/week default; exit offer and abandoned-transaction paywalls — [40:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2458s). "Weekly's been really good"; yearly tested, dropped, being retested — [41:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2475s).
- **Two paywalls in sequence**: a skippable paywall in onboarding, then a gated (hard) paywall when the user hits send on the first prompt (after upload + prompt + sign-in) — [41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s), [43:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2621s). On screen: "Both have 16.5% conversion rate" [41:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2517s); claim: "combined have a higher conversion rate than just having one alone" [42:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2539s) — no lift figure given. Theory: undecided users explore, build trust, convert at the action moment — [42:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2562s).
- Self-assessment: image gen demand is partly "AI hype", entertainment = not lasting; future focus health, wealth, relationships for retention/LTV — [54:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3289s), [55:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3312s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Core equations**: revenue = views × conversion; conversion per format is constant ("0.5% or 0.3%" of viewers for hook+demo) — [06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=372s), [06:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=383s). Total views = creators × videos/creator × posts/video (platforms) × views/post — [09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=595s).
- Market facts he built on: a new zero-follower account's first video "can get 10 million views" (change since 2022) — [06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=403s), [06:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=410s); oversupply of UGC creators vs brand deals → brand pricing power — [06:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=417s); ~100M US people with Instagram + iPhone — [09:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=552s).
- **One format, no deviation** ("One format, 1.2 billion views") — [01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=80s): absurd AI-generated image hook ("Pranking my dad I cooked his koi fish (he Asian AF)") → screen recording of generating it with a huge "Halo AI" loading screen for ~2 s as the only CTA → text-message story with build-up and twist payoff — [21:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1302s), [23:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1384s). Good format = viral + repeatable + converts — [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1227s).
- Hit rate: ~5% of posts >100k views, ~1% >1M — [24:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1473s).
- **Sourcing**: job post in Reddit UGC subs and "hundreds of Facebook groups" → 30–50 applicants/day, 500–600+ total — [11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=707s), [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=774s), [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=730s). Screen: past UGC experience + US-based or US VPN — [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=869s); English first language and Gen Z help — [17:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1052s).
- **Paid trial**: 5 videos × 4 platforms = 20 posts, $20/video = $100 — [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=758s), [18:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1083s). Pass criteria: consistency, proactive communication, visible grasp of hook → build-up → payoff — [15:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=907s). No view threshold, because of the power law — [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1005s).
- **Uncapped posting**: some creators post 20+/day per platform; YouTube and Instagram "don't care", TikTok needs a ramp (3 → 5 → 10/day), Facebook untested — [18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1128s), [19:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1172s).
- **Winner replication**: an automation pushes any viral video to every creator in Discord to recreate the same day (he illustrates: one 10M video → many 2–3M copies) — [25:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1551s).
- **OpenClaw agent as coach**: all 1M+ view videos downloaded, transcribed via Gemini, stored locally; creators ask it for scripts; wired to TikTok trend data via Scrape Creators — [27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1640s), [29:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1778s). Sounds matter; hashtags don't for virality — [29:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1755s). Discord community (#wins, #ideas, #feedback, late-night voice chats) for compounding learning — [30:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1855s).
- **Pay experiment**: four structures (fixed, CPM with cap, mixed, base + milestones) — [32:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1933s). Offered a choice between CPM (told explicitly it paid slightly more) and base + milestones → 29 of 30 chose milestones — [34:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2044s), [34:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2051s); CPM also more operationally complex (chasing drifting view counts) — [34:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2074s). Standard: $20 base/video + non-stacking bonuses at 20k/100k/500k/1M ≈ $60/$200/$500/$800 — [36:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2165s), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2172s), [37:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2240s). Expected ~$1 CPM, actual lower: views between tiers are free and only one of four platform posts must hit (8M multi-platform views → one $800 bonus) — [36:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2180s), [38:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2304s).
- Next: paid (Meta) ads with existing winners plus new creative per awareness level, goal $1M/month — [52:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3152s), [53:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3190s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Model UGC as views × constant conversion; push creators × videos × platforms × views/post → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Base + non-stacking milestone bonuses beat CPM on creator preference and ops simplicity; cheap paid trial filters creators → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Go all-in only after measured unit economics (5–6:1) on early UGC → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Skippable onboarding paywall + hard gate at first core action; weekly pricing for an entertainment-style AI app → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- New-model + existing-demand idea heuristic; prefer health/wealth/relationships for retention → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Cut non-performing creators the day you notice (he delayed 2–3 weeks) — [56:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3380s) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Organic winners as the first paid creative → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported, ~5 months after launch; no retention data; entertainment use case he himself calls low-retention.
- Narrator says "one TikTok that paid him back 6X ROI" [00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=30s); Dillion says early-days ROAS/LTV:CAC "5:1 or 6:1 or something" — not attributed to a single video.
- Margin contradiction (0% vs ~50%); "$150k/month profit" is the host's arithmetic [52:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3129s).
- Two-paywall result: no numeric lift vs a single hard paywall; still ends in a hard gate before core use, so it is not "let users explore freely" — compare carefully with GlamUp/Nicole's +50% hard-paywall result ([glamup-sprout](glamup-sprout.md)).
- Superwall hosts (sells paywalls); SpyTok sponsor read. Platform posting limits are as of early 2026.
