# Stronger — build the screenshot-able status feature, then scale one format horizontally across accounts

## Idea — how they found it
- Started as a hardware business, pivoted; bootstrapped on government grant funding in Northern Ireland — [01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=99s), [05:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=332s). Every startup pitch told them "Don't go down the workout tracking app sector… you're just going to be a commodity" — same timestamp.
- Insight from a viral Twitter thread ~6–7 years earlier: a web app turning your bench/squat into performance metrics; nobody had done it on mobile — [05:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=317s).
- Differentiator = give strength a **status**: "I'm actually an elite lifter" instead of "I bench 100 kg for 5" — [06:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=369s); chosen because it "was also going to be like our main marketing asset" — [06:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=383s). They had ~12 months with nobody copying the strength score and wish they had "went in harder" — [48:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2909s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- "Strava, but for strength tracking": colored anatomy heat map vs people of the same body weight, "elite chest, novice legs", friends' activity — [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=270s). No designers on the team — [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s).
- **Designed as screenshots**: "how does this look as a screenshot because ultimately… those screenshots are what make us money" — [27:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1626s). Volume-lifted badges ("Empire State Building", "Boeing 747") exist partly as content — [27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1649s).
- Ranks were a **competitor's** evolution of their strength score ("League of Legends for the gym") that Stronger later adopted — [28:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1726s). They limit gamification: it resonates with 15–23-year-olds, "30 year olds are where the money is" — [29:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1760s), [29:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1772s).
- Category caps LTV: a tracker competes with "pen and paper"; a planner competes with a personal trainer and can charge more — [04:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=243s).
- **Paywall**: macro over micro — how often the paywall shows, which features it gates, message/feature fit; a "2% difference" after a week of testing microcopy is noise — [23:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1392s). Biggest win: **hard paywall, found by accident** — a toggle bug on New Year's Eve hard-paywalled everyone for a couple of hours, conversions spiked, few complaints — [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s), [24:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1475s). Now free functionality only after starting a trial; "overnight that was just our biggest boost probably like 25% for us" — [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1502s), [25:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1515s). Trade-off: kills K-factor (nobody shares a paid social app); they don't measure K-factor — [25:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1522s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Pre-launch viral video**: co-founder Jack, on a break at his garden-centre job, posted a Canva timeline of total volume lifted vs objects (Stonehenge slab, Easter Island head) — [31:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1873s), [31:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1901s). The feature was "still just Canva", coded 3–4 days earlier — [33:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2019s). ~15,000 followers in the first week with no app available — [32:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1939s); app shipped within ~1–2 weeks — [32:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1949s). Peaked #3 in Health & Fitness — [34:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2048s).
- **Comment→stitch loop**: viewers commented their lifts ("I bench pressed 220 pounds for five reps, what is that?"); Jack stitched each, typing it into the app on camera — [35:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2109s), [35:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2121s). TikTok page ~4.9M likes — [35:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2147s).
- **"Fade-in" format (Instagram)**: ~6 s, fades from black to one image — [06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=418s); average watch time "over double" the length — [07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=429s). 150–200M views "at least", a couple of videos at 40M — [07:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=446s), [07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=450s); "potentially even 300 million… two, three months ago we did 100 million view month… it is slowing down now" — [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=506s). Conversion per view "quite low" but a couple of hours/week to schedule with internal tooling — [07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=463s), [07:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=470s). Self-critique: viewers often don't realize it's a workout tracker; a more obvious app screenshot would convert better — [27:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1672s).
- **TikTok**: main account = 2–3-slide carousels, historically "a lot higher converting than Instagram", weaker in the last year as "20 workout apps" copy — [30:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1823s), [30:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1832s). Rough beats polished: leave the share/download UI in — [39:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2386s).
- **Horizontal scaling (mistake → fix)**: they posted winners on 1–2 accounts; a competitor ran their format on ~10 accounts and its downloads "ratchet up" — [09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=564s). Fix: micro-adjust each copy (AI rewrites a few hook words, light-mode background, legend) so it isn't flagged as spam — [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=644s), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=654s). Same sound as a "brand sound"; sounds matter less on Instagram than TikTok — [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=696s).
- **Finding formats**: content "either educates or entertains" — [12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=763s); entertainment (gym-bro humour) skews Gen Z, education reaches more profitable 25+ lifters — [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=968s). Look at other industries (e.g. a finance-tracking app), understand *why* it went viral before reskinning — [39:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2398s); only formats you can "do 10 iterations of" — [40:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2458s). Curates a per-app phone/FYP as the ICP ("would the average 20-year-old gym bro find this funny?") — [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=880s), [14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=898s). Treat each video as a funnel; "all three of us are engineers" — [42:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2531s).
- **Brand/ambassadors**: apps doing "a few million a month" vs "say 50k a month" differ by brand/lifestyle (Runna, Represent, Ladder) — [19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1146s); 3–4 ambassadors do most of the lifting, quality over spray-and-pray — [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1211s).
- **Agencies**: in-house wins (no ~20% margin); use agencies only to buy knowledge or to scale a proven format across e.g. 50 accounts — [43:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2626s), [44:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2694s). Peter and his brother make almost all content — [44:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2658s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Pick a feature that is both the product's status hook and its marketing asset; test it as a Canva mock on TikTok before it's finished → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Once a format wins, scale it horizontally (many accounts, micro-variations) before competitors do → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Test paywall at the macro level (hard vs freemium, frequency, gated feature), not microcopy; hard paywall costs K-factor → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Category sets the LTV ceiling (tracker vs planner); gamification depth sets the age skew → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Keep format-finding in-house; agencies only to scale a proven format → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported, rounded estimates ("I would guess", "never added it up"); the +25% has no stated metric or measurement window.
- Intro narration overstates: "replicated the same exact video 300 times" and "more than 300 million views, over a million app downloads" [00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=13s), [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=29s) are the narrator's, not Peter's; Peter says 150–200M "at least" and never gives a post count.
- The "zero-to-one content people deserve equity, not a retainer" and "never outsource the most crucial thing" points are the host's [47:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2843s), not Peter's.
- Early success came from TikTok organic when there were few competitors and no vibe coding (Peter's own caveat [01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=117s)). Video dated 2025-08; Peter is a Superwall user and Consumer Club member (host's community); Superwall sponsors.
