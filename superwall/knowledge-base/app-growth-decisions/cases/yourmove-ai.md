# YourMove AI — the corpus's only web-SEO case: PR → domain authority → long-tail blog, and why it is decaying

## Idea — how they found it
- A joke at a party: use GPT-3 (Davinci 2, before ChatGPT) to reply to dating-app messages. He was already building a Slackbot to "replace myself as a manager" — [00:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=281s), [00:05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=311s).
- The signal was **pull**: friends asked "wait, can I use this? Can I give it to people?" about a half-baked tool built in a day. Earlier projects had only ever felt like push — [00:05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=338s), [00:06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=362s).
- Started in 2022, about a year before the RizzGPT wave. He was early but "did not capture nearly as much of the market" — [00:03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=239s), [00:04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=262s).
- His take on the category: dating is a painkiller that "doesn't go away until it's solved", so even the *promise* of better outcomes monetizes — [00:27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1640s). Look for problems that technology itself recently created (dating apps made everyone their own marketer) — [00:30:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1836s). Match capital to the problem size: VC-backed competitors "didn't go anywhere" — [00:31:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1891s), [00:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1919s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- V1 was a text box: paste a Hinge message, get three flirty replies. It grew into profile writing, profile review and (from ~2024) AI photos. Tried ~12 products; these are the ones that stuck — [00:04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=255s), [00:03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=202s).
- **Soft paywall** for the core product. **Hard paywall** only for AI photos, because compute is expensive — [00:06:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=392s), [00:06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=415s).
- Did not charge for ~6 months ("build users, monetize later"); a mentor pushed him to start — [00:42:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2579s).
- **Web-first** on purpose: one site acting as "a collection of different products we tested", so each blog post or Google ad can route to a different product — [00:16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=963s). Failed bets: a keyboard app ("performed much worse than our core flow") and dating coaching (too high-maintenance) — [00:45:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2722s), [00:16:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=978s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
1. **Didn't work:** flyers in Washington Square Park — [00:12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=729s). **Did work, early:** posts in small dating-optimization subreddits (r/Swipehelper), which still send some SEO traffic — [00:12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=779s), [00:13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=795s).
2. **PR:** Fox, CNBC, Wired, Washington Post, WSJ, plus a Newsweek op-ed, all because AI was novel. Fox Business sent fewer views than "a single decent Reddit post" — [00:08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=506s), [00:13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=822s).
3. **Domain authority "at the door"** from the media backlinks; "we never paid for backlinks or hunted for backlinks" — [00:18:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1099s), [00:18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1118s).
4. **SEO blog:** low-competition, relatively high-volume keywords (pickup lines, messages, best dating apps). In-post pop-up ads push to the matching product. "Nearly half of our traffic" — [00:14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=874s), [00:14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=884s), [00:14:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=893s). It worked partly because the space was unsaturated: one serious SEO competitor, the rest content farms — [00:19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1167s).
5. **Google Ads:** profitable for some products and not at all for others — [00:15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=941s).
6. **Team:** 10–12 Upwork contractors working a few hours a week (SEO, ads, email, design, Webflow, $5/hr support), paid from his salary. He hires people who do the work *and* coach him — [00:43:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2618s), [00:44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2647s).

**Search ceiling (his core lesson).** Google Ads and SEO capture latent demand and are capped by search volume ("area under the curve"). Meta and TikTok create induced demand: more upside, but harder to capture — [00:19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1189s), [00:20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1229s), [00:20:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1243s). Ads are auctions: you must monetize about as well as everyone bidding for that user — [00:22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1342s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Web SEO works when you already have authority and the keyword space is unsaturated; otherwise it is a 3–6-month bet → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Search is capped by existing demand; to go beyond six figures you need a demand-creating channel (Meta/TikTok) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- PR is worth it for backlinks, not users → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- A web funnel lets one domain test many products and route keywords to the best-monetizing one → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Soft paywall for the core product; hard paywall where marginal cost is high (image generation) → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- "Pull" from users of a one-day prototype is the go signal; monetize early (he lost 6 months) → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Sold the business, but the price was not disclosed → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)

## Caveats
- Self-described as "I got lucky": early to AI (2022), media novelty, and an empty SERP. Hard to reproduce in 2026.
- He says his SEO "declined pretty meaningfully" because AI answers make listicle posts redundant — [00:15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=909s). Treat this tactic as **decaying** (as of 2026-01).
- No exit price disclosed. After the sale he joined a16z speedrun with a new company — [00:50:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3024s).
- Web product, not a native app, so App Store / ASO mechanics do not apply.
