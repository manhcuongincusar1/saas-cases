# Dawson Gibbs / Evo — match the creator to the buyer, not to the views (newest UGC evidence, Oct 2026)

## Idea — how they found it
- Dawson posted daily for ~6 years, ~1,500 videos, ~2,000 followers; then went viral within weeks, gained hundreds of thousands of followers in ~4 months, and Cal AI signed him as roughly its first influencer — [00:03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=192s).
- Referred ~100 fitness creators to Cal AI for a per-creator referral fee, then founded Evo off a shout-out from a Cal AI owner — [00:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=284s), [00:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=182s).
- His market read: most founders are vibe-coding viral Gen Z apps; travel and settlement-claim apps for 30–40-year-olds are what is "popping off" now — [00:21:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1310s), [00:22:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1365s), [00:22:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1379s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
Evo sells UGC campaigns; the product choice that matters is **which creator persona carries the message**, set by purchase type:
- **Considered purchase → credible expert.** Jetpack (flights cost hundreds): ex-flight-attendant creator, "tip #1 book Tue/Wed/Sat… tip #2 use Jetpack" listicle. Viewers follow the page and buy weeks later — [00:05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=352s), [00:09:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=567s), [00:11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s).
- **Impulse purchase → relatable peer** ("I am you"): dupe.com ($40 savings), Bible BFF — [00:09:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=588s), [00:06:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=389s).
- **In between → borrowed credibility**: "my mom's an interior designer", "my son showed me", or green-screening the founder — [00:40:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2437s), [00:42:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2548s).
- **Non-visual app → sell the outcome, not the UI.** Jetpack's screens (search flights, send an email) are boring on camera, so videos point to a benefit domain (flightcheatcode.com → redirects to the app). Bonus: the domain isolates Evo's organic attribution from the client's paid ads and other agencies — [00:12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=730s), [00:12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=747s), [00:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=842s). Not needed for already-viral apps (Cal AI: just flash the app) — [00:13:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=829s).
- **Viral product → put the product in the scene** (Bible BFF in the car, reacting to the Gen Z narration) — [00:24:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1479s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Sequence:** first ~2 weeks optimise for views with broad creator types, hooks and formats (long-text, reactions); then rebuild the winning script as a talking head with a credible source for conversion in the next 2 weeks — [00:49:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2956s), [00:50:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=3002s), [00:50:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=3047s).
- **Format conversion ladder (heuristic):** 5-second long-text loops and silent face reactions get views but "conversions are very bad"; a person talking to camera for ≥3–5 s "always" converts better; green-screen reaction is the low-effort middle — [00:38:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2338s), [00:39:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2362s). Green-screen stitch of a viral video = "most viral" format of the last couple of months (one example 214k views) — [00:19:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1194s).
- **Approval rules:** every video approved before posting; creator's hands must be doing something (snack, squeeze ball, ponytail); multiple faces help hold the first 5 s, though he admits one person probably converts the same — [00:28:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1732s), [00:10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=642s), [00:26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1608s), [00:27:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1638s). Spell the URL CTA slowly on screen for ~5 s — [00:41:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2509s).
- **Creator pages as mini-experts (trust products):** CTAs "share with 3 friends" / "comment WORD" → ManyChat DM — [00:31:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1879s). Stories = bottom funnel: ~10k story viewers on a 200k-follower page, $1,000 brand giveaway — [00:32:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1968s), [00:32:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1977s). Creator videos at ~20k views get collab-posted to the brand page — [00:36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2163s).
- **Platform by age:** 30–40-year-old audiences → Facebook + Instagram (cross-post); TikTok/Snapchat → 18–25 — [00:23:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1388s). Mom/older-women creators (30+) "convert the highest" for family/higher-intent products — [00:46:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2811s), [00:47:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2867s).
- **Repost before you pay:** a video at 10–20k views with ≥3% engagement is reposted as-is or re-shot with a new text hook; if the reposts hold ≥10k views over 4–5 days, one usually reaches 500k–1M. Rationale: good videos that stall "just haven't hit the market at the right time" — [00:43:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2597s), [00:43:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2631s), [00:44:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2648s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Choose creator persona by purchase type: credible expert for considered buys, relatable peer for impulse buys, borrowed credibility in between → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Judge creator cohorts by churn/paid retention, not views or signups — "the right 40M views beats 200M wrong views" [00:48:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2923s) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Apps for 30–40-year-olds are under-served and richer, but need same-age creators on Facebook/Instagram → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- If the UI is not camera-worthy, sell the outcome and use a benefit domain (also gives clean organic attribution) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Repost promising videos before putting ad money behind them; compare SideShift's ≥5% ER bar for paid ([sideshift](sideshift.md)) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Views-first for 2 weeks, then convert the winning idea into talking-head formats → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)

## Caveats
- **Conflict of interest:** Dawson sells Evo's agency services; every example is a client pitch. Client revenue/churn (SMB Market) is reported via the agency, not by the client. Host runs Consumer Club; episode carries Superwall, Rork and SpyTok promotions.
- Intro narration says "nearly 2,000 videos" [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=0s); Dawson himself says ~1,500.
- Platform risk: a creator's earlier accounts were banned, and an Evo page that hit ~1M followers got banned — [00:15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=932s), [00:30:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1851s).
- Most rules are heuristics (he admits multiple faces may not matter); formats decay — he drops formats that stall after a couple of weeks [00:27:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1650s). Dated Oct 2026.
