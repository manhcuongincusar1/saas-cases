# How should I design the paywall and price the subscription?

> **Conflict of interest — read first.** Superwall (sells paywall software) produces this show; host Joseph Choi now works there ([00:55:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3344s)). The two most quoted paywall "experts" sell paywall services: Jonathan Parra (ex-Superwall lead designer, now designs paywalls for paying clients through an agency — [00:51:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3114s), "the first test that you'll run with us" [00:54:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3263s)) and Vah Bagdasarian (paywall agency, all tests "built on Superwall" — [00:40:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2440s)). Their claims are tagged *agency*. Ideas also propagate between guests (Pingo copied its winning paywall from "a Superwall podcast" — [00:46:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2779s)), so agreement is not always independence. Weight operator-measured results above agency heuristics.

## Inputs to collect first
- Funnel numbers, split by platform (iOS/Android) and country (US vs rest): install→paywall view, trial start, trial→paid, install→paid, refund/cancel rate.
- Traffic mix: organic/UGC/ASO vs paid. Paid traffic needs monetization tuned *before* scale; organic can lag ([Steve Young 00:30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1848s)).
- Monthly install volume (dropping the trial and micro-copy tests both need volume).
- Audience age / spending power (under-25 vs 25+), and price sensitivity of the problem.
- Usage shape: one-off/short need (dating, "who unfollowed me"), daily habit, multi-week transformation, social/multiplayer.
- Marginal cost per user (text LLM vs image/video generation).
- Goal: cash flow, exit multiple (acquirers want sticky subscriptions), or market share / K-factor.
- Current setup: placements, plans shown, default plan, trial length, abandon/exit offers.

## Default answer
Put a paywall at the end of onboarding that gates the core action (hard, or "trial required"), offer a free trial on the **annual plan selected by default**, show at most two plans (hide the rest behind "view all plans"), and add a **trial-timeline screen** ("today you unlock · day N we remind you · day N+2 you're charged · cancel anytime"). On transaction abandonment show a second offer (discounted *annual* or a longer trial). Then add in-app placements (session start, after the core action, usage limit, trial-cancel) before you touch copy. Price around the category's going rate and then **test upward** on a slice of traffic — most price increases in the corpus held or raised conversion. Test macro changes (gate, placement, trial, plan mix, multi-step) before micro copy.

## Decision rules
1. **If the product solves a specific, pressing problem and traffic is high-intent (niche search, paid, health/fitness) → gate the core action (hard paywall or trial-required).** **[strong]** — [Steve Young](../cases/green-noise-steve-young.md) trial start 13% hard vs 6% with an X, trial→paid unchanged ([00:20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1213s)); [Stronger](../cases/stronger.md) ≈+25% ([00:25:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1515s)); [Nicole](../cases/glamup-sprout.md) +50% vs letting users explore first ([00:44:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2682s)).
2. **If growth depends on sharing, network effects, or market share (social app, referral loop, data play) → keep the core free and monetize later; a hard paywall taxes K-factor.** **[medium]** — [Stronger](../cases/stronger.md) admits the trade but never measured K ([00:25:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1522s)); [Fitted](../cases/fitted.md) "hamstringing user growth because my app is hard paywalled" (plan, not result — [00:18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1089s)); [Nomad Table](../cases/nomad-table.md) social ARPU is low by design ([00:06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=370s)).
3. **If many users close the onboarding paywall but you already convert well in-app → test a skippable onboarding paywall plus a hard gate at the first valuable action, or no onboarding paywall at all.** **[medium, app-dependent]** — [Halo](../cases/halo-ai.md) two paywalls beat one ([00:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s)); [Social studio](../cases/social-app-studio.md) removing the onboarding paywall on Celebs was "a big win" because 10 downstream placements were tuned ([00:17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s)). Directly contradicted by Nicole (rule 1). If you can't name a better moment than the end of onboarding, keep the onboarding paywall ([00:23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1391s)).
4. **Default to a free trial. Drop it only with high volume, trial start well above 15%, trial→paid ≥30–50%, and a pressing problem.** **[medium]** — Steve "typically seen better results with trials" ([00:29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1761s)); Jack Frics: adding a trial took his app from $500–1k to $2–4k/month ([00:16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=992s)); Vah (*agency*) removed Claim's trial at 34% trial start / 50–55% trial→paid ([00:15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=959s), [00:17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1047s)).
5. **Fight trial anxiety explicitly: timeline screen, reminder promise, repeat "free"/"cancel anytime".** **[strong]** — [Sunflower](../cases/sunflower.md) trial reminders +48% revenue ([00:46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2799s)); [Clear 30](../cases/clear-30.md) day-by-day program screen 18–20% → 30% ([00:25:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1559s)); [Pingo](../cases/pingo.md) timeline beat value-prop and testimonial variants ([00:48:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2883s)); Vah mentions "free" 5–7 times across 3 screens ([00:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=746s)).
6. **Select the annual plan by default; keep weekly or monthly as the secondary option (or hidden behind "view all plans", with no trial).** **[strong]** — [Sway](../cases/sway-ai.md) weekly default = "a lot slower growth" ([00:19:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1191s)); [Sunflower](../cases/sunflower.md) annual default → "a lot more annual revenue" ([00:45:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2705s)); [Pingo](../cases/pingo.md) no trial on monthly, hidden ([00:46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2799s)); [Welltory](../cases/welltory.md) mostly sells annual only ([00:39:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2370s)).
7. **If the need is short-lived (dating, ex-stalking, a job hunt) or you sell an AI wrapper with churn-and-burn usage → weekly (or weekly+monthly) can beat annual; you trade exit multiple for cash.** **[medium]** — Josh, M&A buyer ([00:31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s), [00:33:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1996s)); [Halo](../cases/halo-ai.md) $8.99/week, weekly winning so far ([00:41:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2475s)); [Sprout](../cases/glamup-sprout.md) skipped annual for a job app ([00:40:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2405s)).
8. **Show two plans, ordered by length; hide the third.** **[medium]** — Parra (*agency*) ([00:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=662s)); [Pingo](../cases/pingo.md) ([00:46:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2801s)).
9. **Price: start from competitors + your cost per user + the price that keeps paid UA ROAS-positive; then test upward on 10–20% of traffic.** **[strong]** — Vah method ([00:37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2252s), [00:37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2260s)); Josh: test toward $20/month on a 10–20% slice ([00:33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2030s)); Nicole +20% price → conversion up ([00:22:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1372s)); [Cardstock](../cases/cardstock.md) $5 lifetime → $5/mo · $50/yr · $99 lifetime "exploded" ([00:12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=732s)); [Massive](../cases/massive.md) "doubled our revenue from pricing changes" ([00:30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1848s)).
10. **If your users are genuinely price-sensitive (addiction recovery, under-25s) → test *down* as well as up.** **[weak]** — [Sunflower](../cases/sunflower.md) made more money by lowering price ([00:46:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2761s)); [Pep AI](../cases/pep-ai.md) wants cheaper than $40/yr ([00:23:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1403s)).
11. **On transaction abandonment, show one more offer: reframe price (per-week) → discount the annual only → or a longer trial.** **[strong]** — Vah: "discount only an annual product… we tested dozens of times" ([00:34:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2060s)); Parra: 25–33% off, save deep cuts for Black Friday ([00:19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1192s)); Nicole 50% on close ([00:25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1500s)); [PushScroll](../cases/pushscroll.md) ~80% ([00:55:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3348s)); Pingo 7-day trial after a refused 3-day converts higher ([00:51:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3078s)).
12. **Build every placement before optimizing any: onboarding, session start (≤1/day), after the core action or usage limit, transaction abandon, trial cancel.** **[strong]** — Vah ([00:41:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2477s)); [Social studio](../cases/social-app-studio.md) converts in "10 different places" ([00:17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s)); [Coconote](../cases/coconote.md) paywall after a full recorded lecture ([00:31:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1869s)); [Welltory](../cases/welltory.md) >60% of conversions after day one ([00:40:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2434s)); [PrayScreen](../cases/prayscreen.md) to founders who "show one paywall": "just show another one" ([00:09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=569s)).
13. **Test macro before micro: gate/placement/frequency/trial/plan mix/multi-step/design first; button copy last.** **[strong]** — [Stronger](../cases/stronger.md): a 2% weekly difference "is just standard deviation" ([00:23:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1392s)); Parra: design and packaging are the biggest lever, price tests later ([00:37:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2225s)); Vah quick wins = multi-step, abandon, session start ([00:41:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2515s)).
14. **Never ship a famous paywall 1:1; the same design can win in one app and flop in a sibling app.** **[strong]** — Parra ([00:31:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1901s)); Vah ([00:04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=271s)); [PushScroll](../cases/pushscroll.md): ~70% of its users are ~18 or younger, which caps its rates ([00:50:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3045s)).
15. **If heavy users keep coming back for more output → add consumables or a higher tier; whales have no ceiling.** **[weak]** — [Sway](../cases/sway-ai.md) photo packs ≈11% of revenue ([00:18:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1137s)); Nicole usage-triggered upgrade paywall ([00:25:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1531s)).

## When the default flips
- **No trial** works when the problem is pressing and intent is high: Claim dropped its trial at 34%/50–55% (Vah, [00:16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1015s)); Welltory runs a closable, no-trial, annual-only paywall at ~$18M ARR ([00:40:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2413s)); Cardstock's closable no-trial paywall sold "sight unseen" ([00:30:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1830s)). Vah's no-trial variant: a 3-step paywall that hides prices until "Start my journey" (Quitter, no number — [00:29:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1758s)).
- **Soft/closable** instead of hard: organic and word-of-mouth traffic, where hard paywalls draw 1-star reviews. Steve's idea: hard paywall only for paid traffic, soft for App Store / friend referrals (secondhand, no number — [00:38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2336s)); 1-star reviews admitted at [00:20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1250s).
- **Weekly-first**: short-duration needs, AI wrappers, young audiences (rule 7). Parra: weekly users "will pay more… than if they would have just paid for the annual plan" because they forget to cancel ([00:12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=777s), [00:13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=800s)).
- **Trial toggle with a cheaper no-trial price** for low-intent/young audiences (Riz: $9.99/wk with trial vs $6.99 without — [00:23:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1390s)); ~10% pick no-trial ([00:35:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2100s)). High-intent apps (Cal AI) toggle with no discount ([00:25:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1527s)).
- **Ad-supported free tier instead of a harder paywall** when retention is high but install→paid is ordinary: [PrayScreen](../cases/prayscreen.md) (~2.5% install→paid as his example, 60% D30) earned ~5¢/user/day from ads on free users and recouped ad spend in ~14–15 days ([00:08:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=514s), [00:09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=541s), [00:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=548s)).
- **Native look over custom design**: a plain SwiftUI-style paywall beat custom designs "by quite a long shot", twice — when the rest of the app is native ([00:47:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2852s)).

The 111% is a whole-paywall change, not a button test (some summaries say "Continue button beat descriptive by 111%" — that overstates it).
- **Jack Frics** (*measured*, 2025-12) — trial + real paywall: $500–1k → $2–4k/month — [00:16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1006s) (endorses Superwall on Superwall's channel)
- **Kyle / Cardstock** (*measured, no number*, 2025-11) — lifetime $5 → subscription; most subs from the first closable paywall — [00:12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=732s) · [case](../cases/cardstock.md)

## Evidence against / where operators disagree
- **Gate immediately vs let them poke around.** Nicole: +50% for a hard paywall right after onboarding, even though she preferred the explore-first UX ([00:44:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2682s)). Halo: explore first, gate at the action, beats one hard paywall. Social studio: no onboarding paywall at all won — after the same test had failed before ([00:16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1017s)). All three are measured; the difference is how good your downstream placements are.
- **"A hard paywall hides a bad product."** Phoenix Do (*heuristic*, 2025-10) — [00:14:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=857s). Steve's counter: a hard paywall is the fastest product-market-fit test ([00:29:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1749s)).
- **Annual vs weekly.** Sway, Sunflower, Pingo, Welltory push annual; Halo and GlamUp run weekly-only or weekly-first. Josh: weekly can be a good cash business but it is "not as good of a long-term strategy" ([00:31:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1899s), [00:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1919s)).
- **Micro-copy.** Parra: "no commitment, cancel anytime", a chevron on the button and "Continue" each bump conversion ([00:07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=472s), [00:15:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=936s)). Stronger: such changes are noise at real volumes. Both are true: copy wins exist but are small and slow to prove.
- **Price direction.** Most operators raised prices and kept conversion; Sunflower lowered price and earned more. Audience wealth decides.
- **Login before or after paywall.** Coconote: after = significant lift. Halo: before, "could be either way" ([00:44:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2642s)).
- **Discount depth.** Parra caps one-time offers at 25–33% to protect the brand; Nicole (50%), PushScroll (~80%) and Vah (80% with trial / 90% without on abandonment — [00:34:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2087s)) go far deeper. No one compared depths head-to-head.

## Traps
- Copying a friend's paywall: "it fails like happens like many many times every week" ([Vah 00:04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=271s)).
- Shipping a soft paywall that gives people "an excuse to not pay, they won't" ([Stronger 00:24:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1458s)).
- Under-monetizing a niche app: a $2–3 one-time fee left most of Steve's $8–10k/month on the table ([00:18:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1093s)).
- Starting price tests before design tests: cohorts get "a pain in the ass to manage" ([Parra 00:37:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2225s)).
- Over-discounting: users learn to wait, and it "cheapens the product" ([00:20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1235s)).
- Trusting the trial to sell a weak product: ~half of trialists cancel; a lot of monthly buyers means onboarding didn't sell the product ([Parra 00:17:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1044s)).
- Using the paywall to fix a retention problem. A paywall can "trick" people into year one, but renewal shows the truth ([Josh 00:19:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1173s)) → [onboarding-retention](onboarding-retention.md).

## Gaps
- No head-to-head data on price elasticity curves, Android vs iOS price levels, or discount depth (25% vs 50% vs 80%).
- Web checkout (Stripe) margins and conversion vs in-app purchase: mentioned (Pingo, Sprout), never quantified.
- Lifetime plans, family plans, two-tier pricing: mentioned without results (Parra [00:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2972s)).
- Refund rates and chargebacks after hard paywalls: only Vah's guess of "~10% more transaction abandonment" when renaming plans "free" ([00:39:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2392s)).
- Almost all evidence is iOS and US-centric, self-reported, and from apps that chose to appear on a paywall vendor's show (survivorship).
