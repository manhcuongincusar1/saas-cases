# Ad testing tracker

**Use when** you are about to spend on paid acquisition, or you are already spending and can't say which creative is carrying it.
**Decision card:** [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md). If this sheet and the card disagree, the card wins. Where the creatives come from: [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md) · [ugc-creator-brief](ugc-creator-brief.md).

> **Conflicts of interest.** The engagement thresholds and the 20/80 split come from SideShift, which is building a paid-ads product. Marcus Burke is a paid consultant. Ad costs decay fast, so every source below carries its video date.

## Step 1 — Creative checklist (each creative)

- [ ] **It opens like an organic video, then moves to the download.**
  - Bloom starts with an organic-style video to "capture users' attention", then transitions to the download — [00:15:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=948s).
  - A small "download Bloom" line comes late, "so that most people will think this is organic" (2025-07) — [00:16:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=982s).
- [ ] **The viewer knows an App Store page is coming.** Mention the app in the copy or show an app demo. Otherwise "your install rate tends to suffer" (Marcus, 2026-06) — [00:08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=502s), [00:08:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=515s).
- [ ] **The app is visible and the video is captioned.**
  - Alex: most users watch without sound, so captions are essential; also use a green screen with the app — [00:22:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1323s).
  - Marcus says D2C-style talking-head pain→solution "isn't the best format for apps" — [00:07:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=477s).
  - Others win with a talking person over a demo. See the card's "Talking-head" disagreement.
- [ ] **The copy uses the audience's own words.** Text blocks "rooted in a lot of Reddit research" make viewers "feel seen", and an LLM makes the research easy (Marcus) — [00:12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=738s).
- [ ] **The words pick the audience.**
  - Massive (organic skits, 2025-08): "stop using LinkedIn" converted. "Stop using Indeed" got "almost no conversion" because it reached a blue-collar audience — [00:09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=556s).
  - Marcus: Meta targeting is broad, "it's always the algorithm doing it", so the creative does the targeting — [00:13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=823s).

## Step 2 — Test round (Yev, 2025-06)

- **TikTok:** 20 creatives at $20 each, which is TikTok's minimum. You can turn each one off after it spends $5. You have results "within hours" — [00:36:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2164s), [00:36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2172s).
- **Meta:** 30–40 creatives at "as little as a dollar" each — [00:36:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2212s), [00:37:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2236s).
- **Ignore attribution in this phase.** You are hunting variation — [00:36:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2216s).
- **Pick the outlier: "better than the rest", not necessarily great.** The next phase makes variations of it, such as new headlines on the same video or new videos under the same headline — [00:36:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2180s).

**Expect most to fail.**
- Alex (Meta, raw creator UGC, 2026-04): "one video out of 85 would get all the ad spend" — [00:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1241s).
- He also says 97% didn't work "no matter how good they looked" — [00:12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=759s).
- That cost him ~$800k wasted out of $1.5–2M+ spent over ~2.5 years — [00:20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1254s).
- With premium creator ads at $1,000–1,500 each, Casper expects "maybe 20–30%" to be profitable — [00:26:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1583s). That is a different denominator.

## Step 3 — Read the results

1. **Split by country and device; never blend.**
   - Yev estimates purchase value per country and device ("$30 or like $70"; "iOS is much better") — [00:18:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1130s).
   - A blended "$300 cost per purchase" can hide a slice that is "matching our numbers exactly" — [00:19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1163s).
2. **Act on direction, not statistical significance.** Put a few dollars behind each creative. If one gets more clicks, keep the budget going: "it either averages out and it was a fluke or you're on to something" — [00:12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=739s), [00:12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=763s). Card confidence: **[weak]**.
3. **Optimize toward revenue, not cheap trials.** 18–24-year-olds can't spend "like 70 bucks a year", and young trials convert worse (Marcus) — [00:41:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2490s).
4. **Optional: tiered CPA goals.** Yev runs $50, $100 and $200 per purchase. The loosest goal exists to push data into the onboarding flow — [00:16:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=993s).

## Step 4 — Scale or stop

- **Start:** ~$100 a day for the first 14 days. Allocate more only on positive ROAS (Nick, 2026-08) — [00:31:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1897s), [00:31:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1910s).
- **Stop-loss:** if you put in $1 and get 75¢ back, stop, or "you're just going to accelerate the bleeding" — [00:30:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1823s).
- **Scaling decays returns.** Yannis: a 2× ROAS winner scaled to 10× the spend fell to "like 20% net margin" (*heuristic*) — [00:18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1100s). Scale only while marginal ROAS stays positive.
- **Budget split once winners exist:** SideShift says big spenders put ~20% into creative/organic and ~80% into performance (*heuristic*) — [00:26:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1568s).

> **Stop rule, written before launch:** we stop a creative when ROAS < ____ for ____ consecutive days.
