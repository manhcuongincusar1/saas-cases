# Payout — the creator side of the table: reuse a sponsor's proven format for your own app

## Idea — how they found it
- Connor cold-DM'd Casper on Twitter; a mutual friend vouched; Casper says yes when it is "low input" for him — [04:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=285s), [05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=327s).
- **Idea came from a rejected sponsorship**: a class-action app (a Payout competitor) offered ~$500/video, too low for his audience; he declined and brought the category to Connor, who said it was "the easiest one to build". First idea worked — [06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=365s), [06:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=383s).
- Category rule he draws: the easiest sell is "spend X, get more than X back" — $40/year, probably "at least 200" back if you actually apply — [33:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2016s). Niche > broad: "the broader the audience, the harder it is to come up with an idea" — [35:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2138s). He built for the consistent demand in his audience: "saving money" videos get either 100k or 10M views, "side hustles always get at least 300,000" — [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2156s), [35:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2159s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Select companies you've used → app shows which settlements you can claim → simple form (from the video script) — [13:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=828s). Built in React Native by Connor — [25:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1544s). No paywall or pricing-test detail in this episode beyond $40/year.

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Format 1 — reused brand-deal hook**: a print-on-demand brand (Gelato) paid him for ~5 experimental videos; the best did "right about a million views", all organic — [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=765s), [13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=783s). A week later he reused the same hook and structure for Payout ("Watch me make up to $900 in just 30 seconds...") — that video got ~500k views and "like 20,000 comments" and "probably close to 5,000 or more in subscription revenue" — [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=742s), [13:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=824s). Then repeat: swap the dollar figure and background, keep idea/structure/flow — [15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=957s).
- **Format 2 — skit → how-to**: "Hey Temu, is it true I can make $1,000 from your lawsuit?" → ~30-40% skit, rest plain how-to; the Temu video "probably generated close to $10,000" — [16:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1004s), [16:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1011s). "These are the only two formats that make significant money" — [16:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=988s).
- **What flopped**: explaining *why* a company is sued → views, not sign-ups; "talk about the bag" — [22:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1344s), [22:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1373s).
- **CTA craft**: never say the app name ("go to this app"); naming it "triggers ad ad ad... scroll" — [20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1235s), [20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1254s). "Comment CLAIM" + ManyChat DM link where available; on TikTok and YouTube Shorts no ManyChat CTA — a shorter cut plus a soft pinned comment naming Payout — [19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1146s), [19:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1157s). "CTAs reduce retention" — [20:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1202s). Never "link in bio".
- **ManyChat funnel (one video)**: host guesses 25,000 commenters, Casper settles on ~13,000 → ~50% click the DM link → ~6,500 clicks; download→paid ~6.7-7% — [14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=897s), [15:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=916s), [15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=924s), [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=930s).
- **Retention micro-craft**: hands move a little ("eyes are attracted to movement"), emojis pop, one path of logic a 12-year-old can follow — [18:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1081s), [18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1098s), [18:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1117s).
- **Coordinated push**: 5 creators posting the same day → $9,700 revenue that day and #70-80 in Finance; aim for top 20 for organic App Store traffic — [46:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2774s), [47:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2840s). Creator friends charge ~70% off or post free; still lost money on a $1,500 video from someone who normally charges $6,000+ — [48:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2918s), [48:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2926s).
- **Paid**: Connor runs organic winners as Meta ads; one was profitable "for quite some time... few weeks" — [25:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1504s), [25:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1518s). Creator-side economics: bigger creators rarely go under $1,000-1,500/video incl. only a few months of usage rights; "maybe 20-30% of ads will be profitable" — [26:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1583s), [26:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1598s).
- **Long tail**: a 17M-view video had 2.9M in week one, 7.5M in month one (under half its lifetime), still ~30-50 views/hour 500+ days later — [43:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2637s), [44:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2650s), [44:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2673s). "If I stop posting today... a few hundred downloads every single day" — [43:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2583s).

### Creator-partnership playbook (for developers pitching creators)
- Read sponsored posts by like/view ratio: 2M views / 2k likes = boosted; 2M / 50k = organic — [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=624s). Find paid posts via "#partner" in descriptions; repeat bookings are weak evidence (brands pre-buy 5-video packages) — [36:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2195s), [36:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2212s).
- Pitch: "we built this app for you based on this brand deal that went well... I want to give you 50%... Try one video — I'll give you 100% of the revenue" — [11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=694s), [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1761s). His creator friends get ~5 such emails a day — [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=647s).
- Pick creators who optimize for the algorithm (a winner within 3-4 videos) or with a loyal audience; he finally prefers loyal-audience creators launching "something of their own" — [30:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1833s), [30:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1844s).
- Equity is shifting to distribution: 50% on app one; only 15% to the developer on his third app — "I could pay someone 10, 15 thousand to build it" — [50:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=3058s), [51:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=3074s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Underpaid/rejected sponsorship offers are pre-validated app categories; performing brand deals are pre-validated formats → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- "Spend $X, get more back" products sell easily via creators; build for a creator's consistent demand, not lottery topics → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Give a creator real equity and a ready-built app; test one video at no risk to them → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Never name the app; talk about the outcome ("the bag"); comment-keyword DMs instead of link-in-bio → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Coordinate creators to post the same day to chart and pick up App Store browse traffic → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md)
- Re-run organic winners as Meta ads; only ~20-30% of paid creator ads profit → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Short-form compounds (long tail, rising download floor) — judge a format over months, not days → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
