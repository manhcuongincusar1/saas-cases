# PushScroll — validate with a video of an app that does not exist, then win on one repeatable hook

## Idea — how they found it
- Alejandro was posting daily idea videos; one pitched an app that blocks social media until you do 20 push-ups, ending "Should I build this? Let me know in the comments" — it blew up before any code existed — [00:02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=138s), [00:02:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=157s).
- Found Mario on YC co-founder matching; Mario already had a screen-time app built product-first that he could not market ("hit a wall") — [00:02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=163s), [00:03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=193s).
- Their rule: pretend the product exists (or use a similar demo), post several videos, build only if one goes viral — [00:08:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=482s), [00:08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=495s), [00:08:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=505s). The idea was not new (they cite Clear Space) but was novel to their European audience — [00:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=292s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- MVP = Mario's existing blocker + push-up detection: two screens (pick apps to block, "do push-ups"), no onboarding, Android first — [00:09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=595s), [00:10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=609s), [00:11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=689s).
- On-device Google MediaPipe pose detection (LLM too slow); switched from side view to **front view** so creators' faces are visible on camera, which made influencer content work — [00:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=728s), [00:12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=764s), [00:13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=780s). A visual "skeleton" overlay is the camera hook — [00:06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=400s), [00:06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=411s).
- Onboarding rebuilt on Hormozi's value equation, modelled on Clear 30's conversational onboarding: goals → pain → what you already tried and why it failed (with research citations) → micro-commitments (once even a signed pledge); users can try push-up detection before paying — [00:42:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2549s), [00:42:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2552s), [00:46:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2776s), [00:49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2952s), [00:49:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2979s). Conversion rose from ~7% to ~16% after the framework rewrite — [00:51:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3110s).
- Paywall tests: 7-day trial beat 3-day; weekly beat monthly slightly; a UGC-style **video paywall** won; an abandoned-transaction screen offers ~80% off — [00:53:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3186s), [00:53:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3208s), [00:53:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3218s), [00:55:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3329s), [00:55:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3348s). ARPU is the north star, not paywall-view or trial rate — [00:52:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3178s).
- Discord of ~3–4k members votes on features/designs/logo (quick A/B before building) — [00:16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1015s), [00:19:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1159s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Launch (mid-2025).** "Built in 14 days" launch video with a first-week free-lifetime-premium offer (Android); iOS got the same offer for only 48 h "because iOS pays more" — [00:13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=831s), [00:14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=876s), [00:22:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1347s). Their own iOS launch video claims 8,000 Android downloads in 3 days — [00:23:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1409s). Revenue after launch was small: "more or less 5,000", then corrected to "maybe 1k–2k" — [00:24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1483s), [00:25:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1503s).

**Influencers failed.** DM'd ~2,000 creators; deal = flat $800 for 4 videos (avg 100k views × ~$2 CPM), 50% upfront, no view bonus; 5–6 calisthenics creators — [00:25:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1540s), [00:26:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1575s), [00:26:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1594s), [00:26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1608s). A 38k-view collab brought ~200–500 downloads; they lost ~$2k, made it back with organic — [00:27:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1644s), [00:29:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1786s). Their read: creators' audiences came for stunts, not the demo — [00:29:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1748s).

**Organic own accounts (what worked).** Copied a competitor's format; first try 8k views — [00:30:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1839s), [00:30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1850s). Winner: over-the-shoulder clip of a guy doing push-ups at night/in the rain with phone on the pavement, caption "bro is doing push-ups to unlock Instagram 💀" + female AI voice, then a blatant demo — [00:32:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1943s), [00:33:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2006s). Skip rate ~19% (first 3 s) — [00:34:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2097s); target ~18%, at 30% change format — [01:04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3848s). A full "ad" version converted far better than a cinematic non-ad version — [00:36:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2185s). Repeat one hook, keep 3–4 formats to avoid fatigue — [00:34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2049s), [01:03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3800s).

**ManyChat "comment APP".** CTA "comment APP and I'll DM you the link": one video 6,900 comments; ~90,000 automated DMs sent; most viral video 62,000 comments; DM click-through ~33%, Play Store listing conversion ~60%. Main value is engagement inflation that pushes distribution — [00:37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2260s), [00:39:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2367s), [00:39:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2386s), [00:40:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2431s), [00:40:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2432s).

**US push.** VPN/SIM tricks get shadowbanned "like 90%" of the time; plan is US-based UGC creators at ~$1,000/month (cheaper creators gave worse videos) with 3–4 days of account warm-up — [00:59:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3554s), [00:59:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3577s), [01:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3613s), [01:00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3649s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Post a concept video before building; build only if it goes viral → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Make the core feature filmable (front-view skeleton) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Paid micro-influencers outside the use case (stunt athletes) underperformed founder-run organic → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Conversational value-equation onboarding, 7-day trial, video paywall, abandoned-checkout discount; judge by ARPU → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Geography sets ARPU: a European/teen audience halves monetization; US distribution needs US creators → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- "Profit" because no ad spend; founders' time and the ~$2k influencer loss aside. The 10% view→download rate came with a free-lifetime giveaway — not a normal benchmark.
- Revenue right after launch is inconsistent (~$5k vs ~$1–2k) — [00:25:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1503s).
- ManyChat comment-baiting and remote-controlled US phones are platform-rule grey zones; they report shadowbans.
- They paid mentors (ex-Quittr team member, transcribed "Twitter"; another founder) and recommend a ~$200/mo creator academy and Consumer Club (the host's) — [00:45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2716s), [00:47:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2874s), [01:04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3887s). Superwall-sponsored; they built paywalls in Superwall.
