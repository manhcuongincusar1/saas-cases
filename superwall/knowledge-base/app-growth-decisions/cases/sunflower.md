# Sunflower — buy the #1 niche influencer wholesale (as an employee), build around the boring daily job, and kill trial anxiety

## Idea — how they found it
- Kobe is an ex-addict, sober 7 years — [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1131s). "I cloned someone": another "quit something" app existed and he believed he could build it better — [49:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2974s).
- Built v0 himself as a 2020–21 learn-to-code bootcamp project: timer, Fiverr sunflower graphics, a journal — [21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1279s), [21:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1304s). Couldn't get it funded for "three or four years" while leading growth at Rupa ($5M → $75M in 3.5 years) — [39:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2374s), [37:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2261s). Put in $100k of his own money and raised ~$350k from people he had "made really rich" — [40:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2406s), [40:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2427s).
- Advice: "just build the smallest thing you freaking can"; "you're not in the app building game, you're in the self-improvement game" — [22:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1327s), [22:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1356s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- **Surprise 1 — the timer is the core**: "The most important feature isn't the AI sponsor, but it's the timer" — the daily job for ~1.2B people trying to stay sober; build everything around one simple daily action — [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1061s), [17:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1068s).
- **Surprise 2 — the AI sponsor works**: he didn't believe in it; usage proved him wrong → "always second-guess my assumptions"; AI lowers build cost so "just build everything... then trim" — [18:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1139s), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1160s).
- AI companion alone is a bad product: "The engagement is horrible, the retention is horrible, no one is actually going to pay" — must be bundled with tools, courses, community, content — [15:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=943s), [15:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=954s).
- Evolution: ChatGPT wrapper → prompt optimization → agent (payday follow-ups, replies in the community feed, whole-app context) — [23:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1389s); VP of Therapy + clinical escalation — [23:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1418s); biometrics and data erase for users with abusive partners — [24:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1448s); teletherapy clinic in progress — [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1538s).
- Where to build with AI: where model labs "don't know what to optimize for" (Sunflower tracks relapse) and where "the AI agent isn't the whole solution" — [26:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1572s), [26:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1605s), [28:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1701s).
- **Pricing path**: started free (Duolingo-inspired) → AI token costs "hundreds of thousands of dollars" + investors wanting revenue → monthly paywall → added annual → **annual as default** → trial toggle; offer weekly/monthly/annual so users self-select — [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s), [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2647s), [45:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2705s). Rule: make the action you most want the default — [44:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2674s).
- Price-sensitive users (addiction, little money): "we made a lot of gains by actually lowering our price" — [46:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2761s). Host reads ~$9/month or ~$35/year — [43:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2618s).
- **Trial anxiety**: priming screen "we'll remind you before your trial ends" with a cute bee mascot, then paywall — [46:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2776s), [47:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2870s). Result: +48% revenue, +70% annual revenue, +46% trial conversion, +30% trial starts — [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2799s), [46:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2814s), [47:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2824s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Hire the #1 influencer as an employee**: Eric ("Mr. Impulsive"), 50–100M views/month, made VP of Sobriety before Sunflower had revenue — [03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=233s), [03:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=237s); salary + equity + mission — [05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=325s). Drives "50 to 100k link clicks every single month" — [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=290s). 3.5M followers — [54:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3289s). Plus a few smaller influencers and ads — [04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=253s).
- Why: "purchasing in wholesale"; "much more defensible" (other apps constantly pitch Eric); he knows the roadmap and his link-driving approach is "way, way, way different than it was a year ago" — [07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=455s), [07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=463s), [08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=492s). He sits in Slack seeing user testimonials — [16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1017s). Precedent: Function Health + Mark Hyman — [06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=408s).
- **Facebook is underrated**: Eric's biggest accounts are Facebook, then TikTok — [03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=212s), [04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=253s).
- Bootstrapped version: don't pay equity for promotion; make the creator the hero — "I could be your developer... do this 50/50" — [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=685s). He admits he couldn't have built this without VC — [08:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=538s).
- **Arbitrage rule**: "never want to be the smallest sponsor" — be the biggest line item (conference example: ask what $200k buys when the top package is $100k) — [31:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1877s), [32:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1923s); losing a main sponsor costs an influencer 50–75% of revenue, so they treat you better — [33:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2017s).
- **Rejects UGC account farms** ("100 TikTok accounts on a farm") as arbitrage that platforms punish; $1M ≠ $100M — to get there: 10–30M real followers, working ads, own community, or retail partnerships ("four to five different ways") — [29:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1780s), [30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1817s), [30:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1859s).
- **Brand**: "never ever ever do like pure brand marketing"; "all marketing is brand marketing"; spend Super Bowl money building 30–40M followers instead; strength of impression > impressions — [36:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2165s), [36:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2178s), [37:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2237s), [33:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2037s).
- **Fundraising**: first deck (my background, usage: 22k MAU, 762 reviews) failed; the deck that raised talked about the future, PMF proof, and "revenue that goes up, not usage" — [41:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2484s), [42:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2523s), [42:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2573s).

## Lessons → decisions
- One top niche influencer on payroll (salary + equity + mission) can beat a roster of 30 per-post deals; be the biggest sponsor they have → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Don't ignore Facebook Reels for older/adult audiences → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Build around the simplest daily job (timer); AI companion only as part of a bundle → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Annual as default; offer weekly/monthly/annual; lower price for price-sensitive audiences; add trial-reminder priming → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Start free only if you can afford AI costs and investors don't need revenue — he couldn't → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- UGC farms may get you to $1M but not $100M; plan the durable channel (audience, ads, community, retail) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)

## Caveats
- VC-funded (~$6.5M): the employee-influencer model needed capital; Kobe says so himself. YC + a16z speedrun backing; he openly advocates his own contrarian views (subscriptions declining, physical goods like sobriety pins could out-earn subs — [52:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3137s)) — opinion, not data.
- Narrator says hiring influencers as employees is "10 times more effective" [01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=91s); Kobe only says "so much more effective" — no 10× figure.
- Trial-reminder lift is stated as both +56% [46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2788s) and +48% (the on-screen data); use +48%. Sample size and duration not given.
- No retention or CAC numbers; "1.2 billion" and "90% never access care" are his market claims (secondhand stats). Superwall hosts; SpyTok sponsor read.
