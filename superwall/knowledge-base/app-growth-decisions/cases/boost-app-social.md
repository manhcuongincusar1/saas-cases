# Boost App Social — what a paid-only app costs to run, and why they sold

## Idea — how they found it
- 2018–2019: a web hashtag search/generator on a vector database; little traction — [00:04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=255s), [00:04:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=275s).
- Saw a competing iOS app (Hashtag Expert) with traction and had better tech, so moved into iOS subscriptions — [00:04:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=291s), [00:04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=297s). Mobile fit the job: research hashtags on the phone and paste straight into Instagram — [00:05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=350s), [00:06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=365s).
- Unfair advantage: they already sold Instagram services, so they knew the buyer (mom-and-pop shops, influencers) and "what buttons to push" — [00:10:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=622s), [00:10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=627s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Added features beyond hashtags (fonts, video/photo editing) so users stayed and would pay more than ~$40/yr, "maybe $100/yr" — [00:06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=387s).
- Learned onboarding/paywall/statistics by tearing down successful apps + Mixpanel + A/B tests — [00:05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=313s), [00:05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=326s).
- He argues "business" apps sell at better numbers: buyers see a money-making outcome, so they pay $200–300/yr and convert more smoothly (*heuristic*) — [00:33:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2007s), [00:33:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2016s), [00:39:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2380s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
**Spend ramp.** $100 → $500 → $1,000 → $10k/day on Meta — [00:06:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=414s).

**Creator sourcing (cold email at volume).** "A little bit black hatty": ~1,000 Gmail accounts in a shared inbox, a script sending ~10 emails per account, to creator emails scraped from Instagram bios; 10–20% reply — [00:13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=796s), [00:14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=844s). VA replies with a pre-recorded video from Alex, moves creators to a Telegram group, $100 upfront to prove it isn't a scam (1–2 ran with the money), signed contract with **perpetual** usage rights (no renewals, no "pay me $100k now that it works") — [00:11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=679s), [00:12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=720s), [00:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=746s), [00:15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=921s), [00:16:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=982s). Ended with ~200–300 creatives under contract — [00:11:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=710s), [00:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=713s).

**Creative.** TikTok-style UGC from existing creators given a one-page brief of proven hooks — [00:18:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1094s), [00:18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1128s). Three lessons: (1) captions, "80%" watch without sound; (2) green screen — person talking over a big app demo; (3) obsess over the first 5 s, visual hook as much as copy — [00:21:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1316s), [00:22:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1323s), [00:22:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1337s), [00:22:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1375s), [00:23:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1383s). Phone-in-hand shots help; they re-cut 5 delivered videos into ~15 variants, not 2,000 — [00:27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1670s), [00:29:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1762s). Specific-frustration hooks ("stuck at 1,000 views?") beat generic ones — [00:26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1564s), [00:26:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1586s). Today he would use LLMs + Veo for extreme "end-of-emotional-spectrum" AI hooks — [00:24:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1475s), [00:30:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1857s).

**Hit rate.** "One video out of 85 would get all the ad spend and work"; ~97% of creatives never work however good they look; a creative can take $20k and still fail — [00:12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=759s), [00:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1241s), [00:31:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1898s).

**Account risk.** Ad accounts banned 6–8 times, including "permanent" bans, all recovered after long appeals; later a Meta rep — [00:08:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=498s), [00:08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=534s).

Reason: 100% Meta-dependent — "maybe the CPMs are going to double… it's taking chips off the table" — [00:35:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2155s), [00:36:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2195s). ## Lessons → decisions
- Paid-only works only with creative volume: plan for ~1 winner per ~85 videos and budget the misses → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Buy perpetual rights in creator contracts; they become a sellable asset → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
- Captions + green-screen demo + specific-pain hook as the default app-ad template → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Single-channel (Meta) dependence is a reason to sell; an exit packages the ad account, rep and creative library → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
- Prosumer/business use cases support $200–300/yr pricing → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported; the sale price is only "seven figures"; buyer unnamed under NDA.
- 1,000 Gmail accounts + scripted cold email breaks Gmail's terms and anti-spam rules; repeated Meta bans show the account risk.
- Conflict of interest: Alex now sells Screens Design (app teardown library, "2,200 apps") — [00:41:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2514s). Host reads a SpyTok ad mid-episode — [00:25:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1503s); Superwall-sponsored.
- Meta CPMs and policy are as of 2023–2025; secondhand claims (Adam Lyttle "100 apps, 2–3 doing really good money") not verified here — [00:38:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2301s).
