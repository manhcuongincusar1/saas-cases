# Raphael Kramer — meme-to-app distribution engine that works, on products that never retained

## Idea — how they found it
- Engine: "I would find something that pops off, find it early, make a game, put on the app store, and then make videos about it and hope they go viral" — [07:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=473s). Rationale: a trend "is kind of like 2D"; a game lets you interact with the meme, so people share it — [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=490s).
- DaBaby-head-as-a-car meme: saw it the day it dropped, pulled an all-nighter, shipped, promoted from his personal account (~couple hundred thousand followers) — [06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=398s), [06:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=404s).
- Consumer-app ideas come from "doom scrolling and me being a consumer myself" — [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1000s). Debloat AI: bloat transformation videos had ~100 people in the comments asking "how?"; "the comment section… is a really good indicator of how strong a problem is" — [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1066s), [17:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1073s).
- Untapped: founders build for their own age; e.g. a plant-watering app "makes probably like 3 million a month" (his guess) — [26:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1585s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Started at 13 during COVID with a no-code game builder (Buildbox), "zero coding experience"; hired Unity devs once money came in — [08:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=530s), [09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=548s).
- Games monetized only with Google AdMob: ad every second death, 30-s rewarded ad to revive, no IAP — [09:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=572s). ~$0.10 per user on a "somewhat poorly monetized game" vs $0.80–$1 for good mobile games — [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=618s), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=625s). "The game was developed to be good on a TikTok video" — [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=638s).
- Retention: "I didn't consider retention at all" — [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=801s); "we never really cracked retention" — [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=838s); "I've never been a product guy" — [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=823s).
- Debloat AI onboarding: face scan **before** the paywall (sunk cost), then a "little bit exaggerated" bad rating ("you're really bloated"), then paywall the fix — [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1484s), [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1507s). A co-founder ran product; Raphael did framing/angles.
- Amanda AI (relationship coach, analyze text screenshots): TikTok ads got "restricted and suppressed" in the relationship-advice niche; sold to a buyer who liked the app, not its numbers — [27:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1625s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Dev-log TikToks** ("day 4 of making the DaBaby game"), A/B-testing hooks early, adding viewer-suggested features — [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=667s), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=677s). Host counted 9 videos at ~100k–1M views each — [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=732s). Raphael: ~300–400k downloads from the first videos, then K-factor (middle-schoolers beating each other's scores) and chart visibility carried it — [07:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=425s), [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=759s). #2 overall "I believe", #1 in racing "I think" — [07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=443s).
- **Influencer game studio** (founded at 15, ~10 employees) — [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=234s): 50/50 splits with influencers — [33:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2018s). Escape the Toilet: Skibidi Toilet creator (~5M subs then) made one YouTube Short (>50M views) → "2 million downloads, two maybe three million" in ~2 weeks, plus word of mouth; game later taken down ("messy situation") — [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=264s), [04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=293s).
- **Influencer fit > size**: a 5M-follower creator at ~2M views/video produced "a completely flopped" game — [33:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2033s). Look for an inside joke (big forehead → clean-the-forehead game) or a universe fans can only watch (Skibidi) — [34:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2073s), [35:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2105s). Comments where people reply to each other, not flame emojis — [36:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2215s).
- **No scripts for influencers**: work with ones who can already go viral, incentivize with 50% ownership, "they know their audience the best" — [40:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2415s); make them feel it's their game (weekly calls, character updates) — "pretty much transformational" — [41:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2518s), [42:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2539s).
- **Outreach**: cold-email relationship managers (50–100 emails/day) worked less well than a credible Instagram profile with mutuals — [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=326s), [05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=335s); "positioning yourself as one of them" — [33:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1994s); DMs 3 influencers at night, wakes up to 2 replies — [33:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1983s).
- **Debloat AI**: ~8 faceless slideshow accounts paid on CPM ("basically free installs") — [18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1100s), [18:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1114s); TikTok ads at "a couple cents per download", which "saturated in a couple days", then a few weeks at "maybe like 30% profit based off ad spends"; copycats now do $20–30k/month — [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=933s), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=963s).
- **Hiring**: outsource editing immediately ("super low leverage"), keep scripts in-house (never found good script writers) — [20:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1205s), [20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1213s); recruit via Twitter posts and Discord servers, ask for free samples — [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1270s), [21:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1289s); teach high-schoolers who doom-scroll — [19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1164s).
- **Channel preference**: paid ads if you can crack them ("spend 100 bucks, you get 150 back") — [38:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2300s), [38:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2307s); friends with a few retainer influencers at "a 3x ROAS" (secondhand) — [38:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2325s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Comment sections full of "how?" are demand evidence; trends can be turned into interactive products fast → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Distribution without retention caps you at small exits; he is now taking a "big swing" on a product he can keep → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Pick influencers by audience fit and comment quality, give equity and real involvement, no scripts → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- New-niche TikTok ad arbitrage lasts days, not months → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Scan-before-paywall + exaggerated result as conversion lever (ethically aggressive) → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Small apps sell to buyers who like the app itself, even without strong numbers → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
