# Dan Kwan — find a movement already in motion, pick a side, and post scrappy founder content before building a growth machine

## Idea — how they found it
- Rule: "pretty much every product or project I've worked on has stemmed from some sort of momentum wave and an existing cult" — [19:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1195s); "You're either building the cult or finding the cult", building is better for LTV — [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1178s).
- **Conch**: started marketing a friend's "ChatGPT for Google Docs" extension (2 weeks of videos, ~100k views, few conversions) — [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=148s); the turn came when Edward Tian's AI detector (GPTZero) launched — [02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=156s). Underlying cult: students' hatred of busywork — [20:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1207s).
- **Arise**: anime fans were already posting "we need the system in real life" — [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1096s); timed to the Solo Leveling season-2 finale — [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1178s). Existing apps had no long onboarding, no social features and monetized with ads, "probably the worst way to monetize your app" — [18:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1114s).
- **Shepherd**: Christian revival + his own failure to read the Bible daily → Tamagotchi-style "raise your lamb" app — [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s); rode Duolingo being "canceled" for AI-first: "God first lamb over the AI first owl" — [27:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1678s).
- **Us vs them**: "anything that's going viral online is often outraging one group"; side with whichever has "the larger or louder emotion" — [09:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=576s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Shepherd was a "two-week build alongside a two-week growth sprint"; "the app broke a ton" — [22:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1352s), [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s).
- Listen to the community: an AI chat feature drew backlash in the ~10,000-member Discord (positioning was anti-AI) → replaced by a "custom devotional" with no AI in customer-facing copy — [28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1695s), [28:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1726s).
- **Paused all Shepherd creator ops** to build social loops/K-factor (NGL, Gas, TBH-style prayer/accountability buddies): "not like an app we should be pushing such a hacky version of" — [23:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1380s), [24:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1499s), [25:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1549s).
- Caution: virality is easy-ish; after the influx "that's kind of where you're seeing whether there's actual cracks in your product" — [31:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1868s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Lean to one extreme**: maximum cringe/obvious-ad-but-entertaining (blank billboards, a Google-Slides launch video) or maximum realism — [05:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=331s), [08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=485s). Snap-style captions filmed natively in Snapchat; "the lower quality, the more you're leaning into the realistic pillar" — [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=393s), [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=411s). "Marketing alpha ladder": culture starts on Snapchat, bubbles to IG/TikTok, then marketers copy — [06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=370s).
- **Founder-led**: "if you're scared to do any sort of founder-led marketing, then you shouldn't be building your app" — [16:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1014s); "the founder's biggest influencer" for cold starts — [17:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1044s).
- **Frankensteining**: stitch already-viral clips from your subject (Shepherd: podcast clips of Christian influencer Bryce Crawford + hooks found by searching "Bible reading") — [24:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1464s), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1481s). Shepherd's most viral videos were on Instagram; "much easier to grow an Instagram account from scratch" (he says it's arguable) — [23:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1407s), [23:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1411s).
- **Arise**: carousels about fans wanting the system in real life + influencers/meme pages at **$1–2 CPM** against a **~$6–7 RPM** → ~$50k/month — [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1085s), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1131s), [19:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1144s).
- **Conch**: mostly organic; "We did test paid, but didn't like the margins" — [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=633s).
- **TikTok Shop crossover** ("it's all the same game") — [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=703s): Final Boss Sour hit #1 in food & beverage TikTok Shop revenue — [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=766s). Pillars: small-business factor (shot in the founder's kitchen even though VC-backed) and clearance/flash-sale framing — [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=860s). Script "we made way too much sour candies, so we're putting it on sale…" rinsed by every creator, "every single time it's hit" — [14:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=888s), [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=929s).
- **Sequencing advice**: post about the product to find content-market fit, don't hunt for the perfect format first — [31:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1886s); stitch videos found by searching problem phrases — [31:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1909s); turn good influencers into full-time UGC creators on a brand account; "don't touch paid until you've cracked at least five winning formats" — [32:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1931s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Ideas = existing community + current momentum + a clear enemy; ship in weeks to catch the wave → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Founder posts first; scrappy/native beats polished; stitch existing viral clips → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Influencer CPM vs your RPM is the unit-economics check ($1–2 CPM vs $6–7 RPM) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Hold paid until ~5 formats win; Conch tested paid and kept organic for margin → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- After a viral spike, decide whether to keep pushing UGC or rebuild for retention/K-factor (Shepherd paused creators) → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Beware fake acquirers fishing for info → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)

## Caveats
- Transcript gap 02:44–05:25: the Conch growth story is only in the cold-open clip. The narrator's "Conch AI, which hit 500k in revenue from a single viral video" [00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=29s) overstates — Dan says "500k in a month". $2.2M is lifetime revenue, not ARR.
- Shepherd's $75k MRR is a 2-week figure from a launch spike; creator ops were then halted, so durability is unknown. Arise's $50k/month is unsourced beyond his statement.
- Conch was an AI-detection-bypass tool: cease-and-desist from his school, local news, near-expulsion [09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=595s) — reputational/legal risk of "cheating" categories. Video 2025-10 (no-code era); Superwall sponsors; host's Consumer Club promoted.
