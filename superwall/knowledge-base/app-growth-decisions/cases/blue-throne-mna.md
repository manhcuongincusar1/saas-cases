# Blue Throne (Josh) — the acquirer's scorecard for a sellable app

## Who is talking
- Blue Throne buys apps that did 0→1 (one channel, $1–2M ARR) and does the 1→10 with its team — [00:03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=228s), [00:04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=259s). 70 people (infra, growth, monetization, ad monetization, finance) — [00:41:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2518s). Targets apps doing ~$400k/yr up to ~$15M/yr — [00:46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2799s).
- Everything below is *heuristic* from a buyer, except where marked *secondhand* (other companies' numbers).

## Valuation mechanics
- Two paths to $100M+: **growth story** (30–50% EBITDA margin + strong growth) valued on run rate, best case **6×**; or **past performance** valued on LTM revenue — [00:06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=387s), [00:06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=398s), [00:06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=406s). Roughly ~$17M ARR on the growth path vs ~$30–33M ARR on LTM — [00:08:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=511s), [00:08:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=521s).
- Run rate is extrapolated from a quarter or 6 months, not one (seasonal) month — [00:07:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=459s).

## The six rules
1. **Own a narrow category** you can credibly claim #1 in (Flo = women's health; Runna = Couch-to-5K, bought by Strava) — but not so narrow it isn't believable — [00:09:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=575s), [00:10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=647s), [00:11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=702s). Size markets by summing the top ~30 apps in Sensor Tower — [00:13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=805s).
2. **Locals, not tourists.** "Revenue multiples are really just present value math on LTV", and cohort retention leads LTV. Check the retention curve of your top 20% users by engagement; it should flatten — [00:14:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=889s), [00:15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=900s). Year-1 resubscribe: **50% amazing · 40% good · 30% okay · 20% bad** (dating lower) — [00:17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s). Resubscriber rate beats DAU as a signal — [00:19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1156s).
3. **Earn organic.** Organic share of installs: **≥50% great · 35–50% good · <20% = UA treadmill** — [00:20:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1239s). He counts TikTok UGC as paid ("less measurable, less predictable") — [00:21:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1311s). Buyers ask for the *machine* that replicates virality, not the last viral video — [00:23:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1397s). ASO is getting harder: "80% more apps being submitted" this year — [00:23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1425s). No consumer app he talks to has nailed AI search — [00:24:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1447s).
4. **Charge premium.** Pre-ChatGPT ceiling ~$60/yr; ChatGPT reset the anchor to $20/month — [00:25:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1511s), [00:25:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1524s). $30M ARR needs ~500k payers at $4.99/mo vs 125k at $20/mo — [00:26:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1561s), [00:26:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1569s). If under $10/mo, raise price on a 10–20% test segment until it breaks — [00:33:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2014s), [00:33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2030s). Weekly plans fit short-need categories (dating, ex-stalking trackers) and cash flow, not long-term value — [00:31:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1876s), [00:31:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1899s).
5. **Build the narrative for a buyer type:** aggregators (Blue Throne: clean books, can we 10× it), strategics (pay the highest multiples for synergies, e.g. Strava buying Runna as top of funnel), private equity (cash flow + EBITDA; founder must stay 3–5 years), acqui-hire/IP (rare in consumer). IPO ≈ 2% of consumer companies — [00:35:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2120s), [00:35:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2151s), [00:37:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2220s). Build relationships 6–12 months ahead and read the buyer's last 5 deals — [00:38:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2286s), [00:38:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2303s). Ask buyers what moved the needle on past deals — [00:42:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2551s).
6. **Sell before you need to:** your alternative to selling sets the price; 12 months of growth reads far better than 3 (which may be 3 viral videos); sell before the peak — [00:43:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2605s), [00:44:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2694s), [00:45:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2757s).

## Early-stage levers (six-figure founders)
Fix organic early; test pricing/paywall UI constantly (Flo ran 100+ experiments/month — *secondhand*); stay on one paid channel until its ROAS ceiling — [00:48:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2934s), [00:49:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2959s), [00:49:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2966s). Distribution without retention is still buyable: "60,000 downloads organically a month, they're just not hanging around" is interesting to them — [00:41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2460s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Exit value = growth story × retention × organic share; plan these from six figures, not at sale time → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
- Test higher prices ($20/mo anchor); premium-priced apps (Waking Up) earn far more per download (host: "roughly 10 times" typical, [00:00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=37s)) → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Power-user retention curve and year-1 resubscribe are the metrics buyers read → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Low organic share = treadmill; master one channel before adding another → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Influencer-founded apps convert well but carry key-person risk in a sale — [00:30:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1800s) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
