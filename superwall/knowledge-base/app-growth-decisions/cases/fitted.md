# Fitted — creator-as-co-founder (crawl/walk/run equity) solved distribution, but the founder says PMF is not found

## Idea — how they found it
- Fitted began ~8 years earlier as a dorm-room on-demand laundry service scaled to 18 cities without owning machines — [06:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=363s), [06:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=379s). First taste of virality: an investor posted a plain screen recording of the site → 600k views, but a service business can't use national virality — [06:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=399s), [07:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=422s). (He dates this both "2021" [05:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=356s) and "like 2020" [06:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=407s).)
- Model copied from a friend's brand Breathwrk: TikTok account 0 → ~3M followers in 2020 by giving an aligned creator meaningful equity — [02:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=178s).
- Six friends sent him the same TikTok: Max had built himself an outfit generator in class and it went viral; Reed contacted him on every platform, guessed his email — "before I even had a product" — [03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=207s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Digitize your closet, get outfit recommendations, resell, share with friends — [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=910s). First tool built = the outfit generator, because Max's viral video proved pull for it — [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=917s).
- Web app "just didn't convert"; after a $1,400 15-minute call with Nikita Bier they spent six months building mobile — [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=305s), [05:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=328s), [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=334s). Mobile launch + one mega-viral video → "a couple hundred thousand downloads off the rip" — [05:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=342s).
- **Features tested on TikTok before building**: tease features and watch engagement — [15:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=955s); "stalking my boyfriend's closet" existed only on web, the video hit, so it ships to mobile next — [16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=986s).
- **Honest limitation**: "I haven't found product market fit yet" — [10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=626s); "getting it to a million users is not hard… I know my churn is high. I know my app isn't sticky enough" — [23:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1435s), [24:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1449s).
- Planned fixes: drop the hard paywall ("hamstringing user growth") and make the app free with paywalled features — [18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1089s), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1096s); cut closet-building friction (receipt scraper, selfie add, quick add, TaskRabbit's 20–30k taskers) — [24:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1477s), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1368s). Holding back celebrity posts until the app is "in its most dialed spot" — [25:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1518s).
- Longer game: closet data moat (2M+ pieces, "hundreds of millions of dollars" of clothes uploaded) → AI resale marketplace; Poshmark one-click resale, Paramount "Clueless" official virtual closet — [18:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1124s), [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1138s), [22:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1337s), [22:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1350s). A FitCoin token "close to a quarter million dollars in volume a day" — [19:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1153s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Crawl**: sub-account "Fitted by Max", $20/video "to just cook" — [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1662s), [03:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=229s); 20–30k views per video with no product — [03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=234s). **Walk/run**: when Max graduated with nothing lined up → vested equity + % rev share of all app revenue, rising with contribution — [28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1700s), [28:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1731s).
- Launch week: raised ~$10k from a friend, team in a Palm Springs apartment; a 2M-view video on day one → 100k waitlist — [04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=253s), [04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=270s), [04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=274s).
- Winning video had "no gimmick": just how to use the Fitted Closet, to Max's 50–60k hyper-engaged followers — [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=442s), [07:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=449s). Framing is "follow along in our journey as we build", never "download this app" — [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=859s).
- Playbook: viral ~once a month over 1.5 years; keep inventing content formulas, cross-post everywhere, "with the ones that hit, you just put ad dollars behind them" — [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=284s), [04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=297s).
- Max = day-zero team member: reads comments/DMs as voice of customer, which weighs the roadmap; sits in design meetings — [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=697s), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=718s). "Founder-market fit" in the creator: Max's favourite thing is getting dressed in the morning — [26:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1587s).
- Creator sweet spot: 1k–50k followers (good at views, not yet priced as influencers); 50–100k+ "know their worth" — [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1624s), [26:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1613s). Idea: "an app for every single creator" / studio model — [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1803s), [30:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1812s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Validate demand with a creator posting before the product exists ($20/video, 20–30k views) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Tie the right creator in with vesting equity + rev share instead of a per-post contract; look in the 1k–50k follower band → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Put paid spend only behind organic winners → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Distribution without retention caps the business; a hard paywall can throttle a social/collection product → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Single-creator dependency (95%) is concentration risk → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- Self-reported; no conversion, retention or churn numbers despite admitting high churn. The CPI claim comes from the host, not Reed's own words in this episode.
- Survivorship: one exceptional creator account; not a repeatable multi-creator system. Revenue (~$150k total) is small relative to views.
- Crypto token (FitCoin) and "generational business" claims are forward-looking. Hosted by Superwall (paywall vendor). Published 2025-11.
