# GlamUp · Sprout — arbitrage a proven app into a new audience, then run a scripted creator machine and a referral loop that floods TikTok search

## Idea — how they found it
- Co-founder Aaron was in the H0 cohort with UMAX's founders and saw it work early; they built "loosely inspired by Umax" for women — makeup/skincare instead of rating facial features — after finding few such apps on the App Store — [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=264s), [07:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=431s).
- Name, concept and mechanics decided in "a 10-minute conversation" — [05:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=313s). Host frames it as "market arbitrage".
- Sprout chosen next to experiment with talking-head UGC and a higher-LTV problem; pricing "up to $80 a month" — [51:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3082s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- GlamUp MVP in "3 weeks to a month"; "the app barely worked" but distribution ran in parallel; v2 took another month — [06:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=381s), [06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=393s).
- **Onboarding = priming, not analytics**: questions build need — [17:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1073s); Sprout adds "fun fact" pages that compare you-with vs you-without the app — [38:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2293s).
- **The scan is fake**: the AI API only runs after payment to save cost — [26:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1600s).
- **Referral gate**: results blurred → "view results" (paywall) or "invite three friends" (free) — [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1120s). Users aged ~13–25 don't want to pay — [19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1140s) — so they paste codes in TikTok comments; the copy template is "download Glam Up and use my referral code", so the brand keyword ranks "pretty high on TikTok" search — [19:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1173s), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1178s). ~1.6M referral/share requests tracked — [09:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=578s). Conversion "I don't think it changed much": getting 3 friends through onboarding is hard, so many sharers "ended up converting" — [21:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1301s).
- **Paywall stack (GlamUp)**: hard paywall (weekly, from $8.99) → on close, 50%-off paywall — [25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1500s), [25:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1513s); upgrade paywall when weekly scan quota runs out — [25:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1531s).
- **Forced-rating screen**: "leave a rating" then an "I rated it" button required to continue — [28:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1688s); her current co-founder is uncomfortable with it, so the newest app dropped it — [29:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1745s).
- **Sprout**: no annual plan ("doesn't make sense for a job searching app"), weekly/monthly, billed via Stripe instead of App Store IAP — [40:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2405s), [40:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2418s). Paywall fires on the first right-swipe (action-gated).
- **Hard paywall test (newest app)**: hard paywall straight after onboarding vs letting users try a feature first → "we see a 50% increase in conversion" for the hard paywall — [44:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2682s); she now recommends it — [40:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2445s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Founder goes viral first** on all four apps: she and her co-founder each warmed ~3 accounts, found the winner, then scaled to "30, 40 creators" for GlamUp — [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s), [15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=957s) (earlier she says ~20 creators — [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=610s)).
- **GlamUp format**: faceless slideshow, girl without → with makeup, GlamUp branding, app screenshot; first post ~10M views — [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=627s). Before/after images = Pinterest photos de-made-up ~10× with another app, then enhanced; not real users — [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=653s). Posted "hundreds, maybe even thousands" of times with the same on-screen text, caption, hashtags — [11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=719s); text "never saw myself with the / right makeup routine 😭😭" — [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=752s); caption "I will be unstoppable 😮‍💨" — [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=774s); ~10 rotating audios, others "just doesn't work" — [13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=783s). Line count, text size, off-centre position were tested.
- Failed first: Latina makeup slideshows, color theory, celebrities, tutorials — [14:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=887s).
- **Sprout formats**: "war is over, I finally landed my first full-time job" faceless spreadsheet dropdown → "accepted"; length tested 5/6/7 s, 6.7 s won — [35:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2110s), [35:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2138s); tilted (not 90°) camera, scripted zoom timings — [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2172s); "probably... hundreds of millions of views" — [35:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2147s), but conversion attribution: "We are not sure" — [37:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2233s). Also fake recruiter-call skits — [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2080s).
- **Recruiting**: inbound via application forms posted in big influencers' Discords (where creators are semi-trained) and on Handshake (students wanting a "social media internship") — [47:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2878s), [48:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2905s); plus outbound. 15-minute vibe-check interview — [33:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2014s); mandatory course with module quizzes; >1,000 creators onboarded, "over 50%... finished this course with no previous experiences", going viral within the first 2 weeks on new accounts — [31:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1914s), [32:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1923s).
- **Content bank** (her stated moat): per format — idea, details, example videos, audios, line-by-line script with the emotion per line ("sarcastic tone", "roll your eyes") and shot type; she films example videos herself — [45:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2755s), [46:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2782s).
- **Pay**: flat $10–$30/post + view bonuses on a *decreasing* CPM: 10k views $40, 50k $50, 100k $80, 1M $800 (vs flat 10k $10 … 1M $1,000) — [48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2938s), [49:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2953s).
- **Accidental ASO win**: a copycat with a similar name went viral but didn't rank, so traffic flowed to GlamUp — "made us 100K in a week" — [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=359s).
- Learning diet: ~90% from downloading and walking through competitor apps, ~10% from X — [53:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3230s); "hours" of TikTok scrolling — [55:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3321s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Clone a working mechanic into an adjacent, unclaimed audience; decide fast → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Founder finds the format on warmed accounts before hiring; then script creators line-by-line (emotion + shot) → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Referral-to-unlock with a template containing the app name doubles as TikTok search SEO → [aso-search](../decisions/aso-search.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Test price up, hard paywall after onboarding, and a discount paywall on close; her results contradict "let users explore first" → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Decreasing-CPM bonus ladder keeps creator cost bounded on mega-hits → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)

## Caveats
- All numbers self-reported; several are rough ("I think", "probably"). Creator counts conflict (20 vs 30–40 for GlamUp; Sprout "400M in 7 days" vs "400–500M monthly"). GlamUp plans: "only weekly and monthly" [22:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1348s) vs "we only have weekly" [25:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1515s).
- Ethically grey tactics (fake scan, Pinterest faces shown as results, forced "I rated it") — her own co-founder pushed back; App Store review risk is not discussed.
- The +50% hard-paywall result is one test on one app; Halo AI measured the opposite pattern. Sprout's own paywall is action-gated, not post-onboarding.
- She sells consulting/courses for creator programs. Superwall (paywall vendor) hosts; SpyTok sponsor read in-episode. Dated May 2026; GlamUp tactics are ~2 years old.
