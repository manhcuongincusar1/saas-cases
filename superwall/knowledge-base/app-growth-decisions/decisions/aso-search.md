# Should I grow through search, and how?

## Inputs to collect first
- **The literal phrase** a target user types into the App Store / Google / TikTok search bar. Can't write it → stop here (rule 1).
- Is the app built and named yet? (Naming is the biggest lever; renaming later costs brand.)
- Niche: painkiller (condition, specific job) or vitamin (meditation, "productivity")? Embarrassing / unshareable?
- Revenue goal: side income / six figures, or seven-plus figures? Time horizon?
- Platform: iOS only (all evidence is iOS), web funnel (needed for SEO), Android (no evidence).
- Can anyone on the team make content or run ads? (Search is the default for builders who can't.)
- Budget for Apple Ads to measure RPI; current RPI, trial-start and trial-to-paid if live.

## Default answer
Treat search as a **pull** channel: it harvests demand that already exists and creates none. Use it when you can name the phrase people already type, ideally a narrow painkiller phrase that big apps don't target. Pick the keyword **before** building, name the app after it, and put the strongest phrase in title + subtitle. Expect a ceiling of roughly six figures a year per app, expect rankings to erode, and start a second, push channel while you are still #1. Web SEO only makes sense with a web funnel and existing domain authority. AI search is unproven for consumer apps: no founder in the corpus reports revenue from it.

## Decision rules
1. If you cannot write the exact search phrase, or the phrase is a crowded generic (calorie/macro tracker) → search is not your channel. Go to [choose-channel](choose-channel.md) / [ugc-creators](ugc-creators.md). **[medium]** — Steve Young ([00:07:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=439s)): "ASO is a pool… people have to search for it"; Dmitri, YourMove ([00:19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1189s)): SEO captures latent demand only.
2. If you're picking an idea → check demand with free signals before any paid tool: App Store autofill ([00:23:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1415s)), search the store for your own problem and find nothing ([Kyle 00:03:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=203s), [Nicole 00:04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=297s)), a Reddit community of thousands with the same problem ([Caleb 00:11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=679s)). **[strong]**
   - Free tooling (tested 2026-10-08, *not from the corpus*): autofill = `https://search.itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZSearchHints.woa/wa/hints?clientApplication=Software&term=<phrase>` with headers `X-Apple-Store-Front: 143441-1,29` (US); competitor ratings = `https://itunes.apple.com/search?country=us&entity=software&limit=10&term=<phrase>` (`trackName`, `userRatingCount`). Paid/trial: Sensor Tower free trials, Astro ([Kyle 00:21:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1263s)); ASO Mobile lists every keyword a competitor indexes for ([Steve 00:15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=905s)). AppFollow got worse at keyword discovery after Apple API changes ([00:25:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1521s)).
3. To judge competition, read the results page and ignore tool "difficulty": how many reviews the top 3 have, and how many put the phrase in their **title**. Few titles plus #2–#3 at ~100+ reviews → beatable ([00:12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=751s)). Check whether they buy ads (Sensor Tower, Meta Ads Library) ([00:25:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1504s)). **[weak]** (Steve only)
4. To find keywords nobody watches, scan a big keyword's results for the app whose review count doesn't fit (400k, 2M, ~1M, 71k, 42k… **4k**), then look up what that app ranks for ([00:24:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1441s)). **[weak]**
5. Volume gate: Steve wants ~30+ "search volume" on AppFollow/ASO Mobile's scale ([00:15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=934s)). He admits he doesn't know the unit. It comes from one app ranking #1 for two keywords at 28 and 34 that got 100–150 downloads/day ([00:17:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1073s)). **[weak]**
6. If not yet built → **keyword first, then name, then build.** Ask "do you want to call your app that?" **[medium]** — Steve: "the ones that got to profitability really quick… we find the keyword first and then build the app" ([00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=485s)). His client named the app after a ~30-volume keyword ([00:15:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=945s)), ranked #1 in week one, made $1,600 in the first month and is near $20k MRR years later ([00:15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=957s)). Kyle went from plain "Cardstock" with no subtitle to keyworded fields and held #1 for 2–3 years ([00:14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=843s)).
7. **App Store mechanics:** title and subtitle do almost all the work. Asked whether they are "80% of ASO", Steve says "Yeah, 100%. That's all I pay attention to" ([00:21:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1269s)). **[medium]** (Steve + Kyle)
   - Title = strongest phrase + brand: `Green Noise App`; `Cardstock: Sports Card Scanner` ([Kyle 00:15:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=901s)).
   - Subtitle = the autofill line, or the high-value phrase that didn't fit: `Green Noise for Better Sleep` was literally the autofill suggestion ([00:21:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1311s)); `Find Value for Baseball Cards`.
   - Keyword field = remaining words, comma-separated: `green,noise,app,calm,free` ([00:21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1278s); the transcript has "comm"). Apple's 30/30/100-character limits are *not stated in the corpus*.
   - Ship the bare minimum with keywords, then test. Don't spend months perfecting it ([Kyle 00:17:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1071s); [Steve 00:22:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1359s)).
8. If you already rank #1 for the head term (or just bought an app that does) → expand into variants that contain it instead of diluting. On one acquired app Steve found 24 keywords with ≥24 volume that all contain the main word ([00:47:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2825s)). Long titles stacking related painkiller terms ("Migraine Buddy: Track Headache") ([00:47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2849s)). **[weak]**
9. Choose **painkiller + narrow** over vitamin + broad: "meditation" is a vitamin, "green noise" and "migraine tracker" are painkillers you can hard-paywall ([00:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s)). Paruresis app: 30%+ conversion "just because of how niche it is" ([Caleb 00:12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=774s)). **[medium]**
10. Best target: a niche with **proven revenue and sloppy execution**, e.g. "Migraine Buddy does about 50,000 a month… their onboarding and their paywall, not good" ([00:12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=724s)). Start from the category with the highest RPI ([00:10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=659s)). Steve calls it being "in the corner" ([00:09:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=589s)). **[weak]**
11. Ratings are the second ranking input after title/subtitle ([00:25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1558s)). Ask for a rating inside onboarding: Clear 30 went from 50 to almost 1,000 reviews after copying Cal AI's rating screen ([00:21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1296s)), and GlamUp/Sprout use the same screen ([00:27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1662s)). Hard paywalls bring 1-star reviews ([00:20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1250s)). **[medium]** (Check current App Review rules; the corpus doesn't cover them.)
12. Use organic search plus a little Apple Ads to measure RPI before any Meta spend: "at least a $3 RPI before you can think about Meta" ([00:17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1029s)), or $10–15 cost per trial ([00:34:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2056s)). When there are untouched keywords, Steve moves budget from Apple Ads to ASO ([00:09:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=568s)). **[weak]**
13. Plan for a **ceiling**: "six-figure business you can do that with ASO… seven figure it's a little bit harder" ([00:16:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=976s)). SEO has "a ceiling… capped by the area under the curve" ([YourMove 00:20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1229s), [00:21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1278s)). Kyle sat at ~$9–10k/mo for two years until he added TikTok ([00:15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=941s)). **[strong]**
14. **Rankings get lost and keywords get copied → start the second channel while you are still #1.** Kyle lost #1 in May 2024 and had to scramble: "this is bad. I need to find another revenue stream" ([00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=975s)). Searching "Bible focus" returned only PrayScreen; 2–3 months later there were ~70 listings ([Lots 00:12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=763s)). Erosion is **[medium]**; "start while #1" is this card's inference **[weak]**.
15. Don't target generic head terms in 2026. Old apps with many ratings hold them ([Josh 00:23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1433s), [Ryan 00:52:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=3145s), [Alex 00:38:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2316s)); see "What changed" below. **[strong]**
16. **Own your brand-name search**, because viral video traffic arrives as people typing your name. Viewers close TikTok and search the App Store for the app named in the video ([Joseph Choi 00:17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1038s)). A copycat with a near-identical name went viral but didn't rank, so its traffic went to GlamUp: "they just made us 100K in a week" ([00:05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=337s)). GlamUp's referral text "download Glam Up and use my code" gets pasted into comments, so the name ranks in TikTok search ([00:19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1166s)). Choose a distinctive, searchable name. **[medium]**
17. Web SEO only with a web funnel and a reason for domain authority. YourMove got press first (Fox, CNBC, Wired, WaPo), which gave "very good domain authority at the door" ([00:18:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1099s)); posts aimed at low-competition, high-volume keywords then drove "nearly half of our traffic" ([00:14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=874s)). Budget 3 months before seeing anything and 3–6 months to learn it failed ([00:24:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1487s)). **[weak]** (one measured case; SideShift and Clear 30 below are negative)
18. AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews): treat as an experiment, not a plan. Only evidence: Clover Labs' agency method: estimate prompt volume as ~30% of Google volume, check which URLs the chatbots cite, then get into those threads ([00:30:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1820s), [00:31:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1882s)). **Don't copy the execution.** Clover says it owns ~4,000 subreddits plus account networks to keep posts and comments live ([00:37:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2237s)), and describes editing a cited comment so it names only one product ([00:34:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2071s)). That is undisclosed, coordinated content manipulation, which **breaks Reddit's rules** on spam and vote/content manipulation. It risks bans and the loss of every seeded asset. Allowed version: write useful posts and comments from your own disclosed account. Comment marketers in the corpus say openly admitting it's your app converts best ([00:10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=648s)). **[weak]**

## When the default flips
- **Search first, even for content-capable teams:** when the problem is embarrassing or private, nobody will share content about it. Caleb's paruresis app: "very difficult for me to market", yet "the majority of users and revenue came through ASO" ([00:12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=743s), [00:12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=750s)).
- **Search first because you can't do creatives:** Steve: "if you don't understand any of this stuff, ASO and Apple Ads are probably the easiest low-hanging fruit… all you need are keywords" ([00:07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=421s)). The founders he knows at $100k+ MRR mostly win with "ASO, Apple Ads, and Meta" ([00:06:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=374s)).
- **Skip search:** the product creates a new behavior nobody searches for yet (most viral AI apps in the corpus). "#1 on the App Store" in the corpus usually means top charts from virality, not keyword rank: Lobby was #1 in five countries and Bro #1 in the US off TikTok ([Roger Chen 00:00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=8s), [00:00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=21s)).
- **Skip search:** the goal is seven-plus figures fast. All the biggest revenue cases in the corpus (Cal AI, Coconote, Pingo, StudyFetch) grew through creators, UGC or ads, not search (see [choose-channel](choose-channel.md)).
- **Skip web SEO:** app-only funnel, no press or backlinks, or a team that can't wait 3–6 months.

## Evidence against / where operators disagree
- **Steve himself:** "a lot of people, especially indies… overindex on ASO thinking that that's enough" ([00:07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=429s)).
- **Clear 30** (2025-08): "we tried influencers, Reddit, organic content, SEO… an uphill battle to convert"; only paid ads started the flywheel ([00:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=190s)).
- **Can a well-optimized newcomer beat incumbents?** Steve says yes on niche terms: he beats apps with more ratings, and a client outranked Canva because "Canva can't go after those niche keywords" ([00:25:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1539s), [00:26:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1601s)). Alex (secondhand, about Adam Lyttle's 100 apps) says no on generic terms: older apps with good reviews keep the ASO advantage, and a new app "placing some couple ASO keywords" won't outrank them ([00:38:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2316s)). Both fit: newcomers can win narrow terms, not head terms.
- **Algorithm factors are opinion.** Steve's order is title/subtitle, then ratings, then "maybe" revenue ([00:25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1558s)). Nobody in the corpus has tested it.
- **What changed from 2026 (newest evidence wins):**
  - *Crowding:* "80% more apps being submitted to the App Store this year… top keywords are kind of dominated by these legacy apps… 5, 6, 7, 8 years ago" ([Josh, Blue Throne, 2026-07, 00:23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1425s), [00:23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1433s)). Ryan expects "a whole lot more competition for keywords… so hard now to knock the top apps" ([2026-01, 00:52:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=3138s)). Kyle: "now there's a billion of them" ([00:14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=855s)).
  - *Blog SEO eaten by AI:* "a lot of blog posts like this have effectively become redundant with AI"; YourMove's SEO "declined pretty meaningfully" ([2026-01, 00:15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=909s)). ## Traps
- **Launching under a brand name with no keywords.** Cardstock was "just Cardstock… I don't even think I had a subtitle" and got little organic traffic ([00:17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1027s)).
- **Assuming a keyword is yours for good.** Kyle lost #1 after 2–3 years with no backup channel ([00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=975s)). PrayScreen's empty keyword had ~70 clones within months ([00:12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=763s)).
- **Ranking ≠ revenue.** Green Noise App is still #3 for its keyword and "doing nothing" ([00:11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=683s)). Monetization (rule 9, [paywall-pricing](paywall-pricing.md)) decides the money.
- **Finding the keyword and not building.** Steve knew a health & fitness keyword, was "too lazy" to build for it, and someone else did $500k on it. He then bought their app ([00:41:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2487s)).
- **Trusting tool difficulty scores** over reading the results page ([00:12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=762s)).
- **Over-researching before launch** ([Kyle 00:17:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1071s)).
- **Reddit self-promotion bans.** Kyle got banned from a card subreddit for app posts ([00:07:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=465s)). Industrial seeding (Clover) multiplies that risk.
- **Expecting PR to bring users.** It brought YourMove backlinks, not money ([00:18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1129s)).
- **A generic, unsearchable app name** hands the search traffic your videos create to a competitor with a similar name (rule 16).
