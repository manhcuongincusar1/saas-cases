# Sway AI — prove willingness to pay before code, pick the niche for monetization, then scale with ads; plus what Daniel applies at Cal AI

## Idea — how they found it
- Started from monetization, not product: an AI personal-finance advisor idea was dropped because finance apps are "hard to monetize"; dating has some of the highest-monetizing apps (Tinder, Hinge) — [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=152s).
- Demand found on the Hinge subreddit: people post their own profiles asking for feedback → "what if we could productize this?"; the private version reaches a bigger audience than public askers — [03:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=223s), [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=236s), [04:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=244s).
- Positioning vs RizzGPT (what to text) and UMAX (rate your face): rate the profile, because "if you have a great profile, texting is like the secondary part" — [04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=271s).
- **Distribution and payment before code**: a co-founder's Instagram page posting "how I cook on Hinge" prompt ideas popped within weeks; bio link to a pre-order; followers asked to DM for a manual profile review at ~$15 — "People were actually paying us before we built the app" — [04:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=298s), [05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=325s), [05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=351s), [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=357s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Onboarding as a sales funnel: clarify why they're here → label the problem → show the dream result (other members) → personalize with questions → why this app is for you → dream result again on the paywall — [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=971s), [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=992s). Explainer videos added before the questions after users emailed "what does this app even do?" — [23:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1435s). Questions must be trivially easy (multiple choice) — [24:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1485s).
- First paywall: carousel of ~8 slides of everything included — [16:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1016s).
- **Aha after the paywall**: uploading profile screenshots pre-paywall was tested; users leave the app to fetch screenshots and never come back; the thoughtless path "performed a lot better" — [21:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1301s).
- **Default to annual** to collect revenue up front and fund growth; weekly tested = "a lot slower growth" — [19:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1185s), [19:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1191s). Priced for monetization, "the opposite of Cal AI", whose prices are intentionally low — [18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1098s).
- **Consumables / whales**: users re-ran the analysis more than expected → credit upsell paywall ≈ 11% of revenue — [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1120s), [18:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1137s); "biggest upsell" = AI photo packs (one-pack and 10-pack; he says $14.99 and $49.99) — [19:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1141s), [19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1167s). Rationale: no spending cap; top 1% of spenders drive most revenue in dating/gaming apps (insight from a co-founder's game) — [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1232s), [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1270s). He calls Sway's LTV "very high" vs any app he has seen — [15:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=938s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **TikTok slideshows**: his first month ever on TikTok; copied the most viral slideshow text from a "men's improvement" account and re-skinned it — [06:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=397s), [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=420s). Figma background + spreadsheet auto-populating slides → a month of content in 1–2 hours — [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=442s), [07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=452s). Host reads branded posts at ~10–50k views each — [07:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=463s).
- **Warm-up**: start with unbranded aesthetic carousels; a fully branded new account "wouldn't have gone as viral initially"; add the orange branding and CTAs once views are reliable — [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=490s), [08:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=505s), [09:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=554s).
- Christmas Day post: a couple million views when he checked (host reads ~4M) → "5 or 6K in revenue just off that one day", biggest day at that point — [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=608s), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=621s).
- **Organic ceiling ~$30–40k/month**, then ads made it "a lot easier" — [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=645s). Ads came from one influencer (Defunct Sibling) whose videos ran as ads from his own page to his familiar audience — [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=667s), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=684s). Controversial ads with comment fights performed well; bottleneck = creative variety from a single influencer — [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=765s), [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=774s).
- **Niche warning**: dating coaches sell profile reviews, so promoting Sway "cannibalizes their own product"; calorie tracking does not conflict with fitness coaching — [13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=822s), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=853s). Also weigh how many influencers a niche has and their price (fitness many/cheap; beauty expensive due to makeup brand deals) — [43:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2597s).

### What he applies at Cal AI
- Cal AI was already at "a couple million a month" with above-standard onboarding/paywall conversion when he joined; his job = retention through the 3-day trial — [25:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1553s), [26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1563s).
- Redesigned core logging (camera, food database, food detail) — [27:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1627s); clone-resistant retention: groups/community feed (Strava insight), streaks (Duolingo), leaderboards, public topic groups (Ladder) — [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1663s), [28:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1685s), [28:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1707s); progress photos and milestone badges built to be posted to Instagram Stories (product-led growth) — [29:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1778s), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1798s).
- Design for the three screens every influencer video shows: camera → loading → food detail — [36:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2179s).
- Speed: a Cal AI co-founder's line that "human motivation only lasts about 2 weeks" for a feature — [31:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1865s).
- Paid ads were Cal AI's "big inflection point", as with Sway — [47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2863s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Take money for a manual version (DM + $15) from a content audience before building → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md), [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Pick niches by monetization; enter a big established market with one novel, video-legible mechanic (tests one variable) — [37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2252s), [39:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2371s); or plug an app into an already-viral content niche (Astra, horoscopes) — [41:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2486s) → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- Check that influencers' own business doesn't compete with the app before planning an influencer channel → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Warm new accounts unbranded; copy proven slideshow text from adjacent niches; batch production → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Organic tops out (~$30–40k/month here); scale with ads run from an influencer's own page; plan creative volume → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Default annual; add uncapped consumables for whales; keep the pre-paywall path effortless, aha after paywall → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Influencers test an idea faster (one variable: will they download/pay) than UGC (two: virality + payment) — [48:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2913s) → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Retention via streaks/groups/shareable milestones during a 3-day trial → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
