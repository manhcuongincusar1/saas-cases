# Pep AI — invert the category's channel: sign every niche creator before anyone runs ads

## Idea — how they found it
- Peptides booming on social; App Store had only old peptide calculators with "only a couple reviews" and no marketing; GLP-1 trackers existed and did well — [04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=252s). Built from late January, launched end of February — [04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=263s).
- Peptides over GLP-only because they serve many purposes (recovery, tanning, skin) = "more to market" — [05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=335s).
- **Channel inversion**: successful GLP apps "None of them are doing influencer marketing. They're only running ads" — so he signed "pretty much every peptide creator in the space", modeled on Cal AI/Quitter-style influencer programs — [05:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=345s), [05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=351s), [06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=362s).
- Founder fit: had watched peptide creators himself and would have downloaded an app they recommended "in a heartbeat" — [18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1080s); high-school esports team (30–50 players posting, "hundreds of thousands of followers") taught him roster marketing — [37:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2222s), [40:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2459s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Pure tracking tool, all data user-inputted, "no medical advice" — the legal boundary for the whole product — [23:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1432s), [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1446s). AI chat answers logistics ("can I travel with my GLP?") but not medical questions — [25:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1552s).
- Started as a simple dose log, grew from user feedback: cycles, vial run-out, nutrition/macros + meal scan, water, weight (Apple Health), workouts, AI insights — [31:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1897s), [24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1483s). Syringe/dose calculator delayed because it "seems like medical advice"; cleared with attorneys; "drove a ton of traffic" — [31:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1914s), [32:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1934s).
- Pruned unused features: AI body scan, bloodwork input; achievements simplified — [32:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1944s), [32:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1968s). Ships 2–3 updates a week with two developers (given equity) — [34:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2072s), [40:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2408s).
- Why users pay: safety + convenience (last dose, injection-site history); "$30 to $40 for the year" is "a no-brainer" next to drug spend — [27:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1646s), [27:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1653s).
- **Age-segmented onboarding**: ~60% of users are over 25; under-25s buy less and quit long onboarding → short flow; 25+ get a long flow that builds a nutrition plan (sells surrounding behaviors, not the tracker) — [28:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1725s), [28:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1738s), [29:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1751s).
- Paywall: yearly with 3-day free trial or monthly; $40/year, $10/month; wants to go cheaper — [23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1391s), [23:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1403s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Influencer marketing "by far" the #1 channel; ~100 creators posting — [08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=506s), [08:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=511s).
- **Formats**: slideshows beat talking-head ("most important things to do on [peptide]" → protein/water/electrolytes → train 3–5×/week → low-intensity cardio → "Pep AI makes it easier to track") — [09:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=592s); humor creator with app at the end (one 19-s video: 400k views) — [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=673s). His own videos and scripted copies of GLP-app ads failed — [06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=402s).
- **Link-in-bio as a channel**: peptide creators funnel all their informational content to bio links (Discord/School communities); a creator who posted nothing for 2 weeks still drove "a ton of downloads" from the bio link alone — [14:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=867s).
- **Attribution**: creator codes at signup + AppsFlyer OneLinks in bio (click → download → subscription per creator); renegotiate pay up/down from that data — [09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=540s), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=743s).
- **Deals**: 1-month trial = 4 posts + 4 stories + permanent link in bio, priced on views/engagement not followers (5k views/video ≈ $200/month) → 3-month deal if it works — [15:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=915s), [15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=931s), [15:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=948s). Bigger creators up to $4,000/month; one large fitness influencer signed for "over 20,000" — [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=953s), [16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=970s). Plus ~20% one-time affiliate commission for almost every creator → they post more than contracted — [21:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1316s), [22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1324s). No formal view bonuses; extra pay ad hoc.
- **Brief**: Google Doc of best videos + "you know your audience the best"; first video from each new creator approved in group chat — [18:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1126s), [36:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2166s). Lifetime app access before a deal; near-daily texting — [46:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2783s).
- **Outreach**: 4 dedicated outreach hires, 100+ DMs/day, 1,000+/week; first line "paid promo / affiliate deal?" → phone/WhatsApp → same-day call by one of 2 marketing managers (or him for big names) — [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1211s), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1216s), [42:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2527s), [43:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2602s). Fraud check: a signed creator had 120k views in an hour, no comments, no sign-ups → read comments — [44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2687s).
- **Paid**: only after organic winners; winners reposted as Meta ads — [06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=387s). Best creative: a cluttered square static (~8 info blocks) "cooked up on ChatGPT in like 2 minutes" by his ads guy, under $2/download, male and female versions — [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2833s), [47:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2855s). TikTok keeps rejecting Spark Ads (reads as medical advice); Meta is permissive — [50:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3038s).

## Lessons → decisions
- If incumbents in a hot niche only buy ads, lock up the niche's creators first; ads later with proven creatives → [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
- Trusted niche influencers + permanent tracked bio link + affiliate % > anonymous UGC for a trust-heavy health product → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Price creators on engagement; start with a cheap 1-month trial; audit comments for bought views → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Segment onboarding length by age; sell the surrounding plan, not the tracker → [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Cheap annual ($40) with 3-day trial for a price-insensitive niche spending heavily elsewhere → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md)
- Regulated-adjacent niche: keep a hard "no medical advice" line; expect TikTok ad rejections, Meta works → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md), [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md)
