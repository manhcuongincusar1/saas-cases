# Which acquisition channel should I start with, and in what order?

## Inputs to collect first
- **Can the value be shown on screen in under 15 s?** Examples: a scan that gives a number, a before/after, an AI that reacts to the user. Or is the value invisible (savings that arrive weeks later), or private/embarrassing so nobody will film it?
- **Does search demand exist?** Do people already type a name for the problem (a condition, "green noise", a card-scanner use case)? See [aso-search](aso-search.md).
- **Audience age and country.** Gen Z vs 30+. US/tier-1 vs global. Which countries can actually pay?
- **Purchase type.** Impulse (cheap, emotional) or considered (higher price, needs trust)? Social app with low ARPU, or hard-paywall subscription?
- **Founder:** willing to post on camera daily? Already skilled at content or at ads?
- **Money and stage.** Current MRR. Monthly budget. Has *any* organic video already converted?
- **Platform availability** (iOS/Android, which App Store countries). This decides which views are worth anything.

## Default answer
Start with **founder-made short-form, posted to both TikTok and Instagram Reels**. Find one format that drives downloads (not just views) before you pay anyone. Next, scale that format with paid creators ([ugc-creators](ugc-creators.md)). Once several formats are proven, put **paid spend behind the organic winners** ([paid-ads](paid-ads.md)). The case for going organic first is economic: one operator who sees many campaigns puts organic margin at ~50% vs 10–30% for paid ads. Organic also tops out at roughly $1–3M/yr, and that ceiling is why paid comes second, not never ([0mOLs315i04 00:01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=101s), [00:03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=192s)).
Start with **ASO + Apple Search Ads** instead in two cases: when the product cannot be filmed, or when demand is search-shaped. Treat AI search as an experiment. No founder in the corpus reports revenue from it.

## Decision rules
1. **If you have not personally posted for the app yet → post yourself before hiring creators or an agency.** Creators will not take performance (CPM) deals until you can show the app goes viral. Joseph Choi's line: at $0/mo you should not be hiring an agency. **[strong]** — [Nomad Table: first 50–100k downloads from own content](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=500s) · [Nomad Table: CPM needs proof](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2366s) · [GlamUp: founder first viral on all 4 apps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s) · [Pingo: founders made every video to $20k MRR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1141s) · [Dan Kwan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1014s) · [Driss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=3133s) · [Joseph Choi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=857s)
2. **If the value is visible on screen fast, or the product is "naturally viral" (Gen Z, impulse, funny, a score/result) → start with TikTok + Reels and show the app inside ~15 s.** **[strong]** — [Cal AI 00:22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1336s) · [Pingo "show the product" 00:16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1005s) · [Evo/Bible BFF 00:24:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1479s)
3. **If short-form still fits but the app screens are boring (travel deals, marketplace) → film a credible person giving the tip, and send viewers to a benefit-named URL instead of an app demo.** **[weak]**, Evo only — [00:14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=840s)
4. **If demand is search-shaped, the topic is private or embarrassing, or you are not a content person → start with ASO + Apple Search Ads.** ASO is "a pool": it only pulls in people who already search. Details in [aso-search](aso-search.md). **[medium]** — [Steve Young: easiest low-hanging fruit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=421s), ["ASO is a pool"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=439s) · [paruresis app: TikTok failed, ASO brought most users](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=750s)
5. **If the audience is 30+ or the purchase needs trust → put organic on Instagram + Facebook (cross-post) with creators of the buyer's age. If the audience is 18–25 → TikTok (and Snapchat).** On Meta ads the placement chooses the audience: Facebook feed skews older, Reels skews younger. **[medium]** — [Evo 00:23:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1388s) · [Marcus Burke 00:39:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2359s)
6. **TikTok vs Instagram: post to both. Judge each by downloads per country, not by views.** Mid-2026 data says Instagram now earns more raw views and TikTok suppresses "make money" niches. Earlier data says TikTok views are higher-intent and more US. Details in the Instagram ≠ TikTok table below. **[medium]** — [SideShift 2026-09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1183s) · [StudyFetch 2026-05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s)
7. **If the account is brand-new → warm it up before posting branded content.** Spend 3–5 days behaving like the target user, then start with unbranded or aesthetic posts and add branding once reach exists. **[medium]** — [SideShift 3–5 day warm-up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1102s), [act like the end user](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1032s) · [Sway unbranded carousels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=505s) · [GlamUp warm-up course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=986s)
8. **If views come from countries you cannot monetize, or outside your target → kill that content and track viewer country per video.** Where the creator lives does not decide where the views come from. **[strong]** — [Pingo: ~200k unmonetizable Russian users](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=114s), [UK creator, 90%+ Russian viewers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1722s) · [StudyFetch >80% US target](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s) · [Halo: US-based creators only](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=835s)
9. **If you want more reach and can monetize abroad → add countries deliberately.** Clover measured ~+20% views per country added. Spain, Germany and Australia were the easiest; Canada adds ~20% to a US campaign. **[weak]**, one agency — [Clover 00:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=863s), [00:15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s)
10. **If videos get views but no downloads → fix intent, not volume.** Change the text hook, the framing (solution vs novelty) and who is on camera. A view is not a download. **[strong]** — see Traps; [Coconote](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s) · [Yannis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2293s) · [Nomad Table](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1114s) · [Evo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1033s)
11. **Do not open paid until organic has winners.** Thresholds operators use: ~5 winning formats (Dan Kwan); ~$30–40k/mo from organic (Sway); "winners" (Cedric). After that, run the organic winners as ads. Scripts written from scratch to copy competitors' ads failed. **[strong]** — [Dan Kwan 00:32:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1931s) · [Sway 00:10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=645s) · [Pep AI 00:05:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=345s), [00:06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=402s) · [Bloom 00:14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=874s) · [Joseph Choi 00:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=221s)
12. **If organic has plateaued → add paid, and expect it to be the bigger lever.** Sway: paid took revenue to 4–5× its organic level. Daniel: paid was Cal AI's inflection point. Kobe: getting to $100M needs ads plus real followers. **[medium]** — [Sway 00:12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=742s), [00:47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2863s) · [Sunflower 00:30:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1838s)
13. **If it is a social app with low ARPU → use paid only to break the cold start.** Pair content tests with an invite loop. **[medium]** — [Nomad Table 00:36:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2201s) · [Hunter Isaacson 00:28:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1705s)
14. **If you cannot tell which channel works → run a dozen small parallel tests (influencers, ads, communities) and track cohorts per source before concentrating.** **[medium]** — [Evan: tested Reddit/X/influencers/paid, influencers won](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=378s) · [Hunter 00:28:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1682s)
15. **If the niche looks "too small" → that can be an advantage.** Narrow niches convert better and formats last longer, but they top out around $10k/mo. Plan the adjacent audience you will move into next. **[weak]** — [SideShift 00:22:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1346s), [00:23:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1421s)
16. **AI search (ChatGPT/Gemini/AI Overviews) → side bet only, when capacity allows.** The only claimed result is an agency's aggregate across its clients. **[weak]** — [Clover 00:29:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1782s) vs [Josh, Blue Throne 00:24:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1458s)

## When the default flips
- **ASO/Apple Ads first.** Trigger: nobody will film the product, or a nameable keyword exists. Steve Young says 1–2 channels can reach $5M MRR and the "big three" are ASO, Apple Ads and Meta. One of his apps did ~$4M in a year from App Store search alone ([00:06:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=367s), [00:09:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=553s)). The risk is that a ranking can vanish overnight: Cardstock ran ~$10k/mo passive from one #1 keyword, then lost it in May 2024 ([00:15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=913s), [00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=975s)). Search captures latent demand up to a ceiling; Meta/TikTok create induced demand ([YourMove 00:20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1229s), [00:20:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1243s)) → [aso-search](aso-search.md).
- **Paid first.** Trigger: the founder already knows Meta ads. Joseph Choi notes that founders at $1–5M ARR typically grew "mostly through Facebook ads" ([00:01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=71s)) → [paid-ads](paid-ads.md).
- **One big influencer instead of UGC volume.** Trigger: the niche has one dominant voice. Sunflower hired theirs as "VP of Sobriety": 50–100M views/mo by himself ([00:03:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=233s)) → [ugc-creators](ugc-creators.md).
- **Comment seeding / Reddit / AI search as the main channel.** No founder-reported revenue at scale in this corpus; treat as experiments.

## Evidence against / where operators disagree
- **TikTok or Instagram?** StudyFetch (2026-05): TikTok has higher intent and Reels converts poorly abroad. SideShift (2026-09): Instagram now out-views TikTok by 10–20%. *Prefer the newer data for reach and StudyFetch's for intent. Measure downloads per platform yourself.* SideShift sells creator-marketplace access.
- **Money-making apps on organic.** SideShift's Drew Levin (2026-06): apps where users "earn money or get something back" do best organically ([00:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=288s)). SideShift's Canyon (2026-09): that niche is now saturated and shadowbanned on TikTok ([00:21:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1268s)). Newest says: lean on Instagram.
- **Paid early or late?** Dan Kwan tested paid and disliked the margins ([00:10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=633s)). Jay never relies on it for a social app. Daniel: "if you can get paid ads to work, it's the best option, but it's also kind of hard" ([00:47:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2876s)).
- **Only proven winners go to paid?** SideShift and Yev (Sizzle) say organic flops can still win as ads ([00:49:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2999s), [00:05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=353s)). That conflicts with "≥5 winners first".
- **Multi-account farms vs real audiences.** Yannis opens 50–100 TikTok accounts/week ([00:00:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=5s)) and Clover runs accounts in 20 countries. Kobe calls this platform arbitrage that won't reach $100M ([00:29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1796s)).
- **AI search.** Clover (an agency selling it) calls it the most untapped channel. Josh (Blue Throne, 2026-07) talks to 4–5 founders a day and "no one says AI search".

## Traps
- **View-bait audiences.** Coconote's "PDF to brainrot" feature drew 100–200M views and their record 3,000 trial starts, which converted to ~$25k against ~$360k possible ([00:04:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=298s), [00:05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s)). Nomad Table had a 300k-view video with 174 likes and 8 comments ([00:18:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1114s)).
- **Relatable ≠ actionable.** Yannis ran the same creative with two overlays. "Post-breakup" got millions of views and no conversions. "Procrastinating for exams" jumped the app up the App Store in several countries ([00:39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2342s), [00:38:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2293s)).
- **Geo leakage** (Pingo, above): native speakers found language-learning content funnier than learners did.
- **Branding a cold account on day one** (Sway); **posting a winning format only "a few times"** while a competitor runs it on ~10 accounts (Stronger, [00:09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=564s)).
- **Buying big-follower influencers or hiring Gen Z "trend kids"** as the first move. Joseph Choi calls it the biggest tell a strategy will flop ([00:04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=290s), [00:05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=320s)). **Hard-selling** in organic video ([00:05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=337s)).
