# PrayScreen — clone a proven mechanic into an underserved niche, then sell fast

## Idea — how they found it
- Tried One Sec (breathe 3 s before Instagram opens), saw it "making 100k a month" (*secondhand*), and copied the mechanic for Christians because he already built in the faith space — [00:04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=289s), [00:04:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=295s), [00:05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=303s).
- Was first: "Bible focus" search showed only his app; 2–3 months later ~70 listings — [00:12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=763s). First-mover window in a clone niche is months, not years.
- Pattern across his apps: never invent, re-skin what works (Magic Music built within 4 days of Suno's launch, with no API) — [00:15:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=919s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Block apps; pray to unlock. Users prayed 4×/day; **60% D30 retention** of daily praying — [00:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=335s).
- Install→paid was unremarkable (he uses "2.5%" as an example) — [00:08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=508s), [00:08:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=514s). Because retention was high, they added a **free tier monetized by ads** (one ad per prayer, ~4/day): ~5 cents/user/day, payback on ad spend in ~14–15 days — [00:08:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=532s), [00:09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=541s), [00:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=548s), [00:10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=625s).
- Splash screen designed as a brand moment for videos: show splash → core action (prayer) → "congratulations" screen; no onboarding on camera — [00:03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=235s), [00:07:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=473s).
- Advice he gives founders stuck on conversion: "I show one paywall" → "just show another one" (*heuristic*) — [00:09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=569s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- Launched ~Dec 2024 (transcript garbled); 10–12 TikTok accounts with an agency — [00:02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=132s), [00:02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=143s), [00:02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=174s).
- Hook = shock/controversy ("the opposite of religion") to farm comments; comments turned from "why would you do this" to "I needed this" — [00:03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=189s). Pick the one emotion the viewer should feel — [00:03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=228s). Christian songs as audio to trigger on-topic comments — [00:07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=452s).
- Organic → paid: winning TikTok re-run as Meta ad (Instagram). One video took ~70% of spend out of 20–30 videos on the same angle; he can't say why — [00:06:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=409s), [00:06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=418s), [00:07:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=428s). Logic: organic proves the creative to a random audience; paid shows it to the targeted one → low CPI — [00:06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=390s).
- Earlier app Bible Buddy: started as a WhatsApp bot (~2 cents per conversation window cost); grown with X/Twitter ads targeting followers of the top 20–25 celebrity pastors — [00:13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=806s), [00:13:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=820s), [00:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=845s). He never cracked its monetization — [00:14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=861s).
- Toxic Traits format = ambiguous "red flag" screenshot so commenters argue (rage bait) — [00:15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=957s).

## Exit playbook (his heuristics)
- Tweeted the app at ~$120k; several buyers DM'd; buyer was someone who had tried to buy Bible Buddy ~2 years earlier — [00:02:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=143s). He says "within like one week" there and "within three days" later — [00:20:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1238s). A failed earlier deal = warm buyer.
- "90% plus" of deals fall through → take the **third-highest price** if that buyer can close in 4–5 days — [00:21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1297s).
- Multiples seen: **2×–6× ARR** — [00:21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1279s). ≥3 months of consistent revenue history is enough; longer is better — [00:22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1368s).
- Leave upside for the buyer: he deliberately never localizes, so "you haven't even tried this" is in the pitch — [00:22:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1322s). Offer 1–2 months of transition — [00:23:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1407s).
- Decide up front: cash-flow app or life legacy — [00:21:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1284s). Beginners: get a first $5–10k MRR app and sell it rather than chase a Cal AI outcome — [00:18:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1122s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Copy a proven mechanic (One Sec/Opal) into an identity niche you belong to; expect clones within ~3 months → [choose-idea](../decisions/choose-idea.md)
- High retention + weak conversion → add an ad-monetized free tier instead of forcing the paywall → [paywall-pricing](../decisions/paywall-pricing.md), [onboarding-retention](../decisions/onboarding-retention.md)
- Prove creatives organically, then put spend behind the outlier; expect one creative to carry most spend → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Sell small apps fast: public tweet, warm past buyers, third-highest price with fast close, leave growth levers untouched → [buy-sell-exit](../decisions/buy-sell-exit.md)
