# Noise — "shots on goal": one format posted by thousands of accounts, soft-CTA viral UGC + hard-CTA slideshows as retargeting (vendor's own account)

## Idea — how they found it
- App founders with a performance-marketing background; concluded "TikTok was the best distribution system at cost" vs Meta — [08:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=488s).
- Started July 2024 as an internal growth hack for their previous app **Playbite**: paid ~30 of their most engaged users to post Canva slideshows — [21:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1281s); e.g. Brawl Stars content with a hard CTA ("Playbite's the fastest way to get gems") posted by 35 people — [21:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1313s). Aha: one slideshow did 20k views in a week — [08:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=534s). "We initially built it as an internal growth hack tool" — [18:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1107s).

## Product — what they built and the key product choices
- Brand uploads one format with all assets, captions, descriptions; posters (no format invention: "creators on noise are not meant to really invent their own formats") curate their account and post — [10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=632s), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=614s). Platform randomizes hook images, text, order, descriptions, hashtags so every post is unique — [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1340s), [24:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1478s).
- Pay per view only; the brand sets the CPM from its target cost per install — [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=649s). Two tiers: mass "shotgun" slideshow posters and an upskilled tier doing traditional UGC — [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=603s).
- Structure mirrors Meta ads: campaigns → playbooks → posts — [30:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1805s). Give a playbook 1–3 weeks; a playbook with 446 posts in ~2 weeks and ~200k total views was paused — [30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1827s), [30:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1845s); its replacement ("how are we making money by Christmas") hit 9M views — [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1866s).

## Marketing — channel, formats, creator/ads machine
- **Scale**: "50,000 people post this content" vs 5–10 creators twice a day; ≥100k pieces of content a day — [04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=264s), [04:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=275s). Views come from big hits (one at 22M in 4 days) plus a "ground game" of posts at 500–2,000 views — [03:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=231s), [03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=235s).
- **Eggs theory**: on a client's healthy-eating slideshows, higher-view posts always had eggs in the hook image — instantly legible, healthy, cheap = visual relevance to the viewer's identity — [05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=318s), [05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=350s). Small changes = "the difference between 1,000 views and 10 million" — [04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=297s).
- **Word-level sentiment**: "everybody needs a side hustle" flopped; "don't get a second job" went viral — "no one really likes being told what to do" — [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=111s), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=942s). That video (borrowed from a woman cleaning Airbnbs for extra cash) ran at 8¢ installs for ~3 days and took Noise to #7 in category — [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=886s). Washing dishes worked; vacuuming and pouring coffee did not — [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=905s). Anchor on believable money ($20/night → "$500 a month" ≈ a car payment); "$7,000 in a week" (real, per Nick) reads as a scam — [16:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1003s), [17:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1022s).
- **Two prongs at once** — [27:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1658s): high-effort UGC with a soft CTA aiming for millions (drives "what is the noise app?" comments — [14:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=894s)) + thousands of hard-CTA slideshows that "function very similar to retargeting campaigns" — [25:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1536s). Slideshows were only ~2M of the 54M views in 30 days — [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1512s).
- **Comment seeding**: "super implicit" video (Secret Santa $60, app only in the description) + platform users seeding comments; one seeded comment had 1,686 likes, which Nick reads as "at least a thousand people" downloading or searching — [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1131s), [19:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1176s). That format took Noise to #5 in category — [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1085s).
- **Pingo roast format** (client): handed posters the example (originated by a woman crying over a Google pronunciation), exact prompting instructions and why it's funny; ~20M views overnight; ran Oct–Nov in ~15 variations — [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=698s), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=728s), [12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=734s). Reddit (r/RoastMe) offered as an analogy for pre-validated demand and to learn how a community talks, not as where the format came from — [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=773s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=803s).
- "We don't have to be super creative. We just have to have a good eye for what's already gone viral" — [13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=834s).
- Craft: have posters paste the caption text inside TikTok rather than baking it into the image (signals a real user) — [24:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1462s). Simple captions beat AI-generated ones — [28:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1704s).
- **Theories (heuristic)**: videos under 500 views still convert because TikTok routes each to "the right 500 people"; "what app is that?" on 200-view videos — [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1906s), [35:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=2129s). "TikTok has a daily views budget" per content space: flooding a niche spreads the same views thinner, which is why copied formats stop popping — [32:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1943s), [33:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1986s). Anyone can learn virality: Nick never posted before July 2024; his first experiment post did 887k, one hook image 5M+ — [22:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1364s), [23:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1403s), [23:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1414s).

## Lessons → decisions
- Volume of near-identical posts across many accounts finds winners; tiny hook details (image, sentiment of first words) dominate → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md)
- Pair soft-CTA viral content with hard-CTA low-view content that acts as organic retargeting → [ugc-creators](../decisions/ugc-creators.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)
- Kill a playbook after 1–3 weeks without a pop; formats saturate as a niche's daily attention gets split → [build-or-kill](../decisions/build-or-kill.md)
- Pay per view set from target CPI; compare to Meta CPI before choosing → [paid-ads](../decisions/paid-ads.md), [choose-channel](../decisions/choose-channel.md)

## Caveats
- **Conflict of interest**: Noise sells this distribution model and positions itself against Meta ads and agencies ("not here to charge $15–20k ahead of time") — [07:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=438s). Host invited Nick after another guest (Kyle) promoted Noise — [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=411s). Pingo, a featured case, buys views through Noise (see [pingo](pingo.md)).
- Noise's own app pays users to post, so its install costs and "make money" hooks are not comparable to a typical subscription app; no revenue, retention or LTV disclosed.
- Views-budget and <500-view conversion are theories; "plenty of math" is asserted, not shown. Mass multi-account posting may face platform enforcement (see [sunflower](sunflower.md) for the opposing view). Published 2026-01.
